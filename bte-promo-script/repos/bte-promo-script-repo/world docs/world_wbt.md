# LAYER 0 — ESSENCE  ·  *the only default load*

## Three Pillars (locked)
1. **Hidden Potential / Reversal Promise**  *(Thruline)*
2. **Rank = Social Class**
3. **Academy Setting**

## Protagonist — Ren Patinder
**Identity.** A ten-year-old outskirts boy whose elderly parents sold their house for one Academy placement and the cheapest gray egg. At his ceremony the egg yields a *spore* — the 0.01% outcome, "the weakest beast ever recorded." Status zero; after a near-death forest accident he gains encyclopedic beast knowledge no one else has. The gap between what he knows and what he can prove is the engine.
**Combat.** He fights with his own small body, never a beast proxy. He wins by reading mana-pattern flaws — a rival's cultivation defect, the exact angle a scale joint can't cover — and striking the precise weak point. Knowledge, not force.
**Power Source.** The spore grants only **+10% physical strength — that is all it gives.** The *knowledge* is a separate effect of an ancient three-ingredient medicine completed in his blood. It flows through luminescent hair-mushrooms (cosmetic). Hard ceiling: clear to Silver 3; Gold and abyssals are a total void.
**Voice.** Soft-spoken, intellectual, internally narrated; apologetic under humiliation but privately certain. Talks to his spore. Uses his father's cooking metaphors. No bravado. Trembles from excitement/frustration, not fear.

**Voice Samples (verbatim):**
1. "Why you? With such low chances, why did it have to be you?" (Ep 4) — bitter, to the spore
2. "If you're going to follow me on this journey, you'd better at least not get in my way." (Ep 5) — practical
3. "At least you're good for something. Even if just as a lamp." (Ep 8) — survival wit
4. "It works. It really works." (Ep 11) — young wonder
5. "I won't. My spore isn't weak. And I'm going to prove it." (Ep 17) — firm, no doubt

**Drift tripwires.** Hides spore to avoid mockery | wins through strength/beast-proxy | spore secretly powerful all along | spore personified as a named companion pet ("Cloud") that nuzzles/shields/leads | hero-confident voice | omniscient tactical genius (loses the Gold/abyssal void) | loses internal monologue.

## Setting — Entry-Window World (Outskirts → Forest → Academy)
**Feel.** A rigid cultivation society where egg color sorts your life at age ten: gray = failure, black = royalty. Ren's home is a tilted rented cabin on the mana-poor outskirts, kitchen-scented, three candles. Past it: a dark forest of starving expelled beasts, then an ancient spore-tunnel and a crystal cave. Then the marble Imperial Academy, built over ruins, where rank is publicly visible and no adult protects the low-ranked.

**Sensory Samples (verbatim):**
1. "The aroma escaping through the door's cracks made Ren's stomach growl traitorously. His parents were talented cooks." (Ep 3)
2. "Three bioluminescent eyes that glowed with a hypnotic radiance, capable of paralyzing their prey with just a direct look." (Ep 6)
3. "The air here felt older… quieter… as though it remembered things long forgotten." (Ep 8)

**Drift tripwires.** Forest as safe/navigable | parents young & healthy without frailty | Academy as welcoming to the low-ranked | egg color as destiny-myth rather than purchased economic sorting.

## Tone Constraint
Underdog *information-asymmetry* drama, not a power fantasy. Ren's edge is invisible knowledge that gets him mocked, not strength. If a beat lets him simply overpower an opponent, it has broken the show.

# LAYER 1 — ACTIVATION CANON  ·  *load at W1 + canon gate*

## First 5 minutes  *(the 50%-drop gate)*
The Ceremony of Ten Years. Children contract beasts that cure their mana poisoning; the beast's rank fixes their caste ("Iron for the slaves… Silver for the privileged"). Classmates draw fire salamanders and a Gold-potential shadow wolf. Ren's gray egg "simply cracked. A dry, pathetic sound" — a gray spore, the 0.01%. The hall laughs; mushrooms sprout from his scalp: "Look! He's rotting!" A child's whole future dying on schedule, by purchase, in public.

## First 5 episodes  *(the first sitting)*
The funeral-feast nobody eats; Ren runs to the forest seeking a "miracle medicine" to replace the spore. Moon Toads, a Night Excavator, a starving Bronze Mirror Mantis; he falls into an ancient spore-tunnel, the dormant spores consume the Mantis, and a crystal-cave plant — the "female" half of the medicine his mother once took — completes in his blood. **Knowledge floods in.** Parents find him; unconditional-love reunion. He takes the Mantis core, sees his parents' plants can be fixed by a 1,000-day method, and binds them to that promise.

## First 20 episodes  *(texture band)*
Carriage to the Academy: Jin's trio attacks, Luna Starweaver (best beast in class) intervenes; Ren refuses to hide his mushrooms. Gray Wing room 15 (roommates Min/Taro/Liu), the merit/support economy, the gathering-duplication wealth engine, ruins humming beneath. Professor Wei publicly mocks Ren's "heretical" cultivation correction; the first proof-fights begin (Kai's scale-flaw defeat, ~ep 24).

## HARD BLOCKS  *(opening-binding)*
1. **The spore grants only +10% physical strength — nothing else.** It is genuinely the worst beast recorded, not a secret legendary in disguise. Any promo showing the spore as directly powerful in combat breaks the thesis.
2. **The hair-mushrooms never evolve into a combat power or a creature.** They glow (light/camouflage) and pulse to carry knowledge; through ch 100 they remain cosmetic and "dormant." They never become a weapon, a beast, or a dragon.
3. **Beasts do not fight each other Pokémon-style.** Power comes from the *human's* contracted beast(s) fused into the tamer's body. (Yino's edge is humans bearing *two* beasts via abyssal fusion — see below.)
4. **The knowledge is unique to Ren and uncopyable.** It needs the male plant + female plant + a host born with the first half (his mother ate the male; Ren is the missing key). No one else can gain it.
5. **The knowledge has a hard ceiling: Silver 3.** Gold-and-above and all abyssals are a total void — genuine danger remains.
6. **Egg color is purchased economic sorting, not destiny mythology.** Eggs come from underground mana veins near "mother" dragons; the kingdom sells them. Gray is cheapest.
7. **Ren's parents are 60 (mature-Iron plants make them look ~40).** Their frailty is load-bearing — it drives his urgency.
8. **The spore is not a named companion character — there is no "Cloud."** Ren's contracted beast is *the spore*: a fused, near-inert fungus that grows cosmetic hair-mushrooms. It is NOT a sentient pet with a name. It does not nuzzle, shield, bleed, weep, fist-bump, "lead the way," or function as a bond-object / stakes-character. The empathy bond that carries the show is **Ren ↔ his parents** (and the 1,000-day promise), not Ren ↔ a cute creature. Personifying the spore into a companion named "Cloud" is the same category error as putting two beast tamers in FLBM — it sells a show that does not exist. *(Note: "Spore Cloud," "Rotting Boy," and "Pearl Crystal" are legacy shipped-promo **titles** in the benchmark/reference data — the titles are fine; the **in-story personified beast named "Cloud"** inside those promos is the violation and must not be reused.)*

# LAYER 2 — RAILS (eps ~20–100)

## 2a · Conversion Threads
1. **The Wei bet** — Ren stakes all his Academy support on Taro's "impossible" beetle evolving exactly as he predicts. Pays off later (beetle's Living-Tunnel evolution). Initiate the stakes; never deliver the vindication.
2. **The 1,000-day parent promise** — will their plants actually reach Bronze-2 power and extend their lives? Pays off only past ch 100 (ritual still running). Pure faith-against-evidence.
3. **Luna's secret** — the cold girl who defends him is the King's niece, undercover with a cousin spy network. Reveal lives deep (undisclosed in-world by ch 100). Tease the mutual-surveillance romance, never out her.
4. **"Ten million crystals"** — Ren's vow to buy his parents the city life they deserve; the explicit, calculable money-gap. Pays off far out; sell the climb, not the arrival.
5. **The stolen hydra egg → Yino threat** — Ren robs a gold-rank hydra's egg; a surviving Yino agent now hunts the unknown thief (him). Pays off post-ch 100. Tease the closing net.

## 2b · Canon Limits / Never-Happens
- **The spore is never a named companion character ("Cloud")** — it is the fused fungus, not a sentient pet; it never nuzzles, shields, bleeds, weeps, fist-bumps, or leads the way. The bond is Ren ↔ parents, not Ren ↔ a creature.
- **The mushrooms never evolve into a combat power or creature** — cosmetic and "dormant" through ch 100; no dragon, no weapon, no beast.
- **The spore never becomes directly powerful** — its only stat is +10% strength; every Ren victory is knowledge + precision, never overpowering.
- **Beasts never battle Pokémon-style** — humans fight using their fused beast(s); the "fusion" horror is human tamers merging with beasts, not beast-vs-beast duels.
- **Ren never reveals his knowledge source** — he must engineer indirect "proof"; he can't openly explain or he sounds insane.
- **The show never lets Ren read Gold/abyssal beasts** — the ceiling is real and keeps producing danger.
