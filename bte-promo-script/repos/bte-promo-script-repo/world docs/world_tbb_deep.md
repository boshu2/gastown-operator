## World mechanics — full

**The Mystic Jade Pendant.**
- *Origin:* one of two artifacts (with the Dragon Mastery scroll) sealed in the Dragon Cave of Mystics; retrieved by an 8-year-old Brian as the prophesied "chosen one."
- *Threat-sense:* warns Brian of any nearby **ill intent** via a private pulse only he perceives; the closer/more hostile the intent, the sharper the signal. Saved him repeatedly in warzones (snipers, blades, poison). The source of the codename **"The Hawk."**
- *Threat-memory:* remembers anyone who has tried to harm Brian before and warns harder when they reappear.
- *Opportunity-hints:* a distinct class of signal points toward hidden value/relics. Brian has decoded **three** distinct alerts so far; occasional unfamiliar signals he can't yet interpret.
- *Exclusivity:* the effects are Brian's alone — no one nearby notices anything.
- *Mystic Space:* the pendant's interior dimension, brimming with dense energy; houses Eli Grayson's stored mind. Entered by sleeping (Ep 51). Its energy density is what finally allows cultivation breakthroughs the ordinary world can't.

**Dragon Mastery Arts** *(canon variant: also "Dragon Mastery Techniques"; once called "Dragon Slaying Technique," Ep 53 — treat as a source slip, not a separate art).*
- A leveled martial-cultivation system from the scroll in the Dragon Cave; "a martial art so powerful, only legends could wield it."
- Each level reinforces body, reflexes, and energy channels — Brian at level 1 is already "an elite" vs. ordinary fighters.
- *The stall:* breakthroughs require high ambient energy. Brian was stuck at the **late first level** for years because the outside world is too energy-poor; the Mystic Space (Ep 53) gives the surge that pushes him through.
- Training substitutes for sleep — a night of meditation/training restores him like ten hours' rest.

**The Dragon Cave of Mystics.**
- On **Starlight Peak**; reveals itself **only on a full-moon night, only to "the chosen one"** (prophecy carved in fire-lit letters).
- Set up by Eli Grayson's grandmaster. Interior: gold/silver veins, an altar holding a wooden box (scroll + pendant).
- **Escape-before-sunrise rule:** leave before dawn or be trapped inside for **five years** (the "five-year milestone" Brian references). Brian, age 8, escaped at the last second after Samuel Lin knocked him off a cliff to trigger the discovery.

**Brian's skill set (non-magical).**
- Elite hand-to-hand and weapons; trained by Samuel Lin and, at 6–8, by Samuel's unnamed master (Brian grasped ~30% of that master's techniques — already enough to dominate missions).
- **Assassination craft:** stealth, ambush, presence-suppression, sensing other operatives ("people in this line of work can sense each other").
- **Traditional medicine:** brews healing ointments and remedies rivaling military-grade product; reads pulses to diagnose. Inherited from Lin.
- **Con/grift instincts:** out-cons two professional scammers on the train (Ep 2).

**The world.**
- **Chinatown** (Brian's home — patched walls, flickering bulb, cold); **Aspen Hills** ("where all the rich people live") containing **Maple Grove Academy** and **Hart Villa**; **Hart Towers** (Hart Group HQ); **Starlight Peak** (the cave); **North Sahara** (Ep 1 warzone); **Metro City** (Cody's police jurisdiction).
- **Maple Grove Academy:** elite prep school, crisp uniforms, heirs, statewide college-entrance-exam pressure; cherry-blossom courtyard; visible designer-status culture.
- **Hart Group:** construction-rooted empire built by Austin Hart; board of shareholders descended from Austin's old partners — some now scheming against Robert.

**The mission (withheld spine).** Robert hires Brian publicly as a study buddy, secretly as a bodyguard, and tells him the *real* priority is to "build a good relationship with Stella." The danger is corporate: rival shareholders/an inside Hart Group executive engineered Stella's bank kidnapping to leverage Robert into a deal (Ep 39). Brian suspects Stella may be **bait** for a larger hidden threat; Robert withholds specifics "for security reasons."

- **Brian ↔ Harper:** sparring banter; she's the matchmaker pushing Stella toward him.

- **Brian ↔ Robert:** asset/employer with growing mutual respect; Robert impressed by Brian's sharpness (Ep 39).
- **Brian ↔ Cody ("Hound"):** war brothers; Cody idolizes Brian; now cop-vs-mysterious-civilian on the surface, allies underneath. Shared grief over Pangolin.
- **Brian ↔ the Girl in Black:** strangers bound by his mentor's old organization; a debt-of-lineage rescue.
- **Stella ↔ Harper:** best friends and study partners; Harper reads Stella's feelings before Stella admits them.
- **Robert ↔ Austin Hart:** deferential son / influential patriarch; Austin's old partners' heirs are the antagonist bloc.

## Cross-show contamination guard
TBB shares a **Secret/Hidden Identity** pillar (MVS, PE, TSS, SS, SW) and an **elite-school + class-gap** setting (WBT, FLBM, TS, SW). Keep distinct:
- TBB = **Brian Woods**, Chinatown orphan and battle-hardened operative, **undercover as a billionaire's daughter's "study buddy" while secretly her bodyguard**; powers are a **jade pendant** (threat-sense, houses **Eli Grayson**) + **Dragon Mastery** cultivation, both earned as a child in the Dragon Cave. The school is a **cover/arena for a hidden corporate threat**, not where his power originates, and he enters **already trained but stalled at level 1**.
- Do NOT frame TBB as a student *awakening* a power at school (that's SW/MVS/FLBM). Do NOT make Brian secretly rich or status-flexing. Do NOT conflate **"The Hawk"** (Brian's codename, from the pendant) with **FLBM's Hawk** (a literal bonded beast) — unrelated.

## Source notes / flags logged
1. **Technique name inconsistency:** "Dragon Mastery Arts/Techniques" (dominant, matches the locked pillar) vs. "Dragon Slaying Technique" (Ep 53). Standardize on **Dragon Mastery**.
2. **Mission ambiguity is canonical, not a gap:** the source deliberately withholds the full mission ("build a relationship with Stella"; she may be bait). Don't invent a concrete villain plan beyond the Hart Group shareholder/kidnapping scheme (Ep 39) when building promos — the withheld stakes are a feature.
3. **Codename ledger:** Brian = "The Hawk" (pendant) and "Arn" (field); Cody = "Hound"; Pangolin = fallen comrade. Animal-codename unit lore — keep consistent if referenced.
4. **Source coverage:** L0/L1 verbatim-traced through Ep 5 plus the pendant/cultivation reveals (Ep 35, 51–53) and the assassin/Cody threads (Ep 39–41, 25–27); eps 6–93 sampled. Extend verification before anchoring any post-Ep-20 beat.
