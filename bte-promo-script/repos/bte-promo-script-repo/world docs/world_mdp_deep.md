## Cultivation & Dragon Mechanics

**The Dragon Core (Internal Core).**
- A dragon is born with one; it "innately contained the instinct of absorbing the Heavenly and Earthly Spiritual Energy… a supreme treasure of the world" (Ep 20) — the source of a dragon's strength.
- Sophie's was **immature and small**; it fell into Jacob's mouth by accident (Ep 1, explained Ep 8). Because it was weak, he didn't immediately explode.
- **Hard rule (Ep 20):** "If he didn't follow through the Dragon Clan's cultivation technique to mediate and suppress the energy of the Internal Core, his body would explode sooner or later due to the growing power of the Dragon Core."
- **Sophie's clock (Ep 8):** a clan member who loses their core before maturity "can't live for more than five years"; herbs might extend it but are too risky for her.
- **The restoration deal (Ep 13, Drakon):** within ~3 years Jacob must "open up all the openings of the dragon core and transfer half of the Connate Dragon Essence back to her," or she "won't be able to live for more than two years." Achieved via **surrogate core cultivation** — which requires marriage and, eventually, "Consummation" (Elder Frank, Ep 8; Elder Liam confirms it's a real but near-extinct method).
- **The protective lie (Ep 13):** Drakon privately states returning the core would in fact harm no one; he had Liam tell the Elders otherwise so they wouldn't kill Jacob. The engagement is built on this lie.

**The Spirit Concentration Scroll** (the Dragon Tribe's fundamental technique; three levels, Ep 20 verbatim):
1. "Communicate with heaven and earth, feel the energy in nature, and purify your body."
2. "Guide the energy into your body… slow, but it really helps strengthen your body."
3. "Release the energy around your body." → **"You can only cultivate other more formal cultivation techniques after passing the third level."**
- Liam transmitted only the **first chapter** (Ep 9; confirmed limiting his progress, ~ep 52). Drakon hand-coaches the rest (Ep 20). Jacob's element is **water** (Susan, Ep 11). He breaks **Level 1** by ep 20; **Level 2** ~ep 53 (with a visible sky-vortex side effect); still Level 2 by ep 100.

**Cultivation ladder.** Eight levels; the highest is the **Heavenly Level** (Sophie, Ep 19). Above the Human Realm is the **Heavenly Dragon**, reached by surviving the **Heavenly Tribulation** (Kenith's pending ascension). Rough power order: ordinary mortal ≪ Jacob ≪ Sophie's baseline ≪ Liam (a North Atlantic master) ≪ Susan (Inspector, "even two Elder Liams couldn't beat her," Ep 17) ≪ Kenith ("No. 1 in the Human Realm").

**Inspectors (Ep 11, Ep 17).** Authorized clan supervisors who police internal events (bribery, murder), may act before reporting, and are mostly above Heavenly Level. They sense each other and read auras on sight; ordinary cultivators can't detect or hide from them. Susan is one; she "won't hurt you as long as she recognizes your legal identity," and is even bound to protect a legitimate clan member — but will kill to protect the secret.

**Aura.** Everyone emits one; the strong read the weak. Jacob can't yet sense his own. A mortal giving off "the aura of a dragon" is what exposes him to Susan (Ep 11).

## Geography & factions

- **Sturgeon Bay (Wisconsin)** — Jacob's hometown; his grandma's two-story beach cottage, ~200m from the shore; the reef field offshore later proves to sit near the hidden Dragon Palace.
- **Sturgeon Bay University** — Jacob's school; dorm Building No. 7 area, Room 302; Mechatronic Engineering. Drakon's MTX Group is the biggest investor (built the stadium, library, media building); Elder Liam is Managing VP.
- **Cherry Grove High School** — Sophie's elite private school for "affluent and wealthy families." (The earlier "DragonCore" intake mislabels this "Middle School" — non-canonical.)
- **MTX Group HQ** — a ~70-story downtown tower, NASDAQ-listed; Drakon's 75th-floor President's Office and adjoining Elders' conference room (the Ep 7–9 set).
- **The Dragon King's house** — a deceptively plain two-story home, luxurious inside; Helen-designed; the attic studio becomes Jacob's cultivation room (Ep 20).
- **The Dragon Palace** — the clan's true seat, hidden **under the ocean** off the Sturgeon Bay reefs; first named ~ep 48, entered ~ep 84 via a deadly reef-passage of array formations (needs the Spirit Concentration Scroll + a Water Repellent Bead to pass). Houses sub-palaces (Hundred-Flower / Mystic Creature / Ice) and Kenith's Tribulation altar.
- **Wildcat Mountain (State Park)** — site of the dragon-clan **mountain temples** (Celestial Cloud / Oneness Temple, the Founder's Palace, ancestor inscriptions); the family-trip cultivation arc ~ep 24–31. "Not accessible to mortals."
- **Regional Dragon Clans** — **North Atlantic** (Drakon's, "Wisconsin Clan," land-business dominant) vs. **South Atlantic** (cultivation-dominant, a reclusive century-old great master). A long grudge that ignites the ~ep 99–100 hook. Others referenced: South/North Pacific, East/South Arctic, plus lesser River/Lake Dragon Kings.

- **Cultivation coaching → Wildcat Mountain (~ep 20–31):** Drakon then Susan teach the Scroll; Levels 1–2 broken; hand-holding and the first romantic warmth on the mountain.

- **Grandma's illness & the parents reveal (~ep 55–83):** Grandma hospitalized and moved in with Sophie's family; the famous Drs. Brown revealed as Jacob's parents; Grandma's outburst forcing them to account; the two-family lunch giving a soft, conditional marriage blessing.

- **Rock-climbing / Kyle finale (~ep 90–98):** sabotaged climb (Sam's rope tampered), two-climb victory over Michael, the club's predatory fees exposed, Kyle revealed as Kathy's brother and recruiting Jacob & Sam to basketball.
- **Clan-war setup / Sophie's birthday (~ep 99–100):** Susan warns the North–South Atlantic party could mean "serious danger" for Jacob; the quiet domestic ep-100 close (Sophie applies a band-aid to his wound). The hook into the next 100.
