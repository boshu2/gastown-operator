# LAYER 0 — ESSENCE  ·  *the only default load · ≤ ~650 words*

## Three Pillars (locked)
1. **Secret Identity**  *(Thruline)*
2. **Progression Elements / The System**
3. **Hostile Academy / Institution**

## Protagonist — Quinn Talen
**Identity.** A broke, bullied 16-year-old orphan whose only inheritance is a book his dead parents left him — one nobody can open until his own blood touches it. A drop of blood activates a game-like System and turns him into a Halfling vampire. He is not chosen or prophesied; the System is enormous but his public status stays **Level 1**, and hiding what he is becomes the entire game. Engine fit: **secret-power-underdog** — the power is huge, the wristwatch number never moves.

**Combat.** Fights on his own body's HP, in brackets, with broken fists and bent legs. Wins by **Inspect + feint + ambush**, not force — chooses **Agility over Strength** ("fast and agile like an assassin," Ep 18), masks up and strikes at night. Weakness: stuck at Level 1; daylight halves all stats.

**Power Source.** A book left by his parents, delivered by a government agent when he was ten — *"the only thing that his parents possessed - One that they passed down when they died"* (Ep 1). 112 failed attempts to open it (acid, fire, cutting). A drop of his blood activates it; the book **dissolves into dust** as it enters him and no longer exists. Leveling triggers **Halfling evolution** → fangs, night vision, sun-weakness, and blood hunger that ticks −1 HP/hour until he feeds. Blood swipe costs −1 HP per use. NOT an amulet, sword, ring, or social bloodline.

**Voice.** A self-talking runt who argues with his own System notifications. Defiant-under-humiliation, not cocky-hero. Names his own weakness, white-lies to his friends, and carries real moral friction about feeding — he negotiates around the bite, he doesn't relish it.

**Voice Samples (verbatim — never paraphrase):**
1. "I bet you don't even know how many fingers I'm holding up!" (Ep 1) — defiance-as-armor
2. "Why is the world so unfair?" (Ep 1) — bitter lament
3. "Would I like to level up? Why even bother asking." / "Who cares? YES!" (Ep 17) — sarcastic with the System
4. "Have I become a vampire?" he mumbled to himself. (Ep 18) — horror recognition
5. "In the movies it looked so easy. Just the idea of sticking my teeth into someone… I just can't do it! Wait, maybe I don't have to! I could just make a small cut on his arm and lick it" (Ep 18) — moral workaround

**Drift tripwires.** Public power display | wins on raw strength | non-blood activation of the book | relishes feeding | hero-confident voice | fights adults as equals.

## Setting — Entry-Window World
**Feel.** A two-year mandatory **military academy** inside a hidden, over-advanced city. Wristwatches broadcast your power level, hold your credits, gate your movement. Students self-sort by number — low levels pushed to the back of the canteen, extorted in plain sight while guards do nothing. Sunlight is hostile; night is when Quinn can move. The cruelty is institutional and casual — gas the bus, bag the kids, ship them off at sixteen.

**Sensory Samples (verbatim):**
1. "the boy spotted his attacker's glowing wristwatch, which lit up brightly in the dark, displayed the number one." (Ep 17)
2. "Even when there were no street lights, it was as if the moon had lightened everything up. A type of night vision of sorts." (Ep 16)

**Drift tripwires.** Space / Dalki battlefield at entry | adults intervene to protect the weak | Quinn's watch removable or upgradeable | open-world freedom frame | daylight narratively neutral.

## Tone Constraint
Hold the paranoid-underdog register: every humiliation is publicly verifiable on the wrist, and the dread is *being found out*, not being hunted. If it tips into power-fantasy triumph or open vampire spectacle, the secret — the show — is gone.

# LAYER 1 — ACTIVATION CANON  ·  *load at W1 + canon gate · ≤ ~880 words*

## First 5 minutes  *(the 50%-drop gate)*
Quinn, a bullied Level-1 runt with taped glasses, gets blasted into a wall on the last day of school and flips off the bully who did it (Ep 1). At home — a single government-provided room, no living relatives — he runs Test 112 on the half-kilo blood-red book his dead parents left, the one he cannot open. Frustrated, he gashes his thumb; a drop of blood hits the book. It floats, glows, flips its own pages in a language he somehow understands, then dissolves to dust as he blacks out hearing: *"Congratulations you have been granted the Va…."*

## First 5 episodes  *(the first sitting)*
The System wakes: daily quests, stat readouts, the sun penalty (Ep 3). He's drafted — gassed on the bus, bagged, dumped before three glass towers and a hidden city; the wristwatch is strapped on as his "lifeline" and power-level display (Ep 3). The level test scores him 5/5/5 in sun = Level 1 (Ep 6). His group: **Vorden Blade** (copies abilities by touch; an Original), **Peter Chuck** (timid, no ability, given the free military Earth book), **Layla Munrow** (Level 2 telekinesis, beast-bone bow), **Erin Heley** (Level 5 ice, "Ice Queen"). Quinn refuses the free Earth book — the first refusal his instructor has seen — and rooms with Vorden and Peter.

## First 20 episodes  *(texture band)*
Fighting is the fastest XP; Quinn gains **Inspect** (Name/Race/Ability/HP/Blood type + two ??? fields, Ep 11). The hierarchy bites: **Momo** (Level 6) warns the trio off; first-years extort credits at the stairs. Quinn masks up, ambushes bully **Rylee** at night, levels to 2, and **evolves into a Halfling** — fangs, blood hunger, −1 HP/hour (Ep 17–18). Starving, he bites **Layla** in the library (Ep 19–20) for a Strength point and carries her to the doctor **Hayley**. Hook: Peter bursts in — "It's Vorden, he's in trouble!"

## HARD BLOCKS  *(opening-binding)*
1. **Outing Quinn's System / vampire identity to everyone as the promo's cornerstone breaks the show.** The secret is the engine: *"If others were to find out of his rare ability it would start a bidding war between the private companies and the military"* (Ep 20). A near-miss cliffhanger (almost caught) is fine; a *delivered* public reveal is not.
2. **The wristwatch permanently displays Level 1 for Quinn.** It reads mutant-cell count but cannot read vampire MC (confirmed Ep 95). It is a NUMBER on a watch face — not a star, gem, or colored ring. No "squad mode."
3. **The danger is exploitation + surveillance, not a vampire hunt.** No academy-wide "kill vampires on sight" doctrine; "Pure" is an anti-*ability* faction, not a vampire-hunting order. Frame as "if they find out what he is, they'll take him apart for it."
4. **The book is gone.** It dissolved into Quinn on activation (Ep 1) and cannot be shown or referenced as an object afterward.
5. **Activation requires Quinn's own blood on the book.** No other trigger.
6. **Vorden's multiple personalities are a private secret.** Show switches as behavioral shifts; never name/explain the system upfront.
7. **Adults do not protect the weak.** Guards and teachers watch bullying and do nothing — by design (Ep 19, Ep 14).

## Entry map  *(where deeper elements enter — tease ok, anchor flags)*
- **Blood-type → stat mechanic** — enters ~Ep 11 (Inspect shows type); pays off ~Ep 26 (A→Str, B→Agi, AB→Stam) and ~Ep 36 (O→1 free stat point).
- **Soul weapons / awakening** — explained ~Ep 27; first seen ~Ep 46 (Momo's living whip).
- **Vorden's Raten personality** — surfaces ~Ep 44 ("Finally, I, Raten, have returned").
- **Peter's betrayal** — ~Ep 44 (lured Vorden to the assembly-hall ambush for the second-years).
- **VR "Power Fighter" / "Blood Evolver" alias** — ~Ep 50–52.
- **Portals (green/orange/red) & Planet Pioletic** — ~Ep 59.
- **Bloodsucker form** — first triggers ~Ep 80 (0 HP from hunger).
- **Shadow ability (Level 6)** — book activates ~Ep 89; cloak/storage ~Ep 91.
- **Big Four Families / Truedream** — load-bearing ~Ep 95 (steal abilities; rival the military).

## Engines the opening supports
1. **Secret Identity (PROVEN):** the watch makes weakness public while the System makes Quinn secretly dangerous — every scene runs on "they can't know." Best-performing MVS engine.
2. **Underdog Awakening (PROVEN, Q2 risk):** Level-1 runt levels into a Halfling; risk is resolving the book-mystery too early and bleeding Q2.

## IM sources — opening window
- **Blasted into a wall, flips off the bully** (Ep 1): public humiliation answered with defiance. *Injustice, T1*
- **Refuses the free Earth book** (Ep 6): chooses dignity over the military's debt-trap. *Institutional, T2*
- **Gassed, bagged, dumped on a field** (Ep 3): brutal onboarding of children. *Institutional, T2*
- **Bites Layla to survive** (Ep 19–20): the monster he's becoming, against his will. *Internal/moral, T1*

**Injustice density** *(primary conversion driver):*
- **T1 (mandatory):** the visible wristwatch number — everyone can see he's Level 1, so every cruelty is publicly licensed.
- **T2 (supporting):** institutional design — adults profit from the bullying and let it run.
- **T3 (deepening):** the body-horror cost — hunger, sun-weakness, the bite he can't refuse.
- **Drift risk:** if a promo leads with vampire power instead of the humiliation, the empathy nerve never fires and CTI collapses.

# LAYER 2 — RAILS (eps ~20–99)  ·  *load at W1 (threads) + canon gate (limits) · ≤ ~380 words*

## 2a · Conversion Threads
1. **Will Quinn be found out?** — pressure builds across the run (Hayley's bite-mark investigation ~Ep 95; Leo seeing his purple aura ~Ep 95). The core open loop; *initiate* the near-miss, never deliver the public reveal.
2. **Who beat and exposed Vorden — and why** — assembly-hall torture ~Ep 44; Peter's betrayal is the gut-punch behind it. Sells "your weakest friend sold you out."
3. **What is Vorden, really?** — Raten emerges ~Ep 44; the body holds more than one person. A second mystery layer under the first.
4. **The bloodsucker he can't control** — first trigger ~Ep 80; Quinn's own body becomes the monster. Tease the loss of control, withhold the aftermath.
5. **The world is bigger than the academy** — red portals and Planet Pioletic ~Ep 59; the Big Four Families rival the military ~Ep 95. Frame as scale, not as the promise to pay off.

## 2b · Canon Limits / Never-Happens
- **The show never lets Quinn operate openly as a vampire among allies who all know.** The secret is single-keeper-deep at the entry window (Layla, then Vorden); broad openness is post-run / off-canon.
- **The show never gives Quinn a soul weapon in the first year.** Soul weapons come after awakening (second-year material).
- **The show never makes the wristwatch a squad-assigner or beast-cell reader.** It reads MC, shows a number, handles credits, gates entry — nothing more.
- **The show never has a vampire clan, the Talen Clan, "Boneclaw," or a vampire ally "Fex" inside eps 1–99.** Those do not appear in the full run; do not build on them.
