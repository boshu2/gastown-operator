## World Mechanics

### Geography (two phases)
- **Phase 1 — Blazehollow (eps 1–~36):** small southern-marches town under Governor Archie Cooke. Rainlovers' Terrace (the estate), Eastwind Spire/Square (the selection arena), the edge-of-town cottage, and **Red Twill Tor** (brutal hill-forest; spirit herbs, wildbeasts, Kingfisher Lake). The listener entry point.
- **Phase 2 — Pyreborough / Amberbourne (eps ~37–100):** the capital + **Amberbourne College/Institute** (Phoenix Court, Phoenix Tor, Everwake/Everwake Lake, the Penley set). Elite tiers: the **Skyward Reliquary** (a.k.a. The Cloister), and the **Inner Sanctum** above it (Duncan Ashdown's seat; the cure lives here). The Skyward Directorate is the school's uppermost division. Ashdown Manor, Thunderbolt Hall, Stormfell Manor, the Vermillion Bird Fellowship sit in the capital's power web.

### Beastmaster cultivation
- Every person on the **Saffronreach Continent** has a **Vitalbound Expanse** — the inner space where the lifebound beast's egg forms (normally hatches ~a week after birth). Jasper's anomalously holds **10 eggs**.
- **Tiers:** **Beastblood** (9 levels; master + beast each link 9 beastblood points into a circuit, refining spirit energy into **Beast Essence**) → **Spiritwell** → **Spiritsource** (stores far more energy; the number of spiritsources formed = the beast's star count, e.g. a 6-star beast → 6 spiritsources each) → up toward **Vermillion-bird level**.
- **Star ratings:** newborn lifebound beasts show stars in the eyes = **potential**; wildbeast stars = current strength. 1-star = common/weak; 5–6 star = elite; further split into low/mid/high tier. Star rating ≈ social destiny.
- **Battle arts** split into martial (human) + bestial (beast) skills, sharpened by the bond. Five ranks: **Beast → Source → Concord → Heavenly → Saint.**

### Manna
- A "divine gift made solid" (stone/crystal/liquid). When matched to a beast's type it triggers **evolution**, raising star level + the master's ceiling. Four grades: **royal (lowest) → profound → terrestrial → celestial** (also rendered as imperial/royal). Even the lowest can push a beast toward 7 stars (a "Royal Beast"). Source unknown.

### Spirit ore / herbs
- Spirit energy saturates the world; stone becomes **spirit ore/gems**, plants become **spirit herbs**, both marked by **heavenly patterns** in nine colours: **red (lowest) · orange · yellow · green · blue · indigo · violet · black · white (rarest).** Gems are condensed energy, traded as currency and used to cultivate.
- Jasper can eat gems/herbs **raw** (fire resistance from the phoenix bond) for the full charge, then refine via the **Evernight Embers Tome / infernalsource** — far faster than orthodox academy technique (the Darkgold Technique).

### Felix — the Primordial Chaos Beast
- One of the **Primordial Chaos Beasts**; true form the **Everblaze Inferno Phoenix**. The star orb cannot read his real bloodline — it shows only the surface (one star pre-manna, six post-manna); "each star was like a whole galaxy." Manna only "loosens his bloodline shackles" — full restoration needs far more.
- Unusual: despite 6 stars, he grants Jasper only **a single spiritsource** (not six), formed at Jasper's core.
- Fire is meaningless to him — the structural edge in early fights (vs Sophie's Flaming Hawk, Albie's Purple-Eyed Bright Bird).

### The black arm / third eye (UNRESOLVED)
- Transforms ep 9: skin to black hexagonal scales, nails to crimson talons, palm splits into a **third eye** Jasper can see through. Matches the **dark shadow** that hunted Felix in the hatching vision ("master of all primordial chaos and rebirth"). Properties: monstrous strength, armour-like defence, near-instant healing.
- Houses a **spirit core** that later accepts the **Maze Gazer** illusion technique (~ep 26): 81 illusion spirit veins form inside the eye; projects **desire**, not fear (freezes opponents with an irresistible image). Origin and meaning unresolved through ep 100.

### The Saintbeast Battle Soul
- Found by Jasper/Midas in the **Abyssal Battlefield** ~3 years prior. Saint beasts sit at the peak of lifebound evolution; their **battle souls** form centuries after death (a change in the beast soul), carry little resentment, and can be guided into a beastmaster's spiritsources for an "amazing" ability — but the soul is **unstable** until fully refined.
- Midas had barely begun absorbing it (refining into the spiritsources on his feathers) when the betrayal struck — which is why Niall could seize it at all, and why he **plucked Midas's feathers** to extract it. Niall has likely **not** fully mastered it even after 3 years.

## Flavor notes
- **The opening is a triple-betrayal engine** — romantic (Lydia), violent (Niall kills Midas), and parental-in-effect (the credit/future stolen, then his father disowns him). Every promo's empathy lives here.
- **Felix is asset and disguise at once** — a god-tier beast that reads as "a filthy little chicken." His banter is the show's comic release valve; never let it lead over the injustice.
- **The cure is the real prize behind the revenge** — Jasper's drive is split: punish Niall/Lydia AND reach the Reliquary in time to save his mother. The clock (lifesbane, ~6 months) is the urgency engine.
- **The grandfather mirrors the father** — both Duncan Ashdown and Archie Cooke abandon their own blood without flinching; the betrayal pattern repeats at a higher tier.
- **Two men loved Freya** — Harvey Blackwood and Colson Garside, both far stronger and "better looking than Archie Cooke." Quiet patronage flows to Jasper from this.
- **The black arm is the buried mystery** — tied to the shadow that "killed" Felix in a past life; it is the show's deepest unanswered question and must stay unanswered as a promo loop.
- **Money/stakes are explicit** — the Order of the Amber Flame, spirit-gem economies, the assassination bounty (1,000 yellow-patterned spirit gems), loans from Sage Blackwood — concrete, calculable pressure.

## Relationship map (ch 100)
- **Jasper ↔ Felix:** brother-bond; Felix is family ("we're a team now"), comic foil, and secret god-tier weapon.
- **Jasper ↔ Freya:** the foundation; everything repays her sacrifice and races her clock.
- **Jasper ↔ Niall / Lydia:** unresolved revenge; they hold his stolen Soul, story, and reputation; Lydia escalates to a kill-bounty.
- **Jasper ↔ Archie / Duncan Ashdown:** father and grandfather, both of whom discard their own blood; the cure sits behind Duncan's shut door.
- **Jasper ↔ Maisie:** fated, inexplicable bond + perfect sync; she boosts him in battle and quietly raises his status; love interest. **Jasper ↔ Elowen:** prickly ally (the Sixth Princess) who warms to him.
- **Jasper ↔ Esme Penley / Harvey Blackwood / Colson Garside:** hidden patrons (the latter two once loved Freya).
- **Jasper ↔ Euan / Albie / Leo / Gemma & Rhys:** the ladder of rivals he climbs — Blazehollow nobodies up to Thunderbolt Hall and Ashdown Manor.
- **Felix ↔ the dark shadow:** the cosmic hunter that killed him in a past life — now mirrored in Jasper's black arm.
