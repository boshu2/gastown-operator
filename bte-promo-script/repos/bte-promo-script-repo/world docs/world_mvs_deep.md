## The System (Quinn's, full)

- **Race progression:** Human → Halfling (Ep 17, after Level 2) → Vampire (after first human kill, ~Ep 86). Irreversible; each changes his appearance.
- **Stats:** HP, Strength, Agility, Stamina, plus later Charm. Level-up grants 1 stat point; optional blood quests grant extra.
- **Sun penalty:** direct sunlight halves all stats (Ep 3). UV-based; mitigations introduced later (Deathbat ring at the shop).
- **Blood hunger:** −1 HP/hour until human blood is consumed (Ep 17); ~48-hour cycle; combat healing draws the timer forward.
- **Blood bank:** stores 100ml; auto-heals; a "second life" in combat.
- **Blood-type → stat (one per person, lifetime):** A → +1 Strength, B → +1 Agility, AB → +1 Stamina, **O → +1 free stat point** (Ep 26 establishes A/B/AB; Ep 36 confirms O via Loop's O− blood). Repeat donors give no further stat.
- **Inspect (Lv1→Lv2):** Name / Race / Ability / HP / Blood type + two locked ??? fields. Blurs in sunlight. At Lv2 reads objects (e.g., lock combinations) and blood samples.
- **Skills:** Blood swipe (Lv1, Ep 17 — 5m red energy line, −1 HP/use, no cooldown); later Blood spray; Daze; Charm.
- **Tutorials:** a blonde "creator" video teaches **Flash step** (≥15 Agility, ~5m, costs stamina, afterimage) and **Hammer strike** (≥15 Strength, internal-force punch) — Ep 40.
- **Bloodsucker form (Ep 80):** triggered at 0 HP *from hunger*; bald scalp, claws, black eyes; all stats doubled, HP halved, blood skills off, mind gone; attracted to human-blood smell; cannot tell friend from enemy.
- **Shadow ability (Level 6, "Blooded"-only, Ep 89+):** requires vampire cells; dimensional storage (objects sink into shadows), shadow control/cloak; fueled by MC.
- **Blood Ritual (Ep 92):** can turn a person "blooded" (loses their current ability); 2 slots, unused in-window.
- **Shop (unlocks Level 10):** beast-crystal currency; known item Deathbat Ring (3 hours of safe sunlight).

## World mechanics

- **Power levels 1–8**, subdivided 1.1–1.9; determined by activatable mutant cells (MC), not raw strength — skill beats level (Erin, Quinn). One mutation per human lifetime (one ability for life); Quinn is exempt because he's no longer human.
- **Ability books:** Levels 1–5 public; 6–8 military/corporate-controlled. The free military Earth book = a debt-trap pipeline (Ep 5–6).
- **Originals:** families who kept abilities private; often immeasurable by the watch; strongest rumored above Level 8.
- **Soul weapons / awakening:** second-year skill; unique weapon grown from MC; strength scales with MC regardless of the ability's combat value (Momo).
- **Wristwatch:** reads MC, displays a number, holds credits, gates entry, tracks location (curfew 10 PM). Cannot read Quinn's vampire MC (Ep 95).
- **Beast weapons:** crafted from beast crystals (tier = quality); activated by ability/MC for +5% (basic). Quinn's gauntlets: +3 Str, +2 Def, blood-swipe +5%.
- **Portals:** green (safe), orange (partial), red (abandoned/undiscovered, no rescue). **Planet Pioletic** — ruined first off-world settlement, six-month night, Glathrium construction (only Leo's blade cuts it), beasts Rattaclaw/Scordana/Deathbat.
- **VR "Power Fighter" (Ep 50+):** cross-school capsule combat using your real stats; Quinn's alias **Blood Evolver**; his red blood-swipe draws a "hacker?" forum thread.

## Flavor notes

- The wristwatch number **is** the injustice engine — humiliation is publicly verifiable; "Level 1 beside a Level 5" is the show's central image.
- Sweet blood-smell is the recurring sensory anchor and a dramatic-irony generator near any injured person.
- Double-layered secret by run's end: vampire identity **and** the Level-6 shadow ability, both invisible to the watch — two distinct cliffhanger shapes.
- The "little one"/Raten democracy inside Vorden is the show's most unconventional worldbuilding — a Self-Sacrifice engine hidden inside a Secret-Identity show.
