## World Mechanics

### The Nightmare Spell
- Appeared decades ago amid recovery from natural disasters and resource wars. Caused fatigue → unnatural slumber → death; the dead became Nightmare Creatures who overran militaries.
- The **Awakened** (trial survivors) stopped the rampage. Spell still infects randomly; carriers must surrender (Third Special Directive). Police vaults have armored walls, turret nests, restraint chairs.
- Falling asleep = entering the **First Nightmare** (a luck-dependent trial). Die inside → a Gate opens in the real world releasing a monster. Surviving Aspirants become **Dreamers/Sleepers**.

### Power Hierarchy (human ranks)
Aspirant → Dreamer/Sleeper (survived First Nightmare; dormant core) → Awakened (survived Dream Realm via a Gateway) → Ascended/Master (Second Nightmare; physically cross worlds at will; 3 stars) → Transcendent/Saint (Third Nightmare; demigod; can carry others). Supreme/Sacred/Divine are theoretical — no human has reached them.

### Aspect / Memory / Echo / Flaw / True Name
- **Aspect** ranks: Dormant, Awakened, Ascended, Transcendent, Supreme, Sacred, **Divine**. Types: combat / sorcery / utility. Sunny's evolved from Dormant Temple Slave to **Divine Shadow Slave** in one Nightmare — unheard of.
- **Soul Cores** = basis of power; improved by consuming **Soul Shards** from kills. Creatures hold multiple cores (beast 1 … tyrant 5 …).
- **Memories** = summonable magical items tied to a slain creature's essence; weightless, undetectable, can be carried to the real world. 7 ranks; tiered I–VII within rank.
- **Echoes** = extremely rare; a summonable creature companion forged from a kill.
- **Flaws** = the price of an Aspect Ability, received with the first ability; harmless → fatal.
- **True Names** = honorary titles; the highest mark of excellence. Sunny and Nephis both earned theirs in their First Nightmares (almost unheard of).

### Sunny's exception
- **Shadow Core**, not soul core. Cannot consume soul shards; instead earns **Shadow Fragments** by killing (12/1000 at ep 16; 24/1000 by ep 47). Soul shards are currency only.
- **Shadow Control** ability: an independent shadow that scouts, shares his sight/hearing, enhances his body or weapons (raw punch 9 → 18 shadow-enhanced, ep 27), cannot speak, cannot touch matter. Snarky personality (facepalms, shrugs, pouts, thumbs-up, ignores him).
- **Transform Echo into a Shadow** option exists (ep 47) but costs 100 Shadow Fragments — unused in-window.
- **Blood Weave** (ep 81) — inherited Attribute; lets him see the inner structure of Memories (nexuses, enchantments, tiers).

## The Dream Realm & The Forgotten Shore

- **Dream Realm:** vast dead world; Sleepers enter on the first winter solstice after their First Nightmare and stay until they find a **Gateway** (exit = becoming Awakened). No fairness rules — any rank can appear anywhere.
- **Forgotten Shore:** an unknown, unmapped region with **NO Gateway** — everyone who arrives is trapped. Sunny, Nephis, Cassie spend ~2 months there.
- **Dark Sea:** tidal, tied to day/night. At night black water floods everything; at dawn it recedes to reveal the labyrinth. Hides colossal deep horrors.
- **Crimson Labyrinth:** vast maze of red coral-like material; all monsters Awakened-rank+.
- **Carapace Legion:** Scavengers (beasts) → Centurions (monsters) → the Carapace Demon. Cursed soldiers of a fallen **Starlight Legion**.
- **Headless Knight Statue:** ~200 m stone knight, seven stars on breastplate, head torn off — Sunny's first shelter (ep 31).
- **Ashen Barrow / Soul Devouring Tree:** hill crowned by the enthralling tree; the Demon is its guardian.
- **Giant beheaded goddess statue:** her outstretched stone hand (seven stars on the robe) is the trio's final refuge (ep 95–97); matches Cassie's "seven severed heads / seven seals" vision.
- **The cursed city + Crimson Spire:** walled ruin reached at ch 100; the Spire (seven locks) ties to Cassie's apocalyptic prophecy. The trio is on the wall, not yet inside, when the source ends.

## Relationship Map (ch 100 state)
- **Sunny ↔ Nephis:** respect forged in survival; she taught him to fight, he saved her from the Soul Devourer, she healed/self-resurrected. Distance remains — he doesn't fully trust her past. *(Both alive, together, at ch 100.)*
- **Sunny ↔ Cassie:** protective; he carries her, she engineers his escape from the Soul Devourer. She seems to know things about his future.
- **Nephis ↔ Cassie:** Nephis protects Cassie "because she wants to" — gave Cassie her armor, tied them with a golden rope.
- **Sunny ↔ Caster:** adversarial; Sunny self-humiliates to seem harmless. Caster knows Nephis's identity — a wildcard if he links them.
- **Sunny ↔ Master Jet:** mentor; outskirts kinship; "no one survives the Dream Realm alone."

## Tonal Execution Guide (Adventure Frame)
Target: **"darkly funny adventure in a terrifying world."** Same material, adventure POSTURE. Five levers:
1. **Loot moments** — write Memory/Echo drops as curious "what is this?" discoveries, shadow inspecting them — not exhausted relief.
2. **Visible counters** — linger on the number climbing (punch 9→18; Shadow Fragments). Treat like a level-up.
3. **Discovery over dread** — the FIRST adjective for any new place/creature must evoke scale/beauty/strangeness, danger second.
4. **Agency over victimhood** — no beat sits in helplessness >1 line; the moment injustice lands, Sunny's scheming starts.
5. **Shadow as comedy companion** — visibly reacting at least every other scene; brief one-line insertions ("His shadow facepalmed.").

**Scene check:** "Is this discovery or dread?" Dread on >2 consecutive scenes = Police-Station territory; reframe. **Beat-sheet check:** ≥2 comedy beats in the first third or it reads as suffering.

## Flavor Notes
- **The Flaw is the hook.** Cannot lie → constant dramatic irony; every social beat is a minefield. Truth-as-deception is his signature move (publicly "claims" a Divine Aspect; no one believes him).
- **The shadow is a silent comedy partner** and the show's stickiest, most expandable IM vehicle.
- **Nephis is the inverse of her image** — a combat goddess who is socially a disaster, hiding a dead family and an agonizing Flaw (healing "burns alive").
- **Cassie is never dead weight** — her oracle/"remember five" plan is Vol. 1's smartest move; underestimating her is a trap the show sets for the audience.
- **"To spite you all"** is the emotional contract — Sunny survives not because he deserves to but because the universe doesn't deserve the satisfaction of killing him.
