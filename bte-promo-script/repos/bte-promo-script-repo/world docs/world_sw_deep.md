## World Mechanics

- **Magic Circuits.** Internal pathways channeling mana through a wand. Destroyed circuits = no magic (Ethan's condition, ch 1). "Ars Vim Extermina" destroys them permanently.
- **Appraisal Stone.** Basketball-sized white crystal; measures power at sorting; determines Manor (ch 7–8). Reaction = caste.
- **Origin Magic.** Each wizard/witch's unique signature (ch 32). Ethan's = **absorption**: kiss a Witch, copy a portion of her power for one day; once per Witch per week; only Witches (ch 63). Requires the partial circuit-healing from Luna's Moon ceremony to function at all.
- **Resonance.** Uniting Origin + Core (wand) magic. **Partial Resonance** (Ethan, ch ~62) is rare and extraordinary; the Mermaid Princess actively helps produce it. Not a repeatable spell.
- **Mana Deficiency.** Overuse → collapse (Alice, ch 11).
- **Spells (Latin-derived).** "Ars Vim Extermina" (circuit destruction), "Nubes Atra Fumus" (black smokescreen), "Ignis Fulmine" (fire bolt), "Imbre Glacies"/"Glacies Carcerem" (ice), "Silentium" (silence), "Ethan Principium"/"Inceptos Porta Aperiam" (ceremony/portal).

## The Academy

- **Five Manors**, each named for one of the Five Heroes who defeated the Demon Lord (ch 5):
  - **Schwartz** (Agmundr Schwartz) — Dark/Black Magic; Langston's; ran this year's Initiation.
  - **Terra** (Svend Terra) — Nature/growth; Kurt's.
  - **Jaeger** (Magni Jaeger) — beast taming/transformation/summoning; Lily's; Prof. Njal.
  - **Eques** (Eileifr Eques) — combat wizards/Magic Knights/Magistratus pipeline; Alice & Nicole's.
  - **Dud** (Fortis Dud) — those with little/no magic; the "Drop Out" Manor; founded by the academy's creator.
- **Pillars.** Each year the top first-years (ages 15–17), tuition waived, 500 Gold Coins/month (ch 7).
- **Initiation Ceremony.** A dungeon designed by Schwartz to fail nearly everyone; zombies multiply the more magic you use; clear by finding the exit or surviving 2 hours (ch 9–13).
- **Currency.** 1 Gold = 100 Silver = 10,000 Copper; potato chips 5 Copper (ch 7 A/N).

## Geography

- **Eastshire** — the academy's territory in the Magical World (ch 19).
- **Brynhildr Academy** — ~500 years old (ch 6); reached via the underground **Brynhildr Express** from an ordinary station, then carriages by ticket class.
- **Great Eagle Forest** — surrounds the academy; home to Koko, the Fairies' Garden, Satyrs, treants; site of the Dud Manor camping trip (ch 43–49).
- **Limeburgh** — nearest town; Sanders (wand shop), Robert's Aviary, broom shop (ch 19–20).
- **Magic Bureau** — governs the Wizard World; Alice's father is its Minister (ch 4).

## Key Items / Artifacts

- **Black Bracelet.** Given by the train snack-lady; faked the Appraisal spark that read Ethan "Dud"; removal = memory wipe (ch 3, 8).
- **Moulding Ball.** Magical Cold Iron; becomes any visualized weapon; Ethan shapes it into his pitchfork (ch 9).
- **Fortis Dud's blue ring.** Storage space "the size of the training ground"; recognizes only chosen masters (rejected the Headmaster); seven abilities, six unknown; chose Ethan (ch 14).
- **Shield of Aeneas** (Nicole) / **Goblet of Fire** (Chloe) — Initiation rewards (ch 14).
- **White Pearls** — consumable light source ("Lux Aeterna" without magic) usable by Duds (ch ~32).

## Flavor Notes

- **The pitchfork is a character statement, not a gag.** Cold-iron Moulding Ball shaped into the farm tool he trusts; he beheads the Boss Zombie with it (ch 13).
- **Comedy is load-bearing.** Agnes's spiked club, candy-extorting Fairies, a gorilla rating humans by taste — the world is likeable before it turns lethal.
- **The Fortis Dud parallel** — both Duds; the founder's quote "Heroes are ordinary people who make themselves extraordinary" (ch 14) is the thesis; spiritual-successor framing is subtext, not confirmed.
- **Ophelia's relief.** Her terror-to-relief at the "Dud" reading (ch 8) shows how close Ethan came to "a fate worse than death" — the hidden-rescue beat under the humiliation.
