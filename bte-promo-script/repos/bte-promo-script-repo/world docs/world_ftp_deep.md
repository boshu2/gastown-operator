## World Mechanics

### The Godforge Technique (HARD-BLOCK core — absolutely secret)
- Granted by the hidden **gold script** inside the Codex of Ascension (one-time, ep 3): "Godforge Technique. The Original Method of Forging. Transform flesh into forge, body into weapon. Purify the vessel through suffering, cleanse it for the awakening of true vitality" (ep 4).
- **Body becomes a literal mystical weapon.** Magical weapons have five stages: Mysterious → Spirit → Mystic → Sacred Arms → Divine Arms (each top/middle/lower grade). Zane's body forges up from "lower-grade Mysterious Weapon." Skin deflects blades (Jace's Hellfire Blade only scratches, ep 25); bones harden past steel; he can't be moved or seriously hurt by ordinary force.
- **Being beaten = taking an Aether Crystal.** Each blow produces *warmth* that purges impurities and advances cultivation ~1,000× faster than normal training. **Hard limit:** the effect scales with the *force* of the blow — once his durability outgrows an attacker, weak hits produce nothing; he needs progressively stronger opponents (or the kiln) to keep advancing.
- **Concealment is mandatory.** Zane must fake injury after every hit. Observers (Owen, Jace, Joe, the masters) only ever register an impossible toughness / "bizarre physique" they cannot explain.

### The nine-dragon Furnace / Cobalt Dragon / Dragon Scale system (secret)
- Awakened during the forging vision: a colossal blast furnace ("The Great Forge" / Ancient Cauldron) wreathed by **nine dragons** of different colors (cyan/Cobalt, black, white, purple, Storm Drakes…). Eight sleep; only the **Cobalt Dragon** at the bottom is awake.
- A **black flame** (Black Fire / Dragon's Breath Forge) lives in his Mindscape. It forges his *soul* in repeating cycles of agony ("Burned to slag, hammered flat, then reforged"). It also **consumes mystical weapons** — it dissolved the Hellfire Blade instantly and fed the molten droplets to the Cobalt Dragon, lighting a single **Dragon Scale** (Azure/Cobalt scales number ~10,000 on that one dragon alone).
- **Ceiling tease:** lighting every scale across all nine dragons is near-unimaginable — "even if I melted down every treasure in the entire Eastern Territory, it would barely be enough to activate the Cobalt Dragon." None of this is known to anyone.
- **Celestial Essence:** the Black Fire's byproduct when it melts a mystical weapon is the *same* silver-droplet substance the Capital's Great Houses bid 25,000+ Crystal Cubes for (ep 99–100) — "the fundamental component of all legendary arms," a spark of Cosmic Order no one knows how to extract. Zane makes it accidentally. Implication unresolved.

### Cultivation ranks (body-refining, not magic)
- **Skin Refining → Strength → Bone Refining → Core Strength → Marrow Refining**, then the **Nature Level** (command of Life Energy itself). Impurities purge outside-in: skin → flesh → bones (pale-white residue) → organs → spinal marrow.
- Milestones: ~1,000 lb single-hand force = "Immense Power," the mark of a true warrior. Reaching Bone Refining before 30 = a prodigy; before 16 = generational (no one in Castermere County had done it in 300 years — hence the untouched Aether Crystals).
- **Aether Crystals:** House Lowell's two sacred heirlooms; one crystal at one-tenth power jumps a user a full stage. Perrin and Andrew each stole one.

### The Skyreach Sect (primary arena, eps 28+)
- Occupies ~a quarter of The Capital; **33 peaks** (Misty Peak ranked last; Eagle Peak, Misty Peak named). Ruled by peak masters.
- **Disciple ranks:** applicants → pledges → **Outer Disciples** (White Robes) → **Core Apprentices** (Shadow Cloaks, more status/resources). Legacy/Great Houses (a "Legacy Charter") skip the entrance exam; everyone else faces the **Entrance Exam → Academy Trials (The Gauntlet / Bloody Mountain)**.
- **Points** = the Sect's lifeblood currency (training grounds, manuals, resources). Earned by missions or bought (~2 Crystal Cubes per Point after inflation). Penalties are brutal (1,000-Point deductions can derail a career).
- **Crystal Cubes** = the elite currency Empire-wide (stored Life Energy), used by Nature-Level masters; ordinary people use gold/silver.

### Castermere County / House Lowell (origin, eps 1–27)
- House Lowell rules Castermere County but ranks low in the Eastern Territory; once the county's strongest family, now fallen from the top three after Milo's death. Twelve branches remain (Zane's was the thirteenth, destroyed).
- The **Greenwind Blade** (Milo's, missing) was the county's only Legendary Weapon. The estate: Training Hall, Council Hall, Forge, Community Garden, new pavilions/ponds the comfortable uncles built — a House rotting from prosperity.
- Signature art: **Purple Flash Fist** (Ultraviolet Power; nearly a lost art). Its pinnacle is the **Shadow Fist** — one punch, multiple internal injuries; mastery measured by the number of impacts (Milo could land 8 at Bone Refining; Zane reaches 7).

## Flavor notes
- **The beating IS the engine** — the show's signature image is a child smiling through a beating because each blow makes him stronger, while faking agony so no one finds out. Every promo must carry the *injustice* of it, not just the cool of it.
- **Five stacked secrets** — weapon-body (Godforge), the nine-dragon Furnace/Black Fire, the Shattered Dagger, the Storage Ring, the soul-forge. None known to anyone; the gap between what Zane can do and what he can show is the core loop.
- **Concealment over flexing** — Zane wins quietly, fakes weakness, lays low (he refuses to provoke Caleb until cornered). Power is a secret he protects, not a banner.
- **Elara is the unreachable north star** — every gain is measured against the distance to Infernal Peak; the goal recedes (the Adrian power-gap, the pivot to Weapon Forging). Never delivered.
- **Class hierarchy at every altitude** — slaves vs Lowell kids → branches vs main branch → ordinary applicants vs Legacy Houses → Outer Disciples vs Core Apprentices vs Great Houses. The strong own the weak everywhere.
- **Two-phase world** — Phase 1 House Lowell / Castermere (eps 1–27, origin & enslavement); Phase 2 Skyreach Sect / The Capital (eps 28–100, primary arena). Promos default to one or the other; don't blur them.
- **Vengeance with a code** — Zane's rage "froze" rather than flared; he punishes rule-breakers by the very House rules they ignore. Principled, not feral.
