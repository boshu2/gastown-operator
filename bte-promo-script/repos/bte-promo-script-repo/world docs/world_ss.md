# LAYER 0 — ESSENCE  ·  *the only default load · ≤ ~550 words*

## Three Pillars (locked)
1. **Weak to Strong (protagonist at zero, orphan bg)**  *(Thruline — "everyone has been written off as worthless")*
2. **Nightmare Spell / Dream Trials**
3. **Hidden Identity (Shadow God / Powers)**

## Protagonist — Sunny (Sunless)
**Identity.** An 18-year-old outskirts orphan, malnourished and mistaken for a child, who surrenders to police as a Nightmare Spell carrier expecting to die. His First Nightmare drops him at the worst possible start — a chained slave with the "useless" Temple Slave Aspect — and he survives by scheming. Secret Identity + Underdog Awakening: the useless Aspect is secretly Divine.
**Combat.** Fights with his mind, not strength. Small and weak and stays that way; weaponizes everything — chains, gravity, poison, a blind tyrant's deafness, other people. Ambush and misdirection over confrontation.
**Power Source.** Temple Slave Aspect evolved to **Shadow Slave (Divine)** after he bled on a dead Shadow God's altar. He has a **Shadow Core**, not a soul core — fueled by Shadow Fragments earned by killing, never soul shards. Innate ability **Shadow Bond** is a trap: anyone who hears his True Name (Lost from Light) enslaves him absolutely. He CANNOT lie, absorb soul shards, or use his Aspect openly.
**Voice.** A self-talking schemer — sardonic, darkly funny, defiant under humiliation, never cocky. Complains while still moving; survives out of spite. Bitter humor as armor; a predator pretending to be prey.

**Voice Samples (verbatim — never paraphrase):**
1. "You people… Look at you! Planning to kill me and still insist on having a good excuse. How very convenient! I hate hypocrites like you the most." (Ep 13) — defiance
2. "But then I changed my mind. Somewhere along the way, I decided to survive. I must survive, no matter what." (Ep 13) — core motivation
3. "No. To spite you all." (Ep 13) — survival contract
4. "In my world, people had thousands of years to turn cruelty into art. And as someone on the receiving end of all that cruelty… don't you think I would know more about being vicious than anyone ever could?" (Ep 14) — survivor philosophy
5. "Your breasts." (Ep 17) — the Flaw forcing truth

**Drift tripwires.** Becomes cocky | reveals Aspect/True Name casually | wins by force | self-frames as chosen | shows mercy for emotional reasons | shadow described as powerful at entry (it's barely learned to move).

## Setting — Entry-Window World
**Feel.** Three phases: (1) a gray, resource-scarce real world — outskirts stench, a police-station vault with armored walls and turret nests; (2) the First Nightmare — a moonlit slave caravan chained on an icy mountain pass, a ruined black-marble temple at the peak; (3) the Awakened Academy — a red-gated fortress-city of snow and alloy. Power is brutal and unquestioned; trust is fatal.

**Sensory Samples (verbatim):**
1. "The skies were black… its light was not enough to pierce the darkness that enveloped the mountain. Only Sunny's eyes seemed to be able to do so." (Ep 10)
2. "Its colossal columns and walls were cut from black marble… it looked like a palace of a dark god. At least it did once. Now, the temple was in ruins." (Ep 14)

**Drift tripwires.** Sunny safe in sunlight | creatures showing mercy | allies fully trustworthy | outskirts as poor-but-noble | Academy as safe haven | Sleepers respecting Sunny (fear, not respect).

## Tone Constraint
Target is **"darkly funny adventure in a terrifying world,"** never "horror survival." Promos MUST counterbalance the dark world with Sunny's sardonic monologue and the shadow's silent comedy. Failure mode: unrelieved grimness reads as suffering, tanks the Fun gate, kills CTI (the Police-Station promo proved this).

# LAYER 1 — ACTIVATION CANON  ·  *load at W1 + canon gate · ≤ ~750 words*

## First 5 minutes  *(the 50%-drop gate)*
Sunny sits outside a police station drinking real coffee he can barely afford, because "his life was about to end," then walks in and surrenders as a Nightmare Spell carrier. Strapped into a restraint chair in an armored vault, a gray-haired cop gives him survival advice as he fades into forced sleep. Cold, gray, claustrophobic, quietly unjust: a kid the world has written off, surrendering himself to die.

## First 5 episodes  *(the first sitting)*
The First Nightmare drops Sunny — against all "fair trial" rules — into a chained slave caravan on a freezing pass. His status reads the **useless Temple Slave Aspect** and three Attributes (Fated, Mark of Divinity, Child of Shadows — the last gives night vision). A blind **Mountain King** — a 4-meter tyrant that turns its kills into larvae — crashes from the sky. Sunny strangles a larva with his shackle chain (ep 4) and lassoes the tyrant off a cliff with a chain and a runaway wagon (ep 6–7). Cast: slaves **Benny** (greedy coward) and **Fraser** (the Scholar, a hidden criminal), and **Gregor**, a young soldier who saves Sunny then plans to kill him.

## First 20 episodes  *(texture band)*
Sunny poisons the slavers' water with Bloodbane berries; it fails on Gregor (Awakened core), so he baits the blind tyrant onto Gregor instead (ep 14). Dying, he reaches the ruined temple, bleeds on the altar, and the dead **Shadow God** tears the tyrant apart. Appraisal "Glorious": True Name **Lost from Light**, Aspect evolves to **Shadow Slave (Divine)**, Flaw **Clear Conscience — cannot lie** (ep 15–17). He wakes in the vault, greeted by **Master Jet** (Ascended, outskirts-born), enrolls at the **Awakened Academy** (4 weeks to the solstice), meets silver-haired **Nephis** and blind **Cassie**, and builds a "pathetic lunatic" cover (ep 18–21).

## HARD BLOCKS  *(opening-binding)*
1. **True Name (Lost from Light) must never be spoken to another character as a resolved reveal.** Anyone who hears it enslaves him via Shadow Bond — the spine of the series. Near-exposure (tease) is fine; outing him breaks it.
2. **Sunny cannot lie (Flaw: Clear Conscience).** Direct questions force a truthful answer; he can only bend the *wording* through pain. He deceives via misleading truth, never a lie.
3. **His Divine Aspect must stay secret.** He performs as a pathetic outskirts rat — even publicly "claiming" a Divine Aspect because no one believes him. Load-bearing cover.
4. **Shadow Core, not soul core.** He CANNOT absorb soul shards; he gains Shadow Fragments only by killing. Unique progression — don't write him farming shards.
5. **The shadow cannot speak or touch the material world.** Silent body-language only; it passes through objects.
6. **Sunny is physically weak and stays weak.** Power is shadow-enhancement and tactics, not muscle (raw punch 9; doubled to 18 only via shadow).

## Entry map  *(where deeper elements enter — tease ok, anchor flags)*
- **The independent Shadow (personality — facepalms, shrugs)** — enters **~ep 18** (police-station shower, post-First-Nightmare). NOT in the opening bands. *Anchoring a pre-Aspect promo on the living shadow flags.*
- **Divine Aspect / True Name / cannot-lie Flaw** — ~ep 15–17.
- **Nephis** — ~ep 19; Immortal Flame lineage revealed ~ep 27. **Cassie (blind oracle)** — ~ep 19; powers ~ep 35+. **Caster (Ascended Legacy)** — ~ep 26.
- **Dream Realm / Forgotten Shore / dark-sea labyrinth** — ~ep 30. **Echo (monster mount)** — ~ep 47. **Carapace Demon** — ~ep 63. **Blood Weave / Memory-sight** — ~ep 81.

## Engines the opening supports
1. **Underdog Awakening (PROVEN):** zero-resource kid, "useless" Aspect, survives a tyrant by wit. First Nightmare is a clean Journey.
2. **Secret Identity (PROVEN):** Divine Aspect + True Name + can't-lie = a triple-layered near-exposure engine, comedy-fueled at the Academy.
3. **Revenge / Justice Denied (MODERATE):** "to spite you all" — his bond to the world is adversarial.

## IM sources — opening window
- **Useless Aspect reveal** (Ep 2): the Spell mocks it as a "useless wretch." *System Discovery, T2*
- **Mountain King crashes from the sky** (Ep 4): blind 4-meter tyrant, larvae from its kills. *Power-Ceiling, T1*
- **Chain-lassoes the tyrant off a cliff** (Ep 6–7): chains + wagon + gravity. *Status Inversion, T1*
- **Shadow God's blessing** (Ep 14): a dead god's shadows tear the tyrant apart. *World Border, T1*
- **Divine Aspect + cannot-lie Flaw** (Ep 15–17): the most cunning character can never lie. *System Discovery, T1*

**Injustice density** *(primary conversion driver):*
- **T1 (mandatory):** the world has written Sunny off as disposable — orphan, slum rat, "not even people," surrendering himself to die before the story starts.
- **T2:** the Spell rigs his trial — worst possible start (chained slave + "useless" Aspect) where everyone else gets a fair shot.
- **T3:** every ally is a potential killer (Benny, Fraser, Gregor all turn on him); trust is punished.
- **Drift risk:** open on monster spectacle and skip the "written-off" injustice → strong engagement, dead CTI (Police-Station failure).

# LAYER 2 — RAILS (eps ~20–100)  ·  *load at W1 (threads) + canon gate (limits) · ≤ ~400 words*

## 2a · Conversion Threads
1. **Will anyone discover his Divine Aspect / True Name?** — runs the whole show, paid off in near-misses at the Academy (~ep 21) and Caster's suspicion (~ep 27). The engine; tease, never resolve.
2. **What is the living shadow actually capable of?** — opens ~ep 18, escalates at the punching machine (~ep 27, doubles his power) and into scouting/enhancement on the Forgotten Shore (~ep 30+). The show's stickiest, most fun loop.
3. **Who is the silver-haired girl, really?** — Nephis revealed as the last heir of the legendary Immortal Flame clan ~ep 27; her healing-that-burns and self-resurrection pay off ~ep 95.
4. **What did the blind girl see?** — Cassie's oracle visions (the castle, the apocalyptic seven-seals prophecy) seed across ~ep 30–95; her "remember five" plan saves the group from the Soul Devourer ~ep 83.
5. **Can three weak Sleepers survive the worst region in the Dream Realm?** — the Forgotten Shore has NO Gateway (~ep 30); pays off ~ep 95–97 when they finally reach the cursed city's wall.

## 2b · Canon Limits / Never-Happens
- **The show never lets Sunny win by strength.** Every victory is a trick, a trap, or shadow-enhancement — never out-muscling an enemy.
- **The show never makes the Flaw go away or let him lie.** It's permanent; he only learns to bend wording.
- **The shadow never speaks and never physically affects matter.** Silent body-language only.
- **The Forgotten Shore never offers an easy exit.** No Gateway; reaching the cursed city wall is the end of the source window, NOT salvation or a way home.
- **Through ch 100, Nephis never dies and Sunny is never exiled / never lives alone with a "Black Knight."** That material is beyond this source — do not anchor on it. At ch 100 all three are alive together, meeting a friendly Awakened (Effie) at the city.
