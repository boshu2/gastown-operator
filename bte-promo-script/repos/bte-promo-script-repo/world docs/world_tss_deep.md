## World Mechanics

- **Two power paths.** Aura (strong body, gathers Aura via the Nexus → warriors) vs Magic (mental energy → mages). Conventional wisdom: a strong body means weak mind and vice-versa; no one in centuries has trained both. Serena trains both (unprecedented).
- **Ranking.** First five ranks = "Apprentice" (Magician/Fighter). At Sixth Rank you specialize. Magic → Magus (offensive), **Warlock** (Curses, hand-signs, no chanting), or Priest (holy). Aura → Swordsman, Knight, or Archer. Serena chose **Archer** (Aura) and is secretly pursuing **Warlock**. Magus ladder: Apprentice → Junior → Intermediate → Senior → Great Magus → Archmagus → **Great Archmagus**. 5 stars per rank; ~4–5 yrs per star.
- **Combat math (ep 6).** A warrior's strength ≈ 40% Aura/Magic, 30% mythological beast, 30% holy weapons (holy weapons rare, so historically ~50/50 self vs beast). Hence beast-bonding is second only to self-cultivation.
- **The Seven Stars Moon Seal.** Seven layers, placed at birth, suppressing all power (and her true face). Layer 1 (ep 2): unlocks Aura+Magic training but caps progress. Layer 2 (ep 41): restores original beautiful face, lifts ceiling. Each break = pain/blackout. Lucien needs absorbed energy to power each break.
- **Demonic cores.** Dropped by Demon Beasts (malevolent species, distinct from magical beasts; high ones shapeshift to human form, eat human organs). Cores hold dense dark energy — corrupting, mostly considered useless scrap, sold cheap at auction. Lucien absorbs them via Serena's body (light streams into her brow). To break seal 2 Lucien demanded 30,000 low-grade (or ~2,000 high-grade) cores — driving the heist arc. Later seals need mid/high-grade cores.
- **Interspatial rings.** Storage devices; grade = capacity (her first: ~175 cu ft; Abel's gift: ~3,500 cu ft).
- **Disguise Potions.** Three grades; low-grade lasts ~3 hrs, 5 bottles cost 500 gold. Serena also uses cheap cosmetics/tinctures (her thief kit) to fake plainness after her face is restored.

## Geopolitics

- **The Ardunian Empire**, on the Brilliance Continent, founded under five ancient mythological beasts. Ruled by a Monarch; five Great Houses.
- **The Five Great Houses:** Phoenix, (Azure) Dragon, (White) Tiger, (Black) Tortoise, Griffin. Each tied to one of the five founding beasts — all of which fell dormant ~a century ago; none re-awakened until the Sage returns. Houses feign friendship but are rivals. Griffin is the richest (vaults rival the Imperial Treasury). Serena robs Phoenix, Dragon, Tiger, Tortoise — never Griffin (her fence). Hits the Imperial Treasury too (~700 high-grade cores, ep 40–41).
- **The God Realm.** A small, army-less but untouchable realm of "divine envoys"; no empire dares challenge it. Source of Sages who alone can awaken mythological beasts. Actively suppresses warlocks.
- **The Silver Hand Guild.** Continent's most infamous thieves (based in the Zephyrian League); their leader announces targets before striking. Serena is mistaken for them, then ruled out.

## Warlock Lore (~ep 56–73, tease only)

- Once popular (~a century ago), warlocks fell from grace when two of the three strongest used forbidden curses to experiment on people — fusing humans with magical beasts, killing high-level masters. The God Realm hunted them; "warlock" became synonymous with "evil." Now near-extinct continent-wide; Saint Laurent's Warlock Division hasn't taken a student in years.
- Warlocks cast Curses via hand-signs (no chanting). Skills are combinatorial — a single "combination curse" may need 7–8 base curses as foundation. Blood curses come naturally to Serena. A trained one-star warlock can reportedly take three other-class opponents at once.

## Saint Laurent Academy

- Most prestigious academy in the empire; six divisions (Magus, Warlock, Priest, Swordsman/Knight/Archer combat tracks, Herbalist/Alchemist, etc.). Sky-high tuition + entrance-exam talent bar. Serena enrolls as an alchemist/herbalist on Adrian's orders, disguised, while secretly studying Warlock (and needing Archer training). Class Allocation Test (~ep 60) controversially mixes divisions into teams. Notable top students: Rian Kestrel, Larry Carter, Dan Delcruz (Magic), Dorian Douglas (Archer), Clyde Sterling (Herbalist), Corbin Shaw (Knight).

## Flavor Notes

- The original Serena's inherited memories are fragmentary — "darkness and an endless, suffocating fear" of the dungeon; she can't recall what happened to the Snow Spirit Fox (it was Lucien).
- Whispers persist that Serena isn't truly Lucifer's child (she looked too plain) — silenced only by Adrian. The face-restoration (ep 41) ends that doubt: she's the image of her beautiful parents.
- Running gag: Serena's thief reflexes ("old habits died hard"; fingers itch to pocket Griffin's wares). The Phoenix sniffs her like a dog; Lucien needles her ("You barely count as a girl, let alone a woman," ep 28).
