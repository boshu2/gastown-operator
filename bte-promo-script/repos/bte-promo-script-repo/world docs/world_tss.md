## Protagonist — Serena Vale
**Identity.** A 24th-century master thief, the "Thief Goddess," reborn into a 14-year-old noble girl of the same name. The body's original owner was a developmentally delayed "dud" (no Aura or Magic) bullied to death by her cousins. The new Serena keeps the "idiot" act as 100% performance while rebuilding power in secret — Underdog/Hidden-Identity, every vindication invisible.
**Combat.** Thief's craft, not brute force: disguise, infiltration, sleight of hand, photographic memory, cold patience. Real edge: Lucien's tutelage and freakish dual Aura/Magic advancement. Weakness: at the opening she's genuinely frail; every win is engineered.
**Power Source.** The Seven Stars Moon Seal — seven layers locking her power, placed at birth. Lucien breaks them one at a time for service. Layer 1 (ep 2): unlocks training BOTH Aura and Magic, unprecedented. Layer 2 (ep 41): restores her true beautiful face, lifts her ceiling. CANNOT skip layers, free Lucien casually, or drop the fool act. Only two of seven broken by ep 41.
**Voice.** Dry, larcenous, transactional — she narrates the world like a heist target, sizing marks and savoring petty revenge. Modern-thief sarcasm against a fantasy court. Warm only toward the few who were kind to the original Serena. Never earnest, never a stock hero.

**Voice Samples (verbatim — never paraphrase):**

2. "You've got a hell of an attitude for someone trying to make a deal." (Ep 2) — haggling with a demon
3. "Neither of them felt the two small coin purses being lifted from their belts and slipped into a certain little girl's sleeves the moment their backs were turned." (Ep 4) — thief's-eye narration
4. "Just thirty thousand demonic cores? Don't even sweat it. With my skills, I'll get you so stuffed with power you'll be sick of it!" (Ep 13) — cocky bravado
5. "If she scrubbed the grime off her own face, she'd be way prettier. If she wanted to look at something beautiful, she could just find a mirror." (Ep 59) — vain, deadpan

**Drift tripwires.** becomes a naive/slow learner | wins through brute strength | drops the "idiot" act publicly before the show does | gets her vindication acknowledged openly | turns earnest-heroic | Lucien named/explained early.

## Setting — Entry-Window World
**Feel.** The Ardunian Empire on the Brilliance Continent — Aura, Magic, and bonded mythological beasts. Serena lives inside House Phoenix, one of the Five Great Houses: opulent, beautiful, rotten with internal knife-fighting over inheritance. She occupies its lowest rung — fed scraps, dressed by sneering maids, barred from the banquet. Power is the only currency, beauty the only social capital; she is publicly bankrupt in both.

**Sensory Samples (verbatim):**
1. "A boy and a girl, maybe fourteen or fifteen, stood beside her bed with their arms crossed... they both shot her the same look of pure contempt, as if they were looking at a piece of garbage." (Ep 1)
2. "Calling it 'helping her dress' was generous. This was more like a daily dose of torture." (Ep 4)
3. "She was a plain sparrow in a flock of peacocks." (Ep 19)

**Drift tripwires.** modern/sci-fi tech in the world | House Phoenix portrayed as warm | Serena openly wealthy/respected | the Five Houses depicted as allies rather than rivals.

## Tone Constraint
Dark vindication played as wry pleasure, never grimdark. The abuse is real and lethal, but Serena's POV reframes it as a long con she's quietly winning. A pure-tragic-victim promo — without the thief's relish and the comedy of the Phoenix and the demon — loses the show's texture.

# LAYER 1 — ACTIVATION CANON  ·  *the opening anchor — load at W1 + canon gate*

## First 5 minutes  *(the 50%-drop gate)*
Serena wakes scarred and half-dead to two beautiful cousins (Clara and Caleb) at her bedside calling her "trash," "the disgrace of House Phoenix," and threatening her into silence. They tricked her into a beast dungeon where she was mauled and left for dead. As they sneer, the dead girl's memories flood in: the body's owner died, and a 24th-century master thief now wears her face — playing the fool, plotting her hand.

## First 5 episodes  *(the first sitting)*
Rebirth and inherited memories (ep 1). Lucien — a demon bound inside her by Soul Contract — offers to break her seal for his freedom; he cannot kill her without destroying himself (ep 2). First seal breaks: she can now train both Aura AND Magic, unprecedented (ep 2–3). Five days of his training vaults her past ranks that took her cousins years (ep 4). "Punishment Day": hauled before family head Adrian Vale over the trespass — and she picks the maids' purses on the way in (ep 4–5).

## First 20 episodes  *(texture band)*
The hearing exposes the dead Snow Spirit Fox (Lucien "crushed it") and the house's lost shot at the Celestial Phoenix (eps 5–8). Adopted brother Silas Vale returns with a replacement fox and pardons her (eps 9–11). Clara tries to burn Serena's face; Serena trips her into her own fire — Clara loses her hair, never tells (eps 10–11, 17). Needing 30,000 demonic cores to break seal two, Serena robs House Phoenix, then Houses Dragon/Tiger/Tortoise, fencing the loot through House Griffin's auction house (meeting heir Rian Kestrel) (eps 12–18). The Sage arrives to awaken the Phoenix; Adrian shockingly names Serena a candidate and the family departs for Lava Valley (eps 18–20).

## HARD BLOCKS  *(opening-binding)*
1. **Serena's "idiot" act is 100% performance — she is a fully competent reincarnated master thief.** Naive/slow-learner framing destroys the dramatic-irony engine.
2. **The original Serena was a real "dud" who died at thirteen from injuries inflicted by Clara and Caleb.** Load-bearing tragedy; don't soften the cause.
3. **Lucien is a demon bound by Soul Contract; he cannot kill her (would cost nine-tenths of his soul).** His name, origin, and reason for being inside her are UNRESOLVED — do not invent.
4. **The Seal has seven layers; only the first two are broken by ep 41.** Don't break more unless source does.
5. **Serena trains BOTH Aura AND Magic — unprecedented in centuries.** Don't understate it or collapse to one path.
6. **The Celestial Phoenix chose Serena, rejecting Alaric, Clara, Caleb.** It manifests as an arrogant 3–4-yr-old boy — not docile, not a giant bird.
7. **At Saint Laurent Academy she enrolls disguised, registered as an alchemist/herbalist.** Don't break cover unless selling the exposure threat.
8. **Serena secretly pursues the Warlock path — despised, near-extinct, hunted by the God Realm.** Her most dangerous secret; don't expose unless that's the arc.

## Engines the opening supports
1. **Underdog Awakening / Hidden Identity (UNTESTED, STRONGEST):** the "disgrace" is secretly a master thief rebuilding power; every reversal stays invisible.
2. **Injustice Engine (UNTESTED):** systematic abuse — starved, dressed by sneering maids, nearly burned, scapegoated; dense and personal.
3. **Secret Identity (UNTESTED):** nested secrets — reincarnated soul, dual training, the demon inside, later Warlock path and male disguise.
4. **Bond / Companion comedy (UNTESTED):** the smug demon Lucien and the arrogant child-Phoenix as bickering foils.

## IM sources — opening window
- **Bedside threat** (Ep 1): scarred near-death girl threatened by the cousins who maimed her. *Injustice, T1*
- **The dressing "torture"** (Ep 4): maids strip and yank her like a scullery rat. *Injustice, T2*
- **Scapegoated for the fox** (Ep 8): the whole house turns to glare, ready to tear her apart. *Injustice, T2*

**Injustice density** *(primary conversion driver):*
- **T1 (mandatory):** an abused, scarred orphan girl, written off as a worthless idiot by her own noble family, secretly holds the power to bury them all.
- **T2 (supporting):** the daily cruelty — starved, dressed like trash, barred from the family table, scapegoated for a disaster she didn't cause.
- **T3 (deepening):** her vindication must stay secret; she can never be seen winning, which sharpens every quiet victory.
- **Drift risk:** skip the injustice layer and lead with heist-comedy or the Phoenix, and the promo loses the empathy nerve that drives completion and CTI.

# LAYER 2 — RAILS (eps ~20–100)

## 2a · Conversion Threads
1. **The seven-layer seal** — only two of seven broken by ep 41; pays off across the series. The locked power and the demon's hidden agenda are the spine — initiate, never resolve.
2. **Why is Lucien inside her?** — he refuses to say until all seven seals break (ep 2). A genuine open loop; tease, never answer.
3. **The Celestial Phoenix chooses the "disgrace"** — awakens ~ep 27–29 and picks Serena over the family's prodigies. Strong single-beat reversal; the bond's payoff lives in the app.
4. **The Warlock secret at Saint Laurent** — Serena secretly studies the forbidden, near-extinct Warlock path (~ep 56+) while Dean Aurelius Vance hunts the warlock he senses on campus (~ep 72–93). Ticking-clock exposure thread.
5. **Silas Vale is not what he seems** — the warm adopted brother is on a hidden agenda; his true nature stays unresolved. Tease the mystery, don't crack it.

## 2b · Canon Limits / Never-Happens
- **The show never lets Serena win in the open.** Her competence stays a secret performance — vindication is private, not public acclaim.
- **The show never resolves Lucien's origin or frees him** within the verified window.
- **The seal never fully breaks** — power comes in metered layers, never all at once early.
- **The Celestial Phoenix is never a docile, majestic mount** — it's a rude, ancient child who insults her.
- **Serena's parents' murder is never explained** (Lucifer Vale and Celeste Alarie killed when she was one month old; circumstances unknown — do not invent).
