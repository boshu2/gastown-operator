## World Mechanics

### Dragon bond system
- Everyone bonds a dragon at puberty: women in Allarion ~18, men earlier (Greyson at 13). Shifting young signals an exceptionally strong dragon.
- First shift is agonizing (bones stretch, scales/claws/horns form); afterward it's effortless — release a thought and the dragon takes over.
- The dragon lives in the human's mind; telepathy both ways; the human can see through the dragon's eyes. Dragons can lend strength, speed, sight, hearing, healing — each loan costs energy.
- Humans hear only their *own* dragon's side of any dragon-to-dragon conversation; another dragon's actual voice would knock a human unconscious (too deep/low-frequency).

### Ancient vs. modern dragons
- **Modern dragons:** created from Dragos Paradise essence, gifted at puberty as blank slates; develop abilities via training.
- **Ancient dragons:** arrive with memory, personality, and full magic intact; chose or were sent to bond a host. Lost abilities include: hiding from other dragons, private dragon-to-dragon channels, erasing/altering human memory through sleeping dragons, sensing a true mate at distance, multi-element breath, healing through the host, building with fire.
- **Hosento** = ancient term for a human host ("sent host"); in ancient times dragons controlled hosts — reversed in the modern era. Ebony is notably *not* a controllable Hosento.

### Mate bond
- Dragons mate once, forever; the bond persists across death and lifetimes. The human bond flows from the dragon bond. Both dragons AND both humans must accept it; a dragon cannot force it. Ebony's refusal blocks (not severs) the Nova–Radion / Ebony–Greyson bond. Artificial (non-true) mate bonds are inferior — "the dragons make the best of it."

### Dragon types
- **Stone (Nova):** rarest fire subtype; line discontinued by the dragon gods for its destructive power; melts stone to obsidian.
- **Silver (Radion):** fire+ice+water; only one alive; would die if gifted as a modern dragon (taught only one of three elements).
- **Others:** Gold/Red (common fire, Allarion royal line, Liam's Adonis), Ice/Blue (rare in Allarion, Bella's Laurel is light blue), Green/Earth (Kara's Mya), Gray (fast, weak in magic), Orange (weak fire).

## Geography
- **Allarion:** clifftop castle over Lake Halua (in a crater, one highway); city of Halua in the Valley of Denton by the River Gil Oris; primarily fire dragons; women's dragons typically half the size of men's; codified anti-women laws.
- **Piroria:** capital Burking; Lake Taconomi (Radion's hidden underwater cave); the Lily Training Center (women's military training, named for Queen Lillette); Fort Ludar (last outpost before the border, Lytle commands); the hidden cabin in the mountains.
- **The mountains / Shepherd's Trail:** between the kingdoms; features Childers Pass, an ancient dragon-magic rope bridge.
- **Dragos:** Idris's mountainous, resource-poor kingdom; economy = mohair + tourism to the ancient dragon caves of Soya (the first home of all dragons). Goliak's intended launchpad for conquest.
- **Green Isles** (Duro/King Ferric; naval trade), **Arkana** (Tye; Briette's secret birthplace), **Cypher** (Adrian; shipping ports), **Lari** (Kern), **Lison** (Beriod; Allarion's closest ally, treats women worse than Allarion).
- **Dragos Paradise:** the dragons' afterlife; rebels who craved power were forbidden to return but came anyway; Radion volunteered to hunt them; leaving causes memory haziness (Nova can't recall if she chose to come or was sent).

## The Prophecy (verbatim parameters, Eps 1 + 39)
Spoken publicly by the seer Auraborn at Ebony's birth: she will be "powerful and brave" and "the first female ruler of Allarion." Told privately to Queen Briette later: (1) she will rule over **two nations that join and become one**; (2) her reign will **not begin in Allarion**; (3) the **realm of man will be under attack** as she ascends; (4) her journey to destiny will be hard. King Liam forbade the prophecy from being spoken. Auraborn also prophesied over Briette as an infant — that she'd mother a great queen.

## Flavor notes
- The ball is the injustice engine made spectacle: each prince is a flavor of patriarchal entitlement (Beriod predatory, Idris incompetent, Tye well-meaning-but-complicit, Duro honest-but-dismissive, Adrian indifferent-drunk). Greyson is the lone equal — and she rejects him.
- Nova is on Ebony's *destiny's* side, not Ebony's side: she scolds, withdraws, withholds help when Ebony is cruel. The "I chose you / you are not yet worthy of it" tension is the internal engine.
- The domestic-incompetence material (fire, fish, water, skinning) is comedy and tragedy at once — privilege made visible by its absence.
- The gems sewn into Ebony's dress are her only material lifeline and her mother's love made tangible — a trackable resource.
- The rebel-dragon threat (Goliak; Sashine's suspicious dragon; "other like-minded dragons" not yet found) is the long-game that will eventually force the personal conflicts into alignment — still in setup at ch 100.
