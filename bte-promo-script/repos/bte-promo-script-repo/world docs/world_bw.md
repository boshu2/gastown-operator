# LAYER 0 — ESSENCE  ·  *the only default load*

## Three Pillars (locked)
1. **Nobody-to-Apex Awakening (109 mana, ~11x human average)**  *(Thruline)*
2. **Apocalypse-on-Campus (Arcadia, day one)**
3. **Blood Manipulation — the unique First Order skill**

## Protagonist — Ben Carter
**Identity.** A broke 20-year-old Arcadia State student working a repair-shop job so his foster sister Megan can study. When the Soul Record descends mid-rainstorm, he kills a giant bee on reflex and absorbs a First Order **Blood Manipulation** skill — "most people wouldn't see [it] in ten lifetimes" (Ep 14). The engine: the gap between what he can do and the family he can't reach. Underdog Awakening fused to Lone-Wolf survival.
**Combat.** Own body, a looted Breaker Blade, and blood — heating an enemy's blood to burst their eyes, forging blood spears and chains. Weaknesses are real: low Stamina/Health early, fast-draining mana, a Level-25 wall. He wins by reading the fight and exploiting terrain, not by overpowering.
**Power Source.** The **Soul Record** — a status-window system, no voice, pure mechanics. Kill → absorb Soul Power → stats / treasure orbs / skill scrolls. Five external active-skill slots; passives unlimited. Blood Manipulation costs mana per cast; it CANNOT control a living enemy's internal blood, and fire counters it.
**Voice.** Flat, dry, deadpan under pressure; guarded to the point of cold; gallows-humor masking grief. Keeps everyone at arm's length, especially Brooke. Privately all family. Calculating, never cruel for sport; kills without flinching when it's for his own.

**Voice Samples (verbatim):**
1. "This damn weather's gonna be the death of me…" (Ep 1) — deadpan, pre-apocalypse
2. "I'm not really looking to have my life essence sucked out of me." (Ep 2) — dry, to Lilith
3. "Survive. No matter what it takes. Crawl through fire if you have to. Just. Stay. Alive." (Ep 4) — internal vow
4. "Not until I find my family. I'm not dying to some mutated dog or overgrown insect before then." (Ep 15) — the engine, stated
5. "As long as it is for my sake and the sake of my loved ones, even if I have to kill hundreds of people, I can kill without remorse." (Ep 39) — cold justice, post-Kyle

**Drift tripwires.** Wins by raw overpowering | controls a living enemy's blood freely | family confirmed dead | Lilith fights for him or becomes a love interest | warm/chatty group-hero voice | evolves past Level 25 inside the opening window.

## Setting — Entry-Window World (Arcadia State, day one)
**Feel.** A prestigious Manhattan campus — "a city within a city" — turned overnight into a life-or-death gauntlet under a relentless apocalyptic storm. Glass towers, the dorms, the steel-doored gym fortress at the center. Outside: zombies (one scratch kills), man-sized bees, truck-sized animals, a fire-wreathed First Order beetle. Predators eat each other to evolve. Phones give a dead-line robotic voice. The world beyond campus is unknown — nobody has left the grounds.

**Sensory Samples (verbatim):**
1. "The sky cracked open like it was mourning the end of the world. Rain slammed against the glass, thunder roared overhead—and Ben Carter had no idea his life was about to end." (Ep 1)
2. "But no one stopped. No one even looked back. They were too busy running for their own lives. Within seconds, she went still." (Ep 1)
3. "The number you are trying to reach is either turned off or out of service. Please try again later." (Ep 10)

**Drift tripwires.** Off-campus world shown/explored | military or confirmed safe zones | zombies as slow comic filler rather than lethal | the storm as backdrop rather than pressure.

## Tone Constraint
Survival-horror underdog drama, not a flex. Ben's power is constantly undercut by lethal stakes (one scratch = death) and the family he can't reach. If a beat lets him win effortlessly with no cost and no injustice, the show has broken.

# LAYER 1 — ACTIVATION CANON  ·  *load at W1 + canon gate*

## First 5 minutes  *(the 50%-drop gate)*
A normal kid griping about the rain. An earthquake; a jolt through his gut. A green screen: **"The Soul Record has arrived on Planet Earth."** Outside, a sobbing teenage girl is run down — bitten, killed, ignored by a fleeing crowd — and rises as a zombie. A man-sized bee crashes the window; Ben beats it dead with a chair, absorbs a First Order **Blood Manipulation** scroll. A woman in the corner smiles: "My name is Lilith… a Sixth Order succubus."

## First 5 episodes  *(the first sitting)*
Ben fights down through the dorm and out into a campus horror show, cutting a path while reluctantly saving strangers (and punching out one he saw get scratched). He loots a **Breaker Blade**, beheads zombies by the dozen, and pushes for the **gym** shelter. He watches Leon throw a girl to a mantis to save himself. Lilith establishes the rules: kill, absorb, diminishing returns, predators everywhere.

## First 20 episodes  *(texture band)*
The gym fills with ~100 survivors. The other strong ones surface: **Brooke Snow** (ice, student-council president, untouchable), **Chase** (archer, military family, believes in law), **Logan** (hammer, brute). Ben claims the cafeteria as a private base and works alone. **Ep 10–11:** mom, sister, dad — all dead lines; he breaks down; Lilith reframes it (the mana wave fried the signal — they're probably alive). He awakens passives from who he is (**Stone Heart**, Ep 13). **Ep 18–20:** he barely survives his first death-fight, a fire-wreathed **First Order Blazing Beetle**.

## HARD BLOCKS  *(opening-binding)*
1. **Lilith CANNOT intervene in combat.** She watches, advises, collects loot; acting beyond commentary strips most of her power (Ep 16). Ben earns every victory himself — load-bearing.
2. **Lilith is invisible to all other humans** and is NOT a love interest through Ep 71 — advisor/teacher/strategist only. Flirting is texture; no consummation, no confession.
3. **Ben's family status is UNKNOWN, not dead.** He couldn't reach them by phone (Queens, across the city). The uncertainty IS the emotional engine — do not kill or confirm them.
4. **The Soul Record is NOT an AI companion** — no voice, no personality, no dialogue. Status windows and mechanics only.
5. **Ben is stuck at Level 25** in the opening window; he CANNOT reach First Order until he passes the brutal Blood Berserker job trial. Do not show him First Order / Blood Will-evolved inside Ep 1–71.
6. **Blood Manipulation is bounded.** He controls his own and dead/spilled blood freely; he can sense but NOT control a living enemy's internal blood; **fire is a hard counter**. Costs mana per cast.
7. **The world beyond campus is UNKNOWN.** No one has left Arcadia's grounds. No military rescue, no confirmed safe zones.
8. **Kyle Lambert is dead (Ep 39–40)** — Ben crippled him and threw him to the zombies. Public, canon. Do not resurrect.

## Engines the opening supports
1. **Underdog Awakening / Hidden Power (UNTESTED):** a nobody with 109 mana (~11x human) + a one-in-ten-lifetimes skill — wins look impossible from outside.
2. **Injustice Engine (UNTESTED):** apocalypse as injustice — strong prey on weak; a crowd lets a girl die; Kyle feeds people to monsters.
3. **Survival / Lone Wolf (UNTESTED):** Ben's default is solo; his walls (Stone Heart) are the engine, not a flaw.
4. **Revenge / Cold Justice (UNTESTED):** calculated punishment — the Kyle execution is the template.

## IM sources — opening window
- **The abandoned girl** (Ep 1): killed and ignored by a fleeing crowd, then rises. *Death of the Weak, T1*
- **The dead phone lines** (Ep 10): mom, sister, dad — all out of service; Ben breaks. *Family, T1*
- **Leon's human shield** (Ep 5): the golden boy throws a girl into a mantis to live. *Betrayal, T1*
- **Kyle's reign** (Ep 32+): feeds people to zombies; brutalizes the women's dorm. *Systemic Cruelty, T1*
- **The First Order beetle** (Ep 18–20): a Level-15 nobody forced into a fight that should kill him. *Underdog Peril, T2*

**Injustice density** *(primary conversion driver):*
- **T1 (mandatory):** the apocalypse rewards the cruel — the strong prey on the weak (a crowd lets a girl die; Leon and Kyle feed people to monsters) while a broke nobody fights toward a family he can't even confirm is alive.
- **T2 (supporting):** Ben is undervalued and alone — the kid who chose his sister's tuition over his own, walled off because trust hurt him before.
- **T3 (deepening):** every win costs — burning legs on the beetle, mana that runs dry, a Level-25 wall he can't climb without risking death.
- **Drift risk:** if a promo leads with the cool blood powers and skips the abandoned-girl injustice + the unreachable family, empathy collapses and it reads as a flex, not survival.

# LAYER 2 — RAILS (eps ~20–100)

## 2a · Conversion Threads
1. **Is Ben's family alive?** — the dead phone lines (Ep 10) open the loop; Megan Keyes and his parents are in Queens, across a ruined city. The uncertainty drives everything. Never deliver a reunion or a confirmation — the payoff lives in the app.
2. **The Blood Berserker trial** — at Level 25 (~Ep 62) Ben is offered 7 jobs (most get 3–4) and picks the unique **Blood Berserker**; the trial demands the near-impossible (kill a First Order enemy in a single strike). Initiate the all-or-nothing stakes; the evolution itself pays off ~Ep 93 — withhold it.
3. **What is Lilith really after?** — a Sixth Order succubus who "placed a gamble" on a nobody and has "her own crisis to manage" (Ep 11). Tease the mystery of her endgame; never reveal it in the opening window.
4. **Who else turns predator?** — Lilith's warning ("your death might not come from a monster… but from someone standing right next to you," Ep 12) seeds Leon, Kyle, and the human threat. Sell the dread, not the resolution.
5. **The female-dormitory rescue** — Megan Shaw begs for the survivors starving in the women's dorm, guarded by a blue-skinned anti-magic zombie (~Ep 42). Initiate the rescue stakes; withhold the outcome.

## 2b · Canon Limits / Never-Happens
- **The show never lets Lilith win a fight for Ben** — she is barred from combat; he earns it alone.
- **The show never confirms the family** (alive or dead) in the source window — the hope is the engine.
- **Blood Manipulation never freely puppeteers a living enemy's blood, and never beats fire** — it has hard, stated limits.
- **The Soul Record never speaks as a character** — it is an interface, not a companion.
- **No Brooke–Ben romance** through Ep 71; Brooke's romantic feelings are absent. Mutual wariness, not attraction.
