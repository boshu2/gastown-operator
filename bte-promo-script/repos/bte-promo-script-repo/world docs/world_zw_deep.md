## The Blessing System (Zompirewolf mechanics)

**Gene economy (one feeding act per gene):**
| Gene | Skill-point source | Constraint |
|---|---|---|
| Vampire | drink blood (rarer source = more points; monster blood counts) | sun nerfs all stats; dormant genes still react to UV |
| Zombie/Undead | eat flesh (rarer = more; monster flesh counts) | his rotten blood is poison to vampires — a weapon (triggers "The Coffin") |
| Werewolf | kill/defeat monsters | hardest points; the open "cover" gene |

**Balance rule.** All active species levels stay within 5 of each other or the genes go out of sync and fight for control — "torn apart and reassembled," potentially lethal (Ep 5). Forces open werewolf training by day, covert blood/flesh feeding by night.

**Deactivation.** One trait: painful but bearable. Two at once: ten-fold pain. All three: activates **Deceased Human** — real death (Ep 5). Vampire tells (pale skin, fangs, crimson eyes) persist even when deactivated and must be suppressed.

**Status plate (what others see).** Julian's hack shows only: Werewolf, current level, Affiliation: Werewolves, Grade F. The Mistress's all-seeing eye found nothing; the Academy scanner pod flashed a self-correcting anomaly (possible future breach).

**Grade ladder.** F → E → D → C → B → A → S → (unknown). Evolution is conditional, can fail into Devolution; ~5% of the population can evolve at all, and most never know without a costly oracle reading.

**Blessing colors (rarity).** Yellow (common) · Red/Blue (rare, faction-sought) · Black (rarest; Tristan's true color — extraction risk).

**Skills observed (entry window):**
- *Vampire:* Low-grade Perception (senses intentions; **Reflex** perk — adapts to enemy patterns), Blood Poison, Skill Absorption.
- *Werewolf:* Battle Tactics (perceived slow-motion dodging), **Enrage** (Director-flagged, among the five rarest), Pain Tolerance.
- *Zombie:* Corpse Parade (commands lower undead), Bleed Resistance, Poison Resistance.
- *Unique:* **Undead Rhapsody** — auto-activates when only zombie+vampire genes are on; stat boost + cold calm; ≤2 minutes or burnout (discovered Ep 11–12).
- *Title "Defiant":* +10% strength/defense vs. creatures 5+ levels above his highest gene, plus bonus EXP.

**Weapons.**
- *Wind Daggers* (Rare): azure short swords from the Elara/Felix kill; 1.5× vs. night creatures, 2× vs. werewolves; hemorrhage effect; locked passive/active gated on A-grade kills. Secret/inventory only.
- *Bone Whip* (Uncommon): Academy entrance-exam reward; used openly.

## Geography & economy

- **Deja** — capital of the Lycan Kingdom; Seraphina's seat of unshakable power (uncrowned queen).
- **Maddencreek** — the city housing Seraphina's mansion; built around a dormant volcano (gothic skyscrapers, volcanic materials), ringed by a deadly mutant-beast forest.
- **Seraphina's Mansion** — ten ivory defensive towers, monster-bone walls, gold throne, onyx columns, teal carpet.
- **Contingent** — the Academy city atop a mysteriously warm mountain; >1M residents, multi-faction trade hub funded by multiple werewolf kingdoms plus vampire interests; multi-gate security; even Seraphina is barred from entering.
- **The Academy** — elite Lycan military program; ~12 buildings (first-years access 7); S/A/B/C/D ranking from the entrance exam; Subjugation Quest at the three-month mark; absolute rule of the Director.
- **The Aurora Continent / Five Stars** — Lycania is one of five prosperous-but-constrained "stars": sea on three sides, night-creature swamp to the north blocking expansion.
- **Currency:** White → Red (×100) → Blue (×100) → Silver (×1,000 Blue) → Gold → Black (no known holder).
- **Treaty:** the three great lords' 50-year pact; each region hosts two embassies for the other factions; vampires may hunt a few werewolves every six months (humans gifted as consolation).

## Late-game arcs (sampled, deep — anchor = break)

- **Academy life & the arena killings (~ep 34–50):** Tristan kills Miles and, during a later in-Academy exam, three noble students; the Director covers for him, blaming the resistance, and confronts him ("She Knows," Ep 35). Bounty placed by noble families.
- **The King turn (~ep 57–65):** King Jonathan takes Tristan's guardianship from Seraphina; reveals his parents were founding resistance members and may be alive; sets the Guild-rank deal (reach Gold in two months) and the long goal of clearing the swamps. Tristan plays both sides.
- **The Guild (~ep 67+):** adventurer ranks Copper→Diamond, each with E–S subdivisions; only five Diamond adventurers exist.
- **The Voidborn reveal (~ep 96):** the "admins"/Syndicate are **The Voidborn**, who engineered the virus as a forced-evolution experiment (human abductions 1947–~2000s). Julian's faction wanted to help humanity peacefully. **Silas Kent** — the lone dissenter, presumed dead — lives inside Tristan, surfacing near death. Other admins would kill Tristan to kill Silas.
- **Ep 100:** Tristan openly activates all three genes and a wolf-summon ("Valhalla") in combat, exposing his tribrid nature to enemies.
