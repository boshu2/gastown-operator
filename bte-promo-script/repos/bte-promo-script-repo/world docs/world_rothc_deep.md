## World Mechanics

### Polity and society
- The state is **the Commonwealth** (the text occasionally renders it "the Federation" or "the Dominion" — treat as one polity). At war with **the Empire** / the **Ferrand Empire**; a war of annihilation triggered by Caius Valerius's assassination.
- **Rigid class ladder decides which planet you may live on.** **Planet Dominion** is the capital and the top rung — high officials, decorated commanders, and old families only. Others named: **Bastion** (logistics base), **Argent**, **Cyan** (a third-rate backwater; House Rhys's seat), **Arcion** (the spaceport in ep 1).
- Society sorts into **common families, military families, and elite families**. The **great houses** hold enough power to take first dibs on academy class placements and to run intelligence scoring.
- **Every child must enrol in a scout academy at six and complete ten years of mandatory schooling.** Scout academies are "nurseries for future soldiers"; they are **independent of the government and the military** and are protected by god-class pilots.
- **Inheritance law: only male relatives may inherit military honours and benefits.** Justified publicly on the grounds that no woman has ever piloted an Imperator — the recoil requires a physique the politicians declared women can't build — so investing in a daughter "wastes taxpayer money."
- Ten thousand years have passed since Alaina's Valora. Cosmetic correctives by injection mean there are essentially no unattractive people.

### Assessment and ranks
- **Newborn assessment pods** report **Physical Fitness** (S / A+ / A / B…), **Spiritual Power** (Tier One / Two / Three / Four), and **Potential** (S / A+ / A / B+ / B). Public devices only handle up to **tier three**; a tier-four newborn would normally die in the womb.
- **Public assessment centres** rank newborns F–S. F gets nothing. D gets two bottles of grade-four gene agent a year; C, grade three; B, grade two. **A** gets a choice: two bottles of grade one a year and independence, or unlimited supply and **lifetime military service**. **S** gets taken by the military, no questions.
- **Psionic power cannot be formally tested until age ten**, when it matures and branches into types.

### Gene stimulating agents
- Four market grades by purity: **grade four = 30%, three = 45%, two = 60%, one = 75%.** Higher purity means less bodily damage and more absorption. An average person might afford one bottle of the lowest grade in a lifetime; each grade up is **ten times** the price.
- **Premium grade** — reserved for the central military, ~**90%** purity, the research ceiling. Ten tubes are worth more than cities and travel under top-mecha escort. Delivered every six months to the heir of a fallen officer.
- The rarest SS-rank infants absorb about **seven** vials. **Alaina absorbed ten.**
- Impurities accumulate as **bulk** — a tougher body at the cost of flexibility and resilience. Little Four scrubs hers with code, so she stays lean.

### The Mindscape (Little Four's learning space)
- A circular hall ringed by sealed doors; only **Physical skills** has ever opened. Entry is gated by a pitch-black **psychological resilience** test — panic means ejection; Alaina scored **Triple S**.
- Three foundational series: **Offensive, Balanced, Defensive** — pick exactly one; practising more than one causes serious bodily damage. Defensive splits into **Endurance** and **Flexible**; the system assigned Alaina Flexible *because she is a girl* — the one entity besides her household that knows.
- Flexibility ladder: newborns are B; untrained adults decay to D–F; Offensive drops below D; Balanced/Endurance top out at C (rarely B); **Flexible reaches A and beyond.**
- **Missions carry deadlines, rewards, and punishments.** Reward formula seen (the only award announced on-page): 1 honour point per day remaining + 100 for a perfect assessment. Punishment menu: **200 honour points deducted**, **200 jolts of electric shock**, or **10 points = 30 days' extension** with the penalty doubled on failure.
- **Honour points** buy skills (Iron Skin at 1 pt; Eagle Claw / Iron Head / Golden Finger / Crippling Legs / Long Ape Arms / **Rabbit's Spring** at 10; a 50-pt tier gated behind stat minimums), courses, and games (50 → 10,000). Unspent points carry over — a fact the system never states and Number One refuses to confirm.
- **Time dilation:** instructors can stretch the training clock. One night has run as long as two subjective months; Alaina once took "three years of bitter suffering" in the days before a tournament.
- **Death inside is real-feeling and non-permanent** — except on an **Exclusive Mission**, where dying fails it forever.
- Prolonged brutality leaves a physical **malevolent aura** / leaking killing intent that will eventually damage her body and stunt her growth. Suppression is not a cure; mastery is.

### The Paths
- Named: **Path of Slaughter, Demonic Path, Ghoul Dao, Heartless Path, Sovereign Path, Sage Path, Path of Fellowship, Path of Benevolence, Heart's / Literary / Martial Dao.** "There are thousands of Paths."
- Grasping one makes all future learning explosively faster — ten years' work in two or three. **Every established Path demands you give up part of yourself**; even Fellowship walkers lose their families.
- Stalling at "the gates of the Path" without a purpose causes loss of control — bloodthirsty rage, a desire to destroy everything. The cure is a purpose, not a technique.
- Alaina refused all of them, forced the "mysterious voice" to admit it was the Mindscape system, answered "What is the Path?" with **"A person's true self,"** and named her own the **Path of Dominance** — free, not bound to any one way, holding the potential for every Path. The system shut down its legacy shortcut and stripped the Path insights back out of her head. **No child had ever done this.** She carries only "a trace of its essence": enough that a hint of it would leave a tenth-grader unable to fight at full strength.

### Talents
- **Profound Insight** (Alaina) — instantly spots an opponent's weakest point once she sees the move clearly. Number One calls it the most valuable combat talent there is and "the perfect natural skill for a mecha pilot"; even in Mandora's advanced society such children are "practically one of a kind." **She does not know it has a name.**
- **Perception / Animal Instinct** (Ronan) — what the Commonwealth calls the **sixth sense**. Forged deliberately by brutal training with an abysmal success rate; a "gift from the gods" here, a lower-tier talent by Mandora's scale.
- **"The Zone"** — a slow-motion perceptual state Number Five names and immediately refuses to believe in a six-year-old. She cannot summon it at will.

### Aether channelling exercises
- Taught by an old Valoran doctor in her first life; practised for over a decade with no result, then live from the moment of reincarnation — she felt Aether for the first time as a fetus.
- Effects: nulls pain in a meditative trance; **replenishes energy as fast as it is spent** and repairs minor damage (so she cannot be exhausted by any opponent weaker than her); **fully repairs a day's training damage overnight at no cost** to life force or cellular energy, which this era's medicine cannot do; massively boosts absorption of the tempering bath; flooding her limbs instantly doubles their strength.
- **Undetectable by this era's technology.** Only Little Four can see it, and only through the host bond. The household credits everything to Caius's genetics.

### The Valerius tempering bath (family secret)
- A hidden room, a waist-high wooden tub, inky green liquid: **96% plant matter, 4% snake, scorpion, centipede and spider venom.** You must stay conscious throughout; the middle thirty minutes are the worst, and most children pass out there. Caius went conscious-but-comatose for half a day.
- Improvement records: **Caius 10–50% across the board (the all-time family record). Alaina's first bath: 30–80%.**

### Mecha
- **Restricted goods**, banned from public sale. Families licensed to keep a private force may own only basic standard models, painted **dark gray**; **Commonwealth White** is standard issue; **blue and white** marks captain-class; ace pilots may paint theirs any colour.
- Pilot ladder: **high-level pilot → ace pilot → Imperator class → god class.** **Only twelve god-class Imperator operators exist in the entire Commonwealth**, each a national treasure and a deterrent.
- Startup times: standard activation 1–3 minutes with full diagnostics; emergency activation 12–15 seconds for standard issue, 10–12 for captain class, ~2–3 seconds faster per class above; **Imperator class starts in true zero seconds.**
- Built of **super-titanium alloys**; height is never stated in-source (the largest Nexus display models run to three metres). Beam sabers, particle beam guns, lightspeed guns, sniper rifles, beam shields, detonation cables, plasma cannons (huge splash, pitiful range, kept because they're cheap), and **Super Homing Projectiles — "the Newbie Killer."** Inertial dampeners deliberately do not fully cancel G-forces, so the pilot can feel the machine — which is why peak physical fitness is required.
- Evasion techniques, ranked: **Freeform Evasion** (the military's most advanced) > **Flickerstep** (a certified master's move) > **Irregular Flash Evasion**.
- **AIs are self-contained; any hacking attempt triggers an automatic shutdown to manual.** Controlling one is considered impossible — which is why Little Four's feat gets laundered as "a House Valerius secret weapon."

### The Central Scout Academy of Planet Dominion
- Nearly **ten thousand students enrol each year**; ten grades, ten years. Enrolment exams: **intelligence, strength, stamina, speed** — no score can be challenged until all four are done, and **any complaint voids your results for the entire year**.
- Strength test: **550 pounds = a perfect 100.** Alaina's was the first perfect score in three years. Intelligence: a three-to-five-minute chat; most six-year-olds score in the nineties; Alaina scored **80**, filed as "Calm and collected, no sign of creative thinking, lacking imagination."
- Speed/stamina: run inside a virtual-reality complex disguised as a parade ground — AOE illusion tech, real rain from ceiling sprinklers, a single ~165-foot-wide closed loop with no real finish line. Groups of **ten** by registration number, because ten is a squad; the hidden objective is squad cohesion. It normally runs three to four hours. Alaina's group finished in **under two — an academy first.**
- **Only the top 100 make the special classes**; top 50 → **Special Class A**, 51–100 → **Special Class B**, then Merit Classes (2,000 students), then regular. Uniform colours: **Special A scarlet red with gold, Special B white, Merit blue, regular green**; lower colours step aside for red. **Everyone is re-ranked every six months**, no exemptions.
- **Special-class students (the top 100 — both A and B)** choose their own courses and may live at home; Class A boarders get a free villa with VR pods. Day-studenting is considered "the most useless and pretentious perk imaginable."
- **Seat challenges:** anyone may challenge a seat's occupant. Win and you swap; lose and you're the winner's gofer for a month; refuse and you forfeit your place in the class. Arena formats: Closed, Private, Small, Semi Open, **Open Arena** (broadcast academy-wide, normally reserved for tournaments).
- **Military force is forbidden on academy grounds.** No student has ever been harmed on campus. Academy footage cannot leave the grounds — it wipes itself off any communicator the moment you step off campus.
- The secret **Initiate Program**: one-on-one mentorship, recruits identified at the first ranking tournament. Tiers — **initiate disciple** (all benefits, no responsibilities) < official disciple < **true disciple** (bound for life, "almost like father and son").

### The Commonwealth Nexus (the virtual world)
- "Humanity's second world," federally regulated with an iron fist after incidents where Nexus anarchy spilled into reality. **Login key is a person's unique, unchangeable brainwaves — one person, one ID, one avatar, no alts.** Only people whose minds have mutated a specific way can mask an identity; **Virtual Spectres** can damage brainwaves — murder inside the Nexus. Time runs 1:1.
- Under-sixteens get restricted access; children not yet in a scout academy cannot enter at all. Academy students are confined to the academy's sealed virtual campus — **a city about a hundred times the size of the real one**. Nexus purchases ship the real item; all sales final. The academy blocks credit conversion and gives students nothing, forcing them onto missions.
- **Mecha Street** sells functioning models built from the same alloy types as real mecha; a three-metre one costs a self-funded student seven or eight years of savings.
- **Legacy missions** can only be created by an **Imperator-class pilot or higher**, to choose a successor — which is the contradiction ep 100 leaves hanging about Caius.

## Flavor notes
- **The disguise is the wound, and the mother is the one who made it.** There is no villain to punish for it. Seraphina apologises to her daughter at birth and enforces it anyway, because the alternative is letting the men who circled Caius's corpse take everything. Every promo's empathy lives in that impossible position.
- **Excellence is the danger, not the reward.** Ten vials gets her file buried. A record bath result gets her diagnosed as defective. Second place gets deleted. A smile gets her warned. She learns before she can walk that the nail that sticks out gets hammered down — and the show never lets her forget it.
- **She is not building an empire; she is buying a private life.** Her stated goal, arrived at in ep 73 and named in ep 94, is freedom, no chains, and a child she can raise openly. Everything she does is instrumental to that. She refuses followers, forfeits titles, and turns down a village on its knees.
- **The comedy is Little Four and it must never lead.** A naked toddler AI who tried to make her call him "Master Four" because "Master" was popular in Earth media (ep 6), then read *Le Morte d'Arthur* and started calling her "Lord" (ep 16), throws jealous tantrums with a kitchen knife, and routes a baby's toxins into her bladder at the worst possible moment. Release valve, not engine.
- **The world is stratified all the way down.** Which planet you live on, which colour you wear, whether your father was a retainer — every layer has a door someone else holds shut. Florian's treason is manufactured by one of those doors, not by malice.
- **Money and stakes are concrete.** Vials worth more than cities, ten-times-per-grade agent pricing, 550 pounds for a perfect score, 240 credits for six meal sets, 500 virtual dollars for a broken window, six vials a year as the price of a man's loyalty.
- **Two men run the machinery above her head and she never meets either.** A general who buried her file for a dead friend, and a lieutenant general who sat on her inheritance for six years. Both think they are protecting her. Neither asks.

## Source ambiguities & translation flags
*(The source is a machine-assisted translation with real internal drift. These are unresolved in-source — do not settle them in a build.)*

- **The pronoun drift.** Most of the script says "he" for Alaina. But the Superintendent's file (ep 26) reads "the daughter of Major General Caius Valerius," and the Dean and Lt. Gen. Scott say "his daughter" and "the girl" repeatedly (eps 85–86) — with no in-story reaction from anyone. Ep 64's Empire handler goes the other way ("the boy… his son"). Treat as translation drift, never as an in-world reveal.
- **The mother's name.** **Seraphina Laine** throughout (19 occurrences), corroborated by ep 1's etymology — "'Alaina', it's close to your name, 'Laine.'" But ep 100's legacy form has Alaina write "Seraphina Alaina." Use **Laine**.
- **Her first-life age.** Ep 1 is explicit: "for twenty-four long years, she'd been a burden." Ep 21 says "her mature mind, honed over thirty years of life," and ep 68 has her tallying two lifetimes as "an old lady of over thirty." Use **24 at death**; the thirties references are two-lifetime totals.
- **Her first world.** Ep 1 names it **Valora**; eps 6 and 15 call it **Earth**. Same place.
- **Does the Dean know she's a girl?** Ep 86: "Caius left me a message. That item is for his daughter." Caius deployed before the birth and could not have known the sex. The source never explains it. **Do not resolve this in either direction.**
- **Aether vs Qi; honour points vs battle points.** The text uses "Aether" (eps 62–63) and "Qi" (ep 66) for the same internal energy, and "honour points" (Mindscape) alongside "battle points" (academy/Nexus). Pick one of each per build; the source does not distinguish them consistently.
- **Commonwealth / Federation / Dominion.** Three surface names for one polity. Use **the Commonwealth**.
- **Ep 35 and 36 are truncated in the export** — Ep 35 runs ~8 lines and ends mid-scene; Ep 36 opens mid-dialogue. Lars's condition and its fallout are partially missing.
