## World Mechanics

### Beast contract & mana poisoning
- Children accumulate toxic mana; by age 10 the **Ceremony of Ten Years** contracts a beast that absorbs it, curing the poisoning. Contract is permanent, for life; the beast fuses with the body (scales, claws, markings — or, for Ren, hair-mushrooms).
- Eggs are purchased; **color sets the outcome band.** Gray (cheapest) yields: Common Plant 94.99% / Marsh Frog 5% / **Spore 0.01%**. Black (most expensive) can yield Gold potential.
- Beasts grant **percentage attribute increases** that scale with evolution. Iron Crimson Salamander: +40% str, +20% all, fire. Common Plant: +20% vitality, +10% all (up to 30–40% cultivated). **Spore: +10% physical strength, period — the only beast granting no base power.**

### Ranking
- **Iron → Bronze (1–2) → Silver (1–3) → Gold → Platinum.**
- **Premature maturation:** an Iron beast accumulating ~1,000 development points *undirected* matures permanently and can't evolve (this is what happened to Ren's parents' plants).
- **Correct cultivation:** exactly 100 directed points (right crystal/timing/sealing) per rank. All real methods are 100 days; "150-day" methods waste 50 days miscalibrating.
- **Caste maps to rank:** Iron = labor/poverty; Bronze = working class; Silver = privilege; Gold = elite (5 city families); Platinum = the King only. Rank gates jobs, districts, business (Bronze min for city commerce).

### Ren's knowledge system
- After completing the ancient three-ingredient medicine (male plant + female plant + host born with the first half), Ren gained encyclopedic beast biology/ecology/cultivation/weakness knowledge — **clear up to Silver 3**; Gold+ and abyssals are a total void.
- Manifests as: seeing cultivation defects, correct evolution paths (contradicting records), invisible mana patterns, predator/prey ecology, behavioral signals.
- Carried through the hair-mushrooms; "always on" while fused. Expelling the spore would lose the stream — why he refuses to hide it. Mushrooms remain **cosmetic/"dormant" through ch 100** (ep 77, ep 90). Ren *speculates* evolving the spore may raise the ceiling — unconfirmed.

### The 1,000-day method (parents)
- "Mature" plants are an intermediate cocoon. True maturation = 1,000 specific development points, **one per day with zero failures** (one miss resets to zero), via a 3-part daily ritual (dawn crystal + vital essence; 24 min midday sun; sunset seal with Night-Flower pollen), then a final-day **Vitality Rune** (~10,000 crystals each). Result: Bronze-2-level power, vitality 40%→100%, decades more life — would cure the father. Nobody discovers it because everyone quits before results show.

### Ren's spore cultivation (ch 100 state)
- Spore→Bronze: 100 days absorbing specially processed Bronze crystals (discarded Bronze tissue + moss + crushed poisonous fungi + deep-earth seal), final day = the golden ancestral fungus. Each session routes lethal mana via a modified Shadow Stalker tentacle — daily ordeal, not montage. ~12/100 days done.
- **Intelligence Rune** charging over 10 days (enhances spore info-processing on the final day).
- **Incorrect-maturation tracker:** ~82/1,000 points from deep-mine mana exposure (margin remaining, can't be reckless).
- **Mirror-Mantis core:** suspended in the 300m geyser, needs ~100,000 mana to revive → planned second beast.
- **Diamond-Hydra egg** + **Hunter-Bug egg:** both stolen from the vein chamber. (The egg is identified as a *diamond* hydra, ep 90/92 — rarer than gold.)
- **Second beasts** are real but rare (only the King + ~12 legendary warriors). Reaching Bronze with the spore opens the possibility — the source of Ren's plan.

### Academy structure & economy
- **5 teaching units:** (1) Basic Knowledge [history/economics/life]; (2) Cultivation Theory [Wei]; (3) Exterior Gathering [Zhao expeditions]; (4) Practical Crystals [Song mine work]; (5) Combat [Yang + Lin].
- **Support ladder:** pass all 5 → Iron→Bronze support (1 → 10 crystals/day). Fail 3 units = −1 point; −3 points = expelled from support. Silver support = 100/day.
- **Gathering duplication:** the Academy matches the value of everything a student gathers/processes themselves (gov subsidy). Ren's primary wealth engine. Belongings are logged at intake so only self-earned gains count.
- **Gray Wing:** low-potential dorm; room 15 = Ren, Taro, Min, Liu — sited near the East Cave mines (poor students mine more). Iron dining hall is deliberately bland as a rank-up incentive.
- **Practice mines:** 4 cave systems under the Academy. Safe to 100m; guarded to 150m; dangerous past 200m (greater/fused worms); Bronze ecosystem past 275m (Living Tunnels, Deep Assassins); 300m = mana vein, geyser, crystal chamber, hydra lair.

### Broader world & geopolitics
- **Yano** (Ren's kingdom): one Platinum tamer (the King, a double tamer, currently absent), 5 Gold families. City sits in a mana-poor zone that repels strong beasts — humanity's only natural defense.
- **Yino** (rival, across the dead abyss): developed **abyssal beast fusion** — tamers bear two beasts (one normal + one abyssal), with **triple-tamer tech "almost ready."** Grotesque but multiplies power and out-produces Gold-rank tamers; Yano considers it an abomination. Runs active espionage.
- **The Abyss:** two perpendicular chasms split the world into four quarters. **Dead abyss** (dry, between Yano/Yino); **living abyss** (a river of pure mana where eggs originate, dragon-guarded). 90% of each quarter belongs to dragons; humans hold a despised mana-poor circle.
- **Dragons:** apex "mother" of each evolutionary line. Decay (Ren's line: fungi/spores/decomposition), Red (fire/reptiles), Blue (marine), Green (forest/plants), White (sky), Black (night/shadow), Articulated (insects/exoskeletons). Mostly theoretical to common people.
- **Depth = danger:** every ~50m roughly doubles mana. 100m ≈ iron-ring; 200m greater/fused worms; 275m Bronze beasts; 300m+ gold-tier hydra territory; 1,000m+ silver hydras; deeper still toward diamond hydras.
- **Beast-core cycle:** Deep Assassins collect prey cores; as they ascend/evolve they discard cores into the depths, which "rebirth" as eggs ascending through mana zones — most become Living Tunnels/Deep Assassins; a rare few reach the surface veins unevolved and become the eggs the kingdom sells.

### Mechanics governing Pokémon-NO and fusion-YES (faithfulness anchors)
- Humans fight using their **own fused beast(s)**; beasts are not sent out to duel each other. The "battles" are tamer-vs-tamer / tamer-vs-wild-beast.
- The spore is **fused into Ren's body** (hair-mushrooms), not a detachable companion. It has no name, no agency, and no personality — it cannot act as a "stakes character" or do anything on its own. Any "Cloud" doing things in a script is a fabrication.
- The Yino "fusion" is a *human* tamer absorbing/merging additional beasts (incl. corpses) into a grotesque double/triple-tamer hybrid — a horror of human ambition, not a creature evolution. Adrian's ch 66–67 "complete fusion" is the canonical example.

## Flavor notes
- **The ceremony is the injustice engine** — egg color is purchased economic sorting; the laughter is a rigged system working as designed.
- **Cooking metaphors are load-bearing** — the father explains cultivation as bread fermentation; food-as-love runs through every emotional beat (the uneaten feast, the bland cafeteria, Lin force-feeding Ren).
- **Mushrooms = asset and stigma at once** — they carry the knowledge, light the dark, mimic glows to save him; they also make him "Rotting Boy." Every benefit is packaged with social punishment.
- **Knowledge-vs-proof is the unique engine** — Ren *knows* but can't prove without sounding insane, so every win requires an indirect demonstration. An information-asymmetry thriller in cultivation-academy clothing, not a power fantasy.
- **The economy is explicit and calculable** — the show tracks crystal sums precisely (house = ~500k; spore evolution ~50k; rune ~10k each; ~105k earned in two months; "ten million" for a city house). The math creates urgency.
- **The mines are a vertical open world** — every ~50m is a new biome; the show escalates by descending. 0–300m mapped by ch 100; the deepest hydra tiers are the long-term ceiling.
- **Lin is the tonal thermostat** — comedy/discipline resets after each dark spike.
