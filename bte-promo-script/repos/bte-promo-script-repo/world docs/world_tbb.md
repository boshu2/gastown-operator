# LAYER 0 — ESSENCE  ·  *the only default load*

## Three Pillars (locked)
1. **Secret Bodyguard / Identity** *(character stakes)* **(Thruline)** — "everyone has had to hide who they really are to fit in where they don't belong."
2. **Jade Pendant / Dragon Mastery** *(fantasy element)*
3. **Elite School / Wealth Gap** *(setting)*

## Protagonist — Brian Woods
**Identity.** Chinatown orphan, ~18, raised since age three by an old martial-arts master, **Samuel Lin**, on odd jobs (weaving straw slippers that sell for two dollars) and near-fatal "missions" Lin sends him on for a hundred or two hundred bucks. No parents — the thing he's "longed for his entire life" is a mother's embrace, a father's hand, things he's "only dreamed of but never truly known." Hired by billionaire **Robert Hart** as the **secret bodyguard** to Hart's daughter **Stella** — $30k/month, school fees and villa covered — under cover as her "study buddy." Nobody can know he's protecting her. Engine: **Hidden Identity / Secret Protector** — a battle-hardened nobody passing as a charity-case classmate inside a school full of heirs.
**Combat.** Elite, controlled, never showy. Disarms by precision, not force — a "subtle, no one saw it happen" ankle-tap that makes a bully stumble rather than fall. Reads a room before he reads a fight. Battle-tested in real warzones (refugee-camp rescue, Ep 1), so schoolyard thugs and entitled rich boys barely register as threats — a gap he finds almost absurd.
**Power Source.** Two linked artifacts, both earned at age eight in **The Dragon Cave of Mystics** (full-moon-only, "chosen one" prophecy): the **Mystic Jade Pendant** and the **Dragon Mastery Arts** scroll. The pendant houses the stored mind of an ancestor-master, **Eli Grayson**, and warns Brian of any nearby ill intent (a private pulse only he feels), remembers past threats, and hints at hidden opportunity — the ability that earned him the codename **"The Hawk."** The Dragon Mastery Arts is a leveled cultivation system reinforcing body, reflexes and energy channels; Brian is stuck at the late first level because the ordinary world is too energy-poor to break through.
**Voice.** Calm, dry, unbothered. Deflects status insults instead of escalating; quietly amused by people who think wealth equals power. Self-aware about being the poor outsider but refuses to perform gratitude or beg approval.

**Voice Samples (verbatim — never paraphrase):**
1. *"If you're expecting me to beg for your approval, you'll be waiting a long time."* (Ep 3) — refuses to perform for the rich girl
2. *"Not a basement. Chinatown."* (Ep 3) — flat, unashamed of where he's from
3. *"My mistake. You see I seem to lose control of my legs when I see a good for nothing brat harassing a pretty girl."* (Ep 3) — dry cover for a deliberate move
4. *"I am no fool, and I am certainly not rich…"* (Ep 2) — self-placement, no self-pity
5. *"Two hundred dollars? After risking my life? That's all?"* (Ep 1) — the underpaid, overexposed life with Lin

**Drift tripwires.** Brian openly flexing wealth or power | hero-confident swagger / one-liner bravado | begging or earning Stella's approval | revealing he's the bodyguard | a chosen-one who arrives already maxed (he's stuck at level one) | the pendant treated as a generic magic gadget rather than Eli's living presence | warzone past erased so the school is his first real danger.

## Setting — Entry-Window World
**Feel.** **Aspen Hills**, "where all the rich people live." **Maple Grove Academy** — pristine gates, crisp uniforms, heirs who "barely glanced his way." Brian arrives in a wrinkled hoodie, faded jeans and scuffed sneakers; the contrast is the whole point. He lives in **Stella Hart's villa** (chandeliers, "expensive perfume and polished wood"), nominally her study buddy, sleeping in the guest room. Status is worn and visible: designer watches, who-gets-tossed-the-leftover-ribs (the dog, Phantom, got downgraded when Brian arrived), and the casual cruelty of guards and classmates toward anyone who looks poor.

**Sensory / Status Samples (verbatim):**
1. *"Maybe he's here to beg for leftovers. Look at him—ragged hoodie, cheap sneakers, looks like he hasn't had a proper meal in days."* (Ep 2 — Hart Towers guards)
2. *"This… is the bodyguard my father sent?"* / *"You look like you just crawled out of a basement."* (Ep 3 — Stella)
3. *"The towering skyscrapers and sleek modern architecture stood before him, like something out of a sci-fi comic book."* (Ep 2 — Hart Towers)

**Drift tripwires.** Maple Grove framed as warm/welcoming | the wealth gap softened or unstated | Brian comfortable and belonging on arrival | the villa as a reward rather than a cover post | classmates as peers rather than people who look down on him.

## Tone Constraint
Grounded fish-out-of-water with dry comedy over a real-stakes spine — a warzone veteran undercover among soft, cruel rich kids, sitting on power he can't fully use yet. Never wish-fulfillment flex. The nerve is "the outsider everyone underestimates, hiding what he really is," not "secretly-rich badass humiliates the school for fun." If a beat has Brian enjoying dominance or showing off, the tone has slipped.

# LAYER 1 — ACTIVATION CANON  ·  *the opening anchor*

## First 5 minutes  *(the 50%-drop gate)*
North Sahara refugee camp under missile fire. Brian is mid-mission, hauling civilians out of the blast radius — *"Move now!"* The jade pendant, which has "always been there… guiding him," **flickers and surges like never before**, a warning he's never felt. He runs back into the flames for a trapped boy whose legs don't work, shields the child through an explosion, and revives him with CPR while the mother begs. Watching her hold her son makes Brian's eyes moist — a mother's embrace is the exact thing he's "never truly known." The opening establishes everything in one breath: the man is a battle-hardened protector, the pendant is alive and escalating, and the emotional wound is an orphan who saves other people's families.

## First 5 episodes  *(the first sitting)*
Brian returns to his Chinatown home and master **Samuel Lin**, who pays him two hundred dollars for nearly dying and reveals "the big mission": move to **Aspen Hills**, meet billionaire **Robert Hart**, no backing out (Ep 1). On the train he out-cons two scammers and befriends **Luke Hartley** (Ep 2). Hart hires him as **Stella's secret bodyguard** — cover story "study buddy," she can't know (Ep 2). At **Maple Grove Academy**, Stella rejects him on sight as beneath her standards; Brian quietly drops the campus heir **Peter Kingston** with one invisible move and refuses to beg for her approval (Ep 3). Ep 4 ("Kisses and Caves") flashes back to the **origin at age eight**: Lin knocks him out on **Starlight Peak** during the Mid-Autumn full moon, the **Dragon Cave of Mystics** opens to "the chosen one," and Brian takes the **Dragon Mastery scroll and jade pendant** from the altar, escaping before sunrise (or be trapped five years). Established here: Brian + the pendant, the secret-bodyguard cover, Maple Grove's wealth gap, Stella/Harper/Peter, and the cave/chosen-one backstory.

## First 20 episodes  *(texture band)*
Brian settles into the villa as study buddy and quietly outclasses everyone — fights (the bank attack, the kidnapping of Stella Hart, Eps 8–10) and academics alike (he re-solves Stella's exam more elegantly than the teacher, Ep 51 territory begins to seed earlier). The pendant's threat-sense keeps saving him and his friends; he's decoded **three distinct alerts** so far and still doesn't grasp its full power. His old comrade **Cody Marlow**, now a police captain, re-enters as an ally on the city's criminal side. The villa dog **Phantom**, Harper's needling, and Stella's slow thaw fill the texture. NOT yet entered: the pendant spirit **Eli Grayson** revealing himself, and the cultivation breakthrough — both land ~Ep 51–53.

## HARD BLOCKS  *(opening-binding)*
1. **Brian is undercover — nobody at school knows he's a bodyguard.** Robert's explicit terms: cover is "study buddy," "no one can know you're there to protect her" (Ep 2). A promo that outs the role to classmates breaks the core engine.
2. **Brian is dirt-poor and unconnected, not secretly rich.** Chinatown orphan, raised on two-hundred-dollar missions, wrinkled hoodie and scuffed sneakers (Ep 1–3). His power is *hidden*, his wallet is *empty*. Never write him flashing money or status.
3. **The pendant is a person, not a gadget.** It contains the stored mind of ancestor-master **Eli Grayson** (revealed Ep 51); its threat-sense ("ill intent → private pulse," memory of past attackers, opportunity hints) is exclusive to Brian and earned him the name **"The Hawk."** Don't reduce it to a glowing trinket or invent powers it doesn't have.
4. **Both artifacts were earned at age eight in the Dragon Cave of Mystics.** Full-moon-only, "chosen one" prophecy, scroll + pendant from an altar, escape-before-sunrise rule (Ep 4 flashback). Not a gift, purchase, or random find. Tonally load-bearing origin.
5. **Brian is stuck at the late first level of the Dragon Mastery Arts.** He cannot break through because the ordinary world is too energy-poor; the jump only comes inside the pendant's Mystic Space ~Ep 53. He is *not* a maxed chosen one at entry — the ceiling is visible but withheld.
6. **The threat is real and external, set up before the school.** Stella is "in a lot of danger" (Robert, Ep 2); Brian comes from actual warzones (Ep 1). The school is the cover, not the source of stakes. Don't make Maple Grove drama the whole world.

## Engines the opening supports
1. **Hidden Identity / Secret Protector (PROVEN spine):** the whole show is a warzone veteran passing as a charity-case study buddy. Strongest fit; the cover IS the premise.
2. **Underdog / Looked-Down-On Outsider (PROVEN top-funnel):** poor Chinatown kid among heirs who openly sneer; status is worn and visible, so every quiet win lands. Strong fit, drives the Thruline.
3. **Hidden Power / Secret Advantage (MODERATE):** the pendant and Dragon Mastery are concealed; he underplays them. Support, not spine — and he's not yet maxed.
4. **Bond / Mentor (MODERATE — Brian + Eli/Lin):** Samuel Lin's gruff raising and Eli's sardonic pendant-mentoring are the emotional core once revealed. Tease in the opening; pays off ~Ep 51.

## IM sources — opening window
- **The refugee-camp rescue** (Ep 1): runs into flames for a stranger's child, revives him, grieves a family he never had. *Cost-of-Entry / Empathy, T1*
- **Two hundred dollars for risking his life** (Ep 1): the orphan exploited by the only family he has. *Injustice / Status, T1*

**Injustice density** *(primary conversion driver):*
- **T1 (mandatory):** the rigged status sort — orphan with no money, no name, no family, walks into a world of heirs and is mocked on sight by guards and the girl he's risking his life to protect.
- **T2 (supporting):** the exploitation cost — raised on near-fatal missions for pocket change; carries lethal skill and a destiny he can't disclose to anyone.
- **T3 (deepening):** the loneliness underneath — the thing he wants (a family's embrace) is the one thing his life of secret service can never give him.
- **Drift risk:** play Brian as a smug secretly-powerful badass dunking on rich kids and the injustice nerve dies (CTI collapse) — empathy is "looked-down-on outsider hiding who he is," not "OP hero enjoying the flex."

# LAYER 2 — RAILS (eps ~20–93)

## 2a · Conversion Threads
1. **Will the rich girl ever see who he really is?** — Stella's thaw from contempt ("crawled out of a basement," Ep 3) to "he actually cares" (~Ep 51); the secret-bodyguard cover means she's falling for a lie about him. Can be *initiated*, never delivered, in a promo.
2. **What is the pendant actually?** — answered ~Ep 51 (Eli Grayson reveals he's a mind stored by an ancestor; "Mystic Space"). The open question of a power Brian himself doesn't understand. Tease only.
3. **Can Brian break past level one?** — pays off ~Ep 53 (the Mystic Space's energy density forces the breakthrough he couldn't reach outside). The withheld ceiling; high-value tease.
4. **What danger is Stella actually in?** — Robert says she's "in a lot of danger" (Ep 2); the real threat sits behind the schoolyard nuisances. A genuine withheld-stakes thread — surface the question, never the answer.
5. **Who else from Brian's past is circling?** — Cody Marlow (cop), Mr. Lin's classified network, the assassins the pendant remembers. The warzone life bleeds into Aspen Hills mid-run. Tease.

## 2b · Canon Limits / Never-Happens
- **The show never lets Brian blow his cover for an easy win.** His whole value is being underestimated; a promo can't have him announce he's the bodyguard or flaunt his skill for status.
- **The show never makes Brian rich or high-status.** He's salaried by Robert and lives in a borrowed guest room; the money is a wage, not wealth. No flexing.
- **The pendant never does whatever the plot needs.** Its established functions are threat-sense, threat-memory, opportunity-hints, and (later) housing Eli + the Mystic Space. Don't fabricate new powers.
- **Cultivation never comes free or instant in the ordinary world.** Brian is stalled at level one precisely because the outside world lacks energy; breakthroughs require the cave/Mystic Space conditions. No casual power-ups.
- **Brian never wins by raw aggression or cruelty.** He de-escalates, deflects, and uses precision; he refuses to humiliate for sport. A "rage-stomps the bullies" beat misreads him.
- **The romance never resolves.** Stella's feelings build under a false premise (study buddy, not protector); the payoff lives in the app.
