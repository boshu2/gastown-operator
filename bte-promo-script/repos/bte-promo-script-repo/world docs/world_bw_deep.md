## World Mechanics

### The Soul Record (system)
- A cross-dimensional "phenomenon or entity" (Lilith, Ep 13) that stirs a world's dormant mana and forces every species to evolve. Arrived on Earth Ep 1.
- **No voice, no personality, no dialogue.** Pure interface: status windows, notifications, evolution requirements. NOT an AI companion.
- **Kill → Soul Power.** A defeated enemy releases a soul orb absorbed by the killer → stats, and if the power gap is large, a **treasure orb** or **skill scroll** instead.
- **Diminishing returns:** killing same-type/level enemies yields less over time as the soul builds "resistance" (Ep 2). Leveling fully restores Health/Stamina/Mana.
- **Why the world broke (Lilith, Ep 11):** dormant mana suddenly moving "rips the old order apart." Fragile humans collapsed → mindless mutated husks (zombies). Sturdier animals/plants evolved fast. Single-celled organisms (viruses) can't resist mana → don't evolve (Ep 17), so disease isn't a mana threat.

### Stats & leveling
- Stats: **Strength, Agility, Health, Stamina, Mana, Magic.** Pre-apocalypse humans had <~10 total across all stats; a Lv2 creature ≈ 2x an average person. Ben's outlier was Magic/Mana 109.
- ~2 free stat points per level; elite/higher-level kills drop bonus stats. Ben hoards points for emergencies, dumps them mid-fight when needed (e.g., into Agility vs. the mantis).
- **Level 25 = the wall.** All beings must choose a **job/class** and pass an **evolution trial** to advance to **First Order**; until then all Soul Power is stored and no further leveling occurs. The gap between Lv24 and Lv25/First Order is "as big as the difference between Lv1 and Lv24, if not bigger" (Ep 18).
- **Orders:** Order escalation is a metaphysical rift, not additive. Killing across Orders is "borderline legendary"; killing a First Order in one strike is what Ben's unique trial demanded.

### Jobs / classes
- At Lv25, a menu of jobs appears — most get 3–4 options. **Ben got 7:** Swordsman, Mage, Assassin, Mechanic, Blacksmith, Martial Warrior, **Blood Berserker**. (Assassin tied to his Special Forces skill; Mechanic/Blacksmith to his old repair life.)
- **Blood Berserker** = a **unique** job (Berserker alone is already rare; "Blood Berserker" Lilith had never heard of). Job appeared because of how Ben used Blood Manipulation.
- **Blood Berserker trial (Ep 63):** (1) kill 100 unranked enemies in a single Blood-Manipulation attack; (2) kill 2 First Order enemies using Blood Manipulation **on himself**; (3) kill 1 First Order enemy **in a single strike**. The third is near-impossible for a Lv25. Completed ~Ep 93 → First Order, title "One Hit to Kill," passive **Blood Will** (resolve/strength harden the closer he is to death).

### Blood Manipulation (First Order skill)
- Came from the blood-hungry Giant Bee; "a skill most people wouldn't see in ten lifetimes" (Ep 14). Pushed to **Level 5 (peak)** immediately — Lilith had never seen a First Order skill maxed that fast.
- **Effects:** control his own blood (steadies fear/mind — the cardiovascular-emotion loop, Ep 2); manipulate dead/spilled blood into spears, chains, frozen arrows, armor; heat an enemy's internal blood to burst eyes / ignite organs.
- **Hard limits:** he can sense but NOT freely control a **living enemy's** internal blood early on (Lilith's blood "steady… control it? Not yet"); **fire is a perfect counter** — flames evaporate the blood (Blazing Beetle, Ep 19); every cast costs mana, and his mana drains him to near-collapse at full power.
- **Evolution path (Ep 14):** collect 100L blood from 50+ Lv5+ enemies; 10L from 5 First Order; 1L from 1 Second Order — brutal, long.

### Other skills (opening window)
- **Special Forces Soldier** (unclassified passive): elite human combat/firearms expertise; turned Ben from "no combat experience" to functional fighter.
- **Stone Heart** (Tier 3 passive, awakened Ep 13): emotional armor — others (esp. opposite sex) can't sway him unless truly close; **side effect:** affection for his inner circle intensifies. Born of past betrayal; subtly hardens his worldview (he kills without flinching post-Kyle).
- **Health Boost** (unclassified passive): +20 Health, flat.
- **Skills come two ways:** scrolls from first-kills, and **internal awakening** from who you are / what you've lived (Stone Heart). Five external active-skill slots max (brain-overload limit); passives unlimited.

### Gear / treasure tiers (Lilith, Ep 8)
- Rainbow tiers, weakest→strongest: **Red (standard) · Orange (rare) · Yellow (magical) · Green (epic) · Cyan (legendary) · Indigo (semi-divine) · Violet (god-tier).** Even a red orb "more valuable than a military-grade rifle at this stage."
- **Ben's early kit:** Breaker Blade (rare; +20 Str, cuts anything <Lv40), Full Coat (rare; +5 all, stops small-arms), Velocity Boots (standard; +10 Agi, melted by the beetle), Piercing Glove (standard; +5 Str), Special Forces scroll. Later: Hurricane Pendant + Draughtbane Dagger (off Kyle).

## Setting / Geography (what's known by ep 100)
- **Arcadia State University** — prestigious, ~tens of thousands of students, "a city within a city" in **Manhattan**; everything on-campus. Men's & women's dorms on opposite sides; the **gym** at center is steel-doored, concrete, has a cafeteria (a week's food for ~100) — Ben's base.
- **Ben's family** is in **Queens**, across the city; even pre-apocalypse a multi-hour drive, now potentially weeks through wrecked streets. He has not attempted the trip in the opening window.
- **The world beyond campus is UNKNOWN.** No one has left the grounds; no military, no confirmed safe zones; phones give a dead-line message (mana-fried towers). The campus is the entire promo-able world.
- **FABRICATION BLOCKLIST:** there is no faction/entity/concept called **"the State"** in the source. It does not exist. Consistent with "world beyond campus is unknown."

## Flavor notes
- **The opening injustice is the crowd, not the monster** — the abandoned girl dies because people won't even look back. The apocalypse is framed as moral collapse, not just survival horror.
- **Lilith is the explainer + the load-bearing restraint** — she carries the rules (so the audience learns the system), but her combat ban means Ben earns every win. Her flirtation is comic texture over an unrevealed agenda.
- **The unreachable family is the whole engine** — Ben's coldness, his refusal to leave campus, his hoarding of power all trace to mom/dad/Megan in Queens. The phone-call breakdown (Ep 10–11) is the emotional spine; do not resolve it.
- **Blood Manipulation is a horror power, not a flex** — bursting eyes, boiling organs, blood spears from corpses. It is undercut by fire, mana cost, and the Lv25 wall, so it never reads as effortless.
- **Stone Heart externalizes the trauma** — a literal skill born of past betrayal that makes him colder to strangers and fiercer for his own. It is why he can execute Kyle without a flicker.
- **The cap is the cliffhanger** — Level 25 / the brutal job trial is the structural "can he break through?" loop; the First Order payoff lives deep (~Ep 93).

## Relationship map (ep 100)
- **Ben ↔ family (Megan/parents):** the foundation; unreachable, status unknown; the source of every choice. Never confirmed.
- **Ben ↔ Lilith:** advisor/protégé with deep mutual mystery; he doesn't trust her fully, she gambles on him. No romance through Ep 71.
- **Ben ↔ Brooke:** two apex survivors circling each other — mutual wariness, grudging respect, NO romance. She backstops him (ice-shatters Kyle's corpse; fights beside him at Ep 93).
- **Ben ↔ Chase:** moral foil — Chase wants law, Ben wants results; uneasy alliance.
- **Ben ↔ Logan:** loose ally; Logan wants to team up, Ben keeps distance.
- **Ben ↔ Kyle:** the human-predator antagonist; resolved by execution (Ep 39–40).
- **Ben ↔ Evan/Katie:** the seed of a recruited fighter-loyalty network (found-family in SETUP, not yet a bonded crew).
