## Protagonist — Ebony

**Identity.** Firstborn of King Liam, barred from the throne because Allarion law forbids a woman to rule. At 14 (the female norm is 18) she shifts into Nova, an ancient stone dragon, and must hide it — a hidden dragon is a capital crime. A seer prophesied at her birth she'll be "the first female ruler of Allarion." Engine fit: **Forbidden Power / Hidden Identity** — the kingdom's strongest dragon, in a kingdom that would execute her for having it. Her cruelty is a real flaw; the arc is becoming worthy of the prophecy.

**Combat.** Trained in secret with Davin (blackmailed into it) for four years — sword and shield, fast, rage-first. Out-spars and pins him by the eve of the ball. Weakness is temper; Nova warns, "If you let him control your emotions, you let him control you."

**Power Source.** Nova — ancient stone dragon; melts sand to glass, dirt to obsidian. Hard limits: she hides her own presence from all dragons (this is what keeps the secret); lends strength/speed but each loan drains Ebony; cannot *control* Ebony, who is not a Hosento. Her command power works on lesser dragons, not her own willful host.

**Voice.** Imperious, entitled, transactional — blackmail and threats of dismissal are casual tools. Quick to fury, slow to apologize, bristling against male authority, capable of withering scorn. Not a stock heroine — a tyrant-in-training the prophecy insists will soften.

**Voice Samples (verbatim — never paraphrase):**
1. "Right now, I'm the one you need to worry about. I could go to my father and say I want a new nanny. He wouldn't think twice. Keep quiet, and you need not worry." (Ep 1) — blackmail as casual tool
2. "If I'm truly to become Queen, I must serve as an example to my people. Pushing through pain is a bravery that wins wars." (Ep 3) — cruelty rationalized as duty
3. "We might be bonded, but I am still the princess of this realm. I hold power over you. You must do as I say." (Ep 2) — commanding Nova
4. "I. WILL. NOT. HAVE. YOU!" (Ep 16) — the ball-rampage refrain
5. "I can't do this anymore. I messed everything up. I messed up my destiny." (Ep 84) — breaking point, on her knees

**Drift tripwires.** Hero-confident voice | forgives easily | trained after shift | accepts male authority easily | cruelty as "misunderstanding" | apologizes unprompted | Nova's lessons land instead of clash.

## Setting — Entry-Window World

**Feel.** Allarion: a clifftop castle over Lake Halua, reachable by one highway. Everyone bonds a dragon at puberty (women ~18, men earlier) and dragon strength sets rank — but women are barred from ruling, fighting, training, or wielding weapons, and hiding a dragon is death. Gilded and oppressive at once: a father who loves his daughter yet would rather a commoner take the throne. The ball is the entry-window's spectacle and trap.

**Sensory Samples (verbatim):**
1. "She will be powerful and brave. She will become the first female ruler of Allarion." (Ep 1) — the seer's prophecy
2. "I would rather see a commoner sit on the throne than a woman. Even my own daughter." (Ep 1) — King Liam, the wall Ebony must break

**Drift tripwires.** Open-world freedom frame | women rule or fight openly in Allarion | Ebony shifts after Ep 1 / at the normal age | Nova arrives as a blank slate | servants respect without fear | prophecy never mentioned | peace already old and settled.

## Tone Constraint
Hold the injustice as a *legal system working as designed* — laws with enforcement (death for a hidden dragon, flogging for disobedience), with a flawed Ebony inside it. Soften her cruelty into a misunderstanding, or drop the injustice into pure victimhood, and the transformation thesis collapses.

# LAYER 1 — ACTIVATION CANON  ·  *the opening anchor — load at W1 + canon gate*

## First 5 minutes  *(the 50%-drop gate)*
Ebony is born; the seer prophesies she'll be "the first female ruler of Allarion"; King Liam, enraged his heir is a girl, jails the seer and forbids the prophecy. Fourteen years later Ebony — sneaking out to train illegally — overhears her father say he'd rather a commoner rule than his own daughter. She runs, and in raw grief first-shifts into Nova, who names herself an ancient stone dragon and promises, "One day, I promise you will be crowned queen."

## First 5 episodes  *(the first sitting)*
Ep 2: the King decrees Ebony picks a husband at a ball on her 18th birthday, or marries the first man at the gate. Ep 3 (four-year jump): now 18, she out-spars Davin (the knight she blackmailed into training her). Eps 4–5: Piroria — Prince Greyson and silver dragon Radion, who senses an awakened ancient dragon in Allarion, pushes Greyson to the ball, and reveals his mission to hunt rebels who fled Dragos Paradise. The listener knows: the hidden dragon, the ball ultimatum, Nova/Radion as ancients, the patriarchal law.

## First 20 episodes  *(texture band)*
Greyson arrives at Allarion (Eps 6–11); Liam floats him as a match. The ball (Eps 13–17): Radion recognizes her; their dance becomes a forbidden-truth exchange about Pirorian women warriors; Ebony's rage becomes a rampage on the princes; Nova's involuntary "STOP!" forces the room to its knees, exposing her power; Greyson whispers "Pick me"; she flees. Eps 18–20: no man will claim her (Radion secretly commanded every dragon to refuse), and Liam sends her to the council. (Banishment lands Ep 36 — tease, don't anchor.)

## HARD BLOCKS  *(opening-binding)*
1. **Nova is an ancient STONE dragon, not a regular Allarion fire dragon** — granddaughter of Omari and Mahala, arrived with memory/personality intact, hides her presence from all dragons. Treating her as standard breaks the premise.
2. **A woman hiding a dragon in Allarion is a capital crime; women cannot rule, fight, train, or wield weapons.** Framing Allarion as women-friendly breaks the show.
3. **Ebony shifted at 14 (years early); the secret holds only because Nova hides them both** — not a public power, not a gift, a death-penalty secret.
4. **Ebony's cruelty is a real flaw** — blackmail, scorn, the rampage that gets wives beaten. Sanitizing her into a pure heroine violates the transformation thesis.
5. **Radion is a silver dragon (fire+ice+water), unique among living dragons, hunting rebels who fled Dragos Paradise** — not merely "the strongest dragon."
6. **Nova and Radion are ancient true mates; Ebony↔Greyson flows FROM theirs.** Her rejection blocks but doesn't sever it.
7. **Boris's identity is a deliberate, unresolved mystery (enters Ep 56)** — never confirm/deny Boris = Greyson; the source never resolves it through ch 100.
8. **The prophecy's terms are fixed:** wise queen, two nations made one, reign NOT begun in Allarion. Reference, don't rewrite.

**Injustice density** *(primary conversion driver):*
- **T1:** A firstborn barred from her own throne for being a woman, in a kingdom that would kill her for the power she already holds.

# LAYER 2 — RAILS (eps ~20–100)  ·  *load at W1 (threads) + canon gate (limits)*

## 2a · Conversion Threads
1. **Who is Boris?** — every detail (survival mastery, mountain knowledge, a stocked cabin, a seer who calls him "Prince," a dragon that talks to Nova) contradicts "vagabond," but the source never resolves it through ch 100. Pays off: **never (open through ch 100)**. The single strongest open loop — *initiate the question, never deliver the answer.*
2. **The refused true mate** — the audience knows Greyson is hers; she rejected him and was banished for it; he may be closer than she knows. Pays off: **unresolved through ch 100.**
3. **From tyrant to wise queen** — the prophecy promises it; her breakdown (Ep 84) is the hinge. Pays off: **incomplete through ch 100** (she begins to change; the crown is not won).
4. **Bella's mirror rise** — the servant who kept Ebony's secret becomes a warrior at the Lily Training Center; ascends as Ebony descends. Pays off ~Ep 78+ (still rising at ch 100).
5. **The rebel-dragon war** — Goliak (via Prince Idris) hunts Ebony's ancient dragon; "the realm of man will be under attack." Pays off: **setup only through ch 100** — a ticking long-game, not detonated.

## 2b · Canon Limits / Never-Happens
- **The show never lets Nova control Ebony.** Ebony is not a Hosento; Nova guides and scolds but cannot command her. (Promote toward HARD BLOCK if a promo's cornerstone is dragon-puppeteering.)
- **The show never makes ancient-dragon power free.** Every loan of strength/healing drains the host; Nova's hiding magic takes concentration she can lose.
- **The show never resolves Boris's identity** (through ch 100).
- **The show never lets Ebony win through redemption-by-apology.** Change comes only through loss of privilege and survival, against her own resistance.
- **The show never frames Piroria and Allarion as long-settled allies** — the peace treaty is recent and fragile.
