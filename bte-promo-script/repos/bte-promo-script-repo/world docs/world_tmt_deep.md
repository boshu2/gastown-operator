## The Mech Designer System — mechanics

- **Core loop:** Ves must design a *viable* mech himself → pay **Design Points (DP)** to fabricate it. The System evaluates/fabricates; it does NOT design.
- **DP earning:** estimated 3.89 DP/day at start (Ep 3); earned from his own original designs only — copying others' designs earns 0 DP (Ep 15). An average mech costs ~1.44 billion DP to fabricate accurately (Ep 3) — establishing the scale of the climb.
- **DP ≠ credits:** cannot convert. Separate economy.
- **Fabrication of impossible objects:** uses [REDACTED] tech to instantiate real items (Lucky) from DP (Ep 4).
- **Welcome package (Ep 3–4):** 10 DP, 3 copper lottery tickets (all lost), 1 Gold-ranked gem cat.
- **Locked at Novice rank:** Missions/Shop/Lottery gated until rank-up.
- **Tone:** the System insults Ves ("good-for-nothing loser," "An all-rounder in incompetence") while claiming neutrality.

## World / setting mechanics

- **The genetic divide:** only **3.5%** of humans ("potentates") can survive the mech neural interface; the other **96.5%** ("norms") cannot — a failed interface fries the brain. Tested at age 10 by DNA/blood. Potentates are conscripted into the Mech Corps. (Ep 1)
- **Mechs:** ~50-ft bipedal war machines; displaced most conventional military. ~400 years since the first mechs (Ep 18). Designers can be as famous as pilots.
- **Mech design economics:** requires capital, hundreds of licensed patents, MTA (Mech Trade Association) certification, and government manufacturing permits. Tiered designer class: superstar contractors → veteran middle class → bottom-rung repair/maintenance wanna-bes (where Ves starts). (Ep 1)
- **Iron Spirit:** ultra-realistic online mech game (BSBH Corp); mechs must be fabricated within its sim; designs sell for in-game gold or real credits. Ves enters here because he can't afford real mechs. Registration 800 cr; virtual licenses 10k–100k cr. (Eps 4–5)
- **The X-Factor:** disputed notion that mechs carry a "life"/emotional resonance that lets a pilot exceed peak performance. Unmeasurable; dismissed by scientists, attested by veteran pilots. Ves's evolving theory ties it to emotion bled into design. (Eps 17–20)
