## World mechanics — full

**Divine Serum / Awakening.** Mana Awakening ("Divine") Serum given to all graduating ~14-year-olds; ~5% compatible. ~1-in-100 fatal reaction; painful even when it works. Reverse-engineered over decades from a broken ancient **System Stone** found under an ancient Sanctorum. Compatible recipients get a class marking (ice/fire/blades/shields/paws/bows/wands documented; Karl's three claws are not). Powers activate only on first skill use.

**Ranking scale (elites & beasts, by what you can defeat):** Common → Awakened → Ascended → Commander → Royal → Monarch → Overlord (Mythical referenced). Internal measures differ by class (mages = mana, druids = mortal grades, warriors = physique). Most students reach Awakened by end of year one; geniuses near Ascended.

**Academy badges (worn on the jacket — the show's visible-status engine):** White = Common, Black = Awakened, Bronze = Ascended.

**Karl's Beast Master class.**
- *Beast Space:* a mental dimension, initially empty darkness; Karl builds terrain by meditation; size/quality scale with energy invested; it sustains the beast's life and growth.
- *Bonding:* only infant/egg-stage monsters, by moving them into an empty space; bond forms on hatch + feeding. **Each beast needs its own space** — they can't be shared.
- *Cost:* bonding when unprepared siphons energy from Karl's body. Bonding Thor + Rae too close together (Ep 81–83) nearly killed him — withered to skeletal, IV drips, Commander-rank healing food; minimum requirement learned: one separate space with ≥ the creature's stored energy.
- *Skill inheritance:* Karl gains the beast's innate skills at reduced effectiveness (can't modify physical weapons, only the energy form). Hawk → Super Vision, Rend, Wind Shield, Shred; Thor → Refreshing Lightning; Rae → Offensive Adaptation; Slash insight (Ep 70) = an energy-efficient stripped-down Rend.
- *Physical feedback:* as a beast grows, Karl's physique strengthens proportionally — the **primary** power-scaling mechanism (training only sharpens skill).
- *Food storage:* the space stores food indefinitely (an infinite cooler); rejects non-food.
- *Communication:* telepathic link with all bonded beasts (concepts Karl translates); beasts develop vocabulary over time.
- *Count by ch 100:* three (Hawk most developed, Thor functional, Rae minimal-but-fast). A space splits off a new one when the first reaches quality + surplus energy (Ep 70).

**Beast biology.** Beasts strengthen by eating higher-rank food/resources (mana-infused monster blood, strength stones, Wind Stones for air affinity, skill-insight foods, restricted Dragonite pills). Skills beyond species norm cause visible mutations (Hawk's red streaks; his abnormal size). Natural plateau set by prey grade (Windspeed Hawks cap low-Awakened on Common mice) unless resource-boosted.

**Academy structure.** Isolated, one road in/out beside the rail; ring of grassland/forest/swamp crossed on arrival. Dimensionally-expanded magical dorms (balconies/halls larger than physics allows; staircases reaching only your floor); first-come room choice. Three cafeterias (mage / warrior / dorm), 4 AM–midnight, all magical-beast meat and enhanced produce. Daily loop: morning skill drills, afternoon martial arts, Saturday team practical (transported to realistic environments), plus tutor sessions and credit-earning field missions.

**Karl's balcony greenhouse** (inherited from the "kitchen witch"): Mountain Gooseberries (monster bait / Hawk's seasoning, weak mana), Honey Berries (bear bait / sabotage), Clear Mind Fruit (limited-use exam aid), catnip (bribe security cats), plus teas, pears, etc. Self-watering by magic; soundproofed. Emotional home base (meditation, Dana's study, Rae's hammock).

**Broader world.** The Golden Dragon Nation (founded by the mythical Immortal Golden Dragon; coal/lithium mines, cities, frontier). Rivals: the Ocean People (water magic), other nations, the Beast Lands. The Church (High Priests/Priestesses, holy/healing magic) predates the Serum and runs Seminary academies. Modern tech (cars, helicopters, computers, phones — but Karl's family can't afford one; armored vehicles, flamethrower turrets). Elites live on stipends scaled to power; **Idols** (singer/actor/influencer elites) are the public face — the track Dana is being pushed toward.

**Bureau of Elite Development.** Government agency (under the National Security Agency); guides promising elites, can override Academy plans. Operates in pairs. Runs Karl (test subject) and Dana (entertainer→messaging) per the **Academy Board**, which plans careers from semester one, debates the Karl–Dana relationship, and weighs using Dana as leverage on Karl. Thesis line (Ep 12): without power he'd "just be at the bottom of a much fancier pile" — the system sorts by power everywhere; the Academy is a shinier mine.

**Inscription tech.** Some elites inscribe skill books that teach an ability on contact and vanish when a compatible reader uses them (Ep 56). Needs an original/mastered template; only a handful exist (Rend, Shred, Golem focus).

## Relationship map (ch 100)
- **Karl ↔ Hawk:** firstborn bond; constant thoughts; Hawk runs food policy, Karl runs everything else; Hawk taught Dana mana control.
- **Karl ↔ Thor:** paternal; a trusting baby who named himself after a god and loves charging barriers.
- **Karl ↔ Rae:** mutual respect; proud, poetic, intelligent; obeys in combat, socializes toward Dana.
- **Karl ↔ Dana:** unspoken slow-burn via proximity (balcony study, dark-maze hand-hold); the Board watches.
- **Karl ↔ Rita:** student–tutor with real mutual respect; she resents the Bureau but never on Karl.
- **Karl ↔ Valerie:** handler/asset, professional and cool ("test subject"; "almost smiled").
- **Dana ↔ Hawk / Dana ↔ Rae:** Hawk mentored her; Rae bonded instantly, builds a hammock over her study spot.
- **Beasts ↔ each other:** hear each other through Karl; Hawk is senior, Rae the smartest (lets them think otherwise), Thor follows.

## Cross-show contamination guard
FLBM and **WBT** are both beast/academy underdog shows. Keep distinct:
- FLBM = **Karl**, mining-town kid, three-claw **Beast Master**, builds mental **Beast Spaces**, bonds a Windspeed **Hawk** (then Thor/Rae). Beasts are real, fight, evolve, have personalities.
- WBT = Ren, spore/**mushrooms** (cosmetic, never a combat power per WBT canon).
Never transplant WBT's mushroom/spore framing onto Karl, and never give Karl a fabricated or WBT-style beast.
