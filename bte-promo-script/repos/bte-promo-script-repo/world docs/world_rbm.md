# LAYER 0 — ESSENCE  ·  *the only default load*

## Three Pillars (locked)
1. **Stolen Future / Triple Betrayal**  *(Thruline)*
2. **Star Rating = Social Destiny**
3. **The God-Tier Beast That Looks Like a Chicken**

## Protagonist — Jasper Cooke
**Identity.** A prodigy beastmaster betrayed at 18: his lover Lydia framed him as a predator and handed his stolen treasure (a Saintbeast Battle Soul) to the noble Niall Redfern, who killed his beast Midas and took the credit. Three years on — disgraced, disowned, mother dying — an egg hatches: a one-star chicken secretly a Primordial Chaos Beast. The engine is the gap between what everyone thinks he is (a ruined predator) and what he actually carries. Revenge/Justice Denied on Underdog Awakening.
**Combat.** Fights with his own body and a knife (Scarlet Vein Knife) on speed, precision, assassination timing — never brute force. Wins by type advantage (fire means nothing to his phoenix), a precise strike, or borrowed strength. Always outranked going in.
**Power Source.** Beastblood cultivation re-started from zero after Midas died, refined through Felix's phoenix bloodline — far faster than academy method. Limits: Felix is a one-star chick, evolves to 6-star only after manna (~ep 26); his nature is secret. The left arm is a black-scaled beast claw with a red third eye ("Maze Gazer," projects illusion) — origin UNRESOLVED. Eats spirit gems/herbs raw via fire resistance.
**Voice.** Dry, deadpan, controlled fury under a calm surface. Banters with Felix; tender with his mother; meets nobles with cold courtesy that curdles into contempt. Not a swaggering hero — a man who already lost everything and is done flinching.

**Voice Samples (verbatim — never paraphrase):**
1. "You were supposed to fly with me. You were supposed to stay." (Ep 2) — grief, to dying Midas
2. "I've just stopped letting people like you think you can spit on us and get away with it." (Ep 6) — cold defiance
3. "I'd rather be crushed standing up than crawl out of town." (Ep 7) — resolve
4. "My past is my own. I don't toss it aside when it no longer suits me." (Ep 11) — to his half-brother
5. "Not dead yet, brother." (Ep 13) — deadpan, blade at Albie's throat

**Drift tripwires.** Wins through raw power | reveals Felix's god-tier nature publicly | Felix shown as already huge/powerful | resurrects Midas | portrays Lydia sympathetically | resolves the cure / the arm / the false reputation.

## Setting — Entry-Window World (Blazehollow)
**Feel.** A small southern-marches town where star ratings sort your life and the governor's family rules. Jasper grew up in the grandest house on the estate, Rainlovers' Terrace — until he's disowned by letter on his mother's birthday and swept out "like unwanted furniture." Home becomes a ramshackle edge-of-town cottage; the town treats him as a cautionary tale and his half-brother Albie as the new golden boy. Beyond: Red Twill Tor, a brutal hill-forest of wildbeasts and rare spirit herbs.

**Sensory Samples (verbatim):**
1. "Her hair had turned white. Her skin had thinned and faded… Her Four Winged Goldroc lay just beyond the doors… half bare, its feathers fallen away, its breathing shallow and uneven." (Ep 4)
2. "It was a tiny yellow chick. It stood in the middle of the broken shell, fluff sticking out in every direction." (Ep 3)
3. "The skin on his left hand began to turn black… flesh shifted and hardened into black scales… each one a flawless hexagon catching the candlelight with an oily sheen." (Ep 9)

**Drift tripwires.** Blazehollow as a fair meritocracy | Jasper's reputation as recoverable | the cottage as cosy rather than fallen | star ratings as soft guidance rather than hard social destiny.

## Tone Constraint
Injustice-driven revenge underdog, not a flex. Jasper's edge is hidden and his shame is public; if a beat lets him simply overpower opponents or skips the triple betrayal + dying mother, empathy collapses and it reads as a power fantasy. The comedy (Felix's banter) is a release valve, never the lead.

# LAYER 1 — ACTIVATION CANON  ·  *load at W1 + canon gate*

## First 5 minutes  *(the 50%-drop gate)*
A storm; a flight home for an anniversary. Lydia lures Jasper in, then turns: "I nearly ended up trapped with you… You're not what I want." She tears her own dress and screams for rescue as Niall Redfern descends on a three-eyed lightning eagle and casts Jasper as her attacker — "your lifebound beast will die." Midas fights, is overloaded, and dies throwing his body across both Jasper and the treacherous Lydia. Niall seizes the Saintbeast Battle Soul from the dying Goldroc and flies off with Lydia. Final bond-pulse: "Jasper. Safe." Triple betrayal — romantic, violent, parental — in one scene.

## First 5 episodes  *(the first sitting)*
Three years later in Blazehollow. Jasper is disgraced; his mother Freya is dying of an ageing curse (40, looks 80). One of ten secret eggs in his Vitalbound Expanse hatches: a tiny talking yellow chick — the Everblaze Inferno Phoenix, a Primordial Chaos Beast, shown as one star that explodes into a starscape only Jasper sees. The governor's third wife Lois delivers divorce papers on Freya's birthday; the two are disowned and evicted. Jasper humiliates half-sister Sophie and her Flaming Hawk using only the chick, then they leave with their heads high.

## First 20 episodes  *(texture band)*
The selection is in ten days, on the governor's wedding to Cora Pennington of Thunderbolt Hall. Jasper's left arm transforms into a black claw with a third eye (ep 9). He crashes the selection, breaks half-brother Albie publicly, then is blocked by Cora's brother Euan (15, five-star Lightning Bolt Cheetah) — stakes a one-month rematch: the Order of the Amber Flame against his life. He heads to Red Twill Tor for his mother's medicine (clear spirit grass) and the manna Thunderbolt Hall hunts; meets Maisie Denholm and Princess Elowen Pryce there. Steals the manna; Felix purifies it and evolves to 6-star (~ep 26).

## HARD BLOCKS  *(opening-binding)*
1. **Felix is a one-star yellow chick secretly a Primordial Chaos Beast (Everblaze Inferno Phoenix); his true nature is a total secret no character knows.** The star orb reads only the surface six stars; revealing his god-tier nature publicly breaks the core engine.
2. **Jasper's Vitalbound Expanse holds 10 eggs; only ONE (Felix) has hatched through ep 100.** The other 9 are dormant; do not hatch additional beasts.
3. **Felix evolves to 6-star only after manna purification (~ep 26); not before.** Even at 6-star his bloodline is only partly unlocked.
4. **Midas (5-star Goldroc) is DEAD — killed by Niall in ep 1; cannot be resurrected.**
5. **The Saintbeast Battle Soul was Jasper's discovery, stolen by Niall via Lydia's betrayal, now credited to Niall.** Ownership unresolved through ep 100; Niall has not fully mastered it (it's unstable).
6. **Lydia's betrayal was premeditated and remains so — never portray her sympathetically.** She framed Jasper, handed the Soul to Niall, and places an assassination bounty via Bloodflower Parlour (~ep 47-49).
7. **Freya's lifesbane curse: 40, looks 80, ~six months to live.** Cure requires the head of the Skyward Reliquary / Cloister — her estranged father, **Duncan Ashdown**, who cut her off 20 years ago.
8. **Jasper's left arm is a black-scaled beast claw with a red third eye; origin mysterious and UNRESOLVED through ep 100.** Linked to the dark shadow that hunted Felix in the hatching vision.
9. **Jasper's false reputation (drug-using predator) persists.** Load-bearing — it drives institutional rejection throughout.

## Entry map  *(tease ok, anchor flags)*

3. **Secret Power / Hidden Beast (UNTESTED):** Felix looks like a chicken, IS a god-tier Primordial Chaos Beast; nobody knows.
4. **Bond / Family Loyalty (UNTESTED):** dying mother, ticking clock, the cure as the prize behind the revenge.

# LAYER 2 — RAILS (eps ~20–100)

## 2a · Conversion Threads
1. **The stolen Saintbeast Battle Soul** — Niall wears Jasper's prize and his story; the Soul is unstable and unmastered even after three years. Pays off deep (unresolved through ep 100). Sell the theft, never deliver the reclamation.
2. **The cure behind the locked door** — Freya's only hope is her estranged father, the Grand Master who cast her out 20 years ago and refuses to help (~ep 70). Pure faith-against-a-shut-door. Initiate the race against her clock; never deliver the cure.
3. **The one-month wager** — the Order of the Amber Flame against Jasper's own life vs Euan. Pays off ~ep 32–36. Sell the impossible stakes; withhold the outcome.
4. **The mystery of the black arm + the shadow** — the claw and third eye match the shadow that hunted Felix and "killed" him in a past life. Unresolved through ep 100. Tease, never answer.
5. **Maisie's sealed powers + strange recognition** — ten sealed abilities (two unlocked), perfect sync with Jasper, a sense they've met before; origin unsolved. Tease the bond, never explain her.

## 2b · Canon Limits / Never-Happens
- **The show never lets Felix simply overpower opponents through size** — early wins are type advantage (fire-immune phoenix), the black arm, precision, or borrowed strength (Maisie's sync), never raw scale.
- **The show never reveals Felix as a god-tier beast in-world** — the star orb only ever reads six stars; the Primordial Chaos truth stays Jasper's secret.
- **The show never clears Jasper's name or resolves the cure within the opening window** — both stay load-bearing, open wounds.
- **The show never makes Lydia a victim** — her betrayal was premeditated and she escalates to a kill-bounty.
