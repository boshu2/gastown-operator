## Protagonist — Karl
**Identity.** Lithium-mining-town kid, fourteen, last to hit puberty, "below average sized," no last name recorded — "dirt floor poor." Parents are living miners he can't afford to phone; his goal is to earn enough to "move them out of the mines." Drew the one marking (three claw marks) no guidebook covers — so the Academy gives him a personal tutor and treats him as a test subject. Engine: **Underdog Awakening** — an unknown class inventing his own power while everyone assumes he's a nobody.
**Combat.** Strategic, observant, never cocky-strong. Reads threats with Super Vision, positions, stays covered; Rend is fast/multi-hit, not heavy. Energy-depletes after a few casts. Reflexively underreports his abilities to avoid becoming a target.
**Power Source.** Three-claw Beast Master class. Bonds infant/egg monsters into a **Beast Space** (a mental dimension built by meditation) and inherits the beast's skills at *reduced* power (Hawk → Super Vision, Rend). Each beast needs a **separate** space; his physique strengthens as the beast grows. First beast came by **accident** — grabbed a kitchen egg out of hunger; it vanished into his mind and hatched. No ritual, bloodline, or mentor. CANNOT will one into being (tries for a dragon, gets nothing).
**Voice.** Self-aware, jokingly defeatist, thinks plans aloud. Dark humor about his situation; treats Hawk as a peer ("Alright, buddy"), not a tool. Names his fears (mines, starvation, being small) instead of projecting hero confidence.

**Drift tripwires.** Karl named legendary/famous at entry | hero-confident voice | hides Hawk from teammates | wins on raw strength alone | celebrated lineage or family name | physically imposing/tall before the Hawk feedback.

## Setting — Entry-Window World
**Feel.** The Golden Divine Academy: a football stadium converted ten years ago, isolated, one road in/out beside the rail, ringed a kilometer out by grassland/forest/swamp the new students cross on foot to arrive. Magically expanded dorms (rooms bigger inside than out; staircases that only reach your floor). Karl's tiny balcony room hides a self-sustaining greenhouse. Badge color worn on the jacket — White = nobody, Black = Awakened — visible to all. Military chain of command; ambient bullying; the poorest, least tech-literate kid is the obvious target.

**Sensory Samples (verbatim):**
1. *"Without all the dust from the mines, it smelled strangely like trees and flowers here."* (Ep 6)
2. *"Blood clogged his lungs, leaving him drowning while sitting in the metal folding chair of his school's auditorium."* (Ep 1)

**Drift tripwires.** Academy framed as an ancient tradition (it's a decade old) | badge system absent/meaningless | beasts as external tools not dependents | Academy as exclusively safe.

## Tone Constraint
Grounded underdog with warm comedy, never wish-fulfillment power fantasy. Growth is real but *costly and public*; the bird has opinions and a stomach. If a beat makes Karl effortlessly strong or chosen-by-destiny, the tone has slipped — the nerve is a poor kid out-thinking a rigged room, not a prodigy steamrolling it.

# LAYER 1 — ACTIVATION CANON  ·  *the opening anchor*

## First 5 minutes  *(the 50%-drop gate)*
The recruitment-drive injection in the school gym. The shot "only supposed to hurt as the needle poked you" instead tears him apart inside — blood in the lungs, world goes black; Karl thinks he's the 1-in-100 fatal reaction. He survives. Three claw marks appear on his forearm, growing more realistic as he stares (flesh torn, skin smooth underneath). The injustice is set: a poor kid risks death for a lottery ticket out, draws a mark nobody can explain.

## First 5 episodes  *(the first sitting)*
The marking isn't in the guidebook ("no last name recorded") — he's on his own. Hungry, he grabs a kitchen egg → *[Suitable Pet Target Found: hatching]* → a Windspeed Hawk hatches in his mind. He's a creature-bonding class: Super Vision and Rend at reduced power, a Beast Space built by meditation. He underreports his abilities on the homework. Established here: Karl + Hawk, the Beast Space, badge ranks (White/Black/Bronze), and the field-crossing arrival where he leads strangers (Dana, James, Kruger) to the gate first.

## First 20 episodes  *(texture band)*
Sergeant Rita as personal tutor; morning skill-interception drills (Karl + Hawk block a whole mage class); afternoon martial arts. Resource cultivation begins — monster blood, strength stones, Headmaster-made Wind Stones rocket Hawk fledgling→Awakened in single sessions, dragging Karl's physique up with him. He outgrows uniforms weekly; the greenhouse balcony becomes home base. Dana is present as ally; Bureau, Thor, Rae and field missions have NOT entered.

## HARD BLOCKS  *(opening-binding)*
1. **Karl's three-claw Beast Master class is genuinely unique** — not in any guidebook, no Academy record, never seen by teachers or brass (Ep 2, 4, 11). The uniqueness earns him a personal tutor. *(NB: wild beasts and bear-shifting Druids exist everywhere — unique is Karl's bonding/taming class, not "beasts exist.")*
2. **The show never gives Karl a beast it doesn't actually grant him.** Source beasts: Hawk (Windspeed Hawk, Ep 3–4), Thor (Lightning Cerro, ~Ep 83), Rae (Bloodbath Spider, ~Ep 84). Karl *tries to will a dragon and gets nothing* (Ep 3). Never invent a beast (wolf pup, dragon) — fabricated-beast promos are the documented failure mode (deep doc).
3. **The first beast came from hunger and accident** — grabbed a kitchen egg, it vanished into his Beast Space (Ep 3). Not a reward, ritual, or curated pick. Tonally load-bearing.
4. **Karl starts small and lowest-status** — last to hit puberty, "below average sized," no last name, no phone, no tech literacy, "dirt floor poor" (Ep 1–2, 10). His growth is a *consequence* of the Hawk feedback loop/injections — never imposing before that.
5. **The elite system is new and experimental.** Serum reverse-engineered over decades from a broken ancient System Stone; Academy a decade-old converted stadium; strongest graduates mid-twenties (Ep 2, 4, 12). No centuries-deep tradition.
6. **Beasts are dependents, not gear.** Hawk has a stomach, ego, grudges, and a vote on his Beast Space. Suppressing the beast's personality breaks the emotional core.

# LAYER 2 — RAILS (eps ~20–100)

## 2a · Conversion Threads
1. **Will the poor kid actually break the badge ceiling?** — pays off ~ep 47–52 (White→Black, first in his year, after Goblin Town). The publicly-visible status reversal; can be *initiated*, never delivered, in a promo.
2. **What is Karl's unknown class actually capable of?** — pays off incrementally (skill books ~ep 55–57; second/third beast ~ep 81–84). The open question of a class with no recorded ceiling.
3. **Karl & Dana — unspoken, watched from above.** — slow-burn through proximity (study on the balcony, held hands in a dark maze); the Board frets over it. Material, social-friendly. Never resolve the romance.
4. **Dana is being slotted into an entertainer/"messaging" career she didn't choose.** — surfaces ~ep 27, named by the Board ~ep 61. Real institutional assignment ("abbreviated career as an entertainer, followed by... messaging" — Ep 61). A genuine injustice thread — but a *Dana* thread that enters mid-run; tease only.
5. **Can the power kill him?** — pays off ~ep 81–83 (bonding two beasts at once withers him to skeletal; IV drips, Commander-rank healing food, "I would most likely be dead"). The cost reveal; high-stakes tease.

## 2b · Canon Limits / Never-Happens
- **The show never lets Karl bond a beast for free.** Each beast needs its own Beast Space; bonding beyond his readiness siphons his own body. A promo can't imply casual collect-'em-all power-ups.
- **The show never lets Karl will a beast into existence.** He can only bond an infant/egg he physically obtains; he tries for a dragon and gets nothing (Ep 3).
- **Beasts never become interchangeable combat tools.** They are dependents with personalities and appetites; Karl's care (feeding, building their spaces) is how he expresses love.
- **The growth never comes purely from fighting.** Raw power scales from feeding/meditation feedback through the beasts; physical training only sharpens skill. A "got buff by brawling" arc misrepresents the class.
- **The elite world never has ancient depth.** Serum, ranks and Academy are modern, improvised, casualty-averse. Don't write timeless lineages.
