## Power & progression mechanics

**Spiritual-warrior ladder (low→high):** Beginner (levels 1–9+) → Apprentice (1–9) → Master (1–7+). Leveling slows dramatically at Master; the only true gauge of a Master's level is battle. Combat against strong foes is the fastest way to advance.

**Examination ranks (student exam, ep 19–24):** ranked 1 (worst) up to 8 (exceptional). Rank 6 ≈ future Apprentice; rank 7 ≈ Master potential; rank 8 (Nathan) ≈ medium Master potential — strong enough to elevate a whole house. No one scored 7 in Nathan's year.

**The Power of Asura.** A rare spiritual magic invoked by speaking "Asura," capable of overpowering higher-level opponents who use weaker magic; accelerates leveling. Named techniques seen:
- **Asura Fist** — blast of blue energy from the knuckles.
- **Asura Eye** — vision overlay revealing emotions/motives/lies and warning of nearby killing-intent.
- **Razor Finger** — extends the cutting power of an object; first step toward the **Asura Slaying Blade**.
- **Asura Speed Slice** / **Asura Weakening Aura** — fast cutting strike + an aura that suppresses an enemy's spiritual energy (ep 61).

**Alchemy.** Nathan retains intuitive alchemy: **Dexterity Elixir** (temporary reflex/skill boost, fatigue after), healing **potion of internal healing**, etc. Ingredients named: Flaming Flower, Frozen Grass, Bitter Nut Milk, Ginger Root, Dragonweed, honey pepper thorns.

**Soul mechanic.** The resurrection (ep 1) is felt worldwide as a shockwave. Nathan can meditate into an "endless ocean of stars" of lost souls but cannot find his own origin (ep 9) — his pre-life is unknown.

## Geography (Kingdom of Calandria)
- **Dunshire** — Nathan's home hamlet in the fiefdom of **Godricsland**; overlooked by **Mount Dun(n)**, Nathan's training peak (ancient broken bridge/ruins).
- **Godricshall** — the great central city; keep called the **Owl's Eye**; the Academy of the Mystic Blades; heart of commerce among the four fiefdoms.
- **Calandria** spans (per Ulrich's map, ep 8): **Mount Cal** (W), **Haroldsland** (N, snowy — Hagar's home), **Howlsland/Howlsland** (S), **Skylarland** (E), **Godricsland** (center). Capital: **Calandriana**. Beyond: **Vasslan**, **Aeonia/Aeonian Universe** (the wider cosmos).

## Late-arc material (ep ~57–100 — tease-only in promos)
- **Dunshire raid & the fall of House Raven** (ep 57–61): House Raven besieges Dunshire; Old Billy's crypt last-stand; Nathan returns, slays Cedric then Remus, earns the epithet **"Demon Knight."** Ulrich kills Claudius Raven; loses an eye.
- **Duke Godricson arc** (ep 65–80): Nathan travels to the trial; humbles the Duke's spoiled grandson, then the Duke himself in a duel; gains noble steed **Ghost Walker**; allies with grandson **Alexander**.
- **Doom Faction Trial** (ep 65→): a survival contest open to warriors up to age 20; thousands compete; winner reportedly granted the title of **Duke** + a fiefdom. Nathan enters partly to hunt his mother's killer.
- **Cross-region "card" survival** (ep 83–95): contestants hold a personal playing card, accumulate points by collecting/winning cards from others, and "cross over" between regions via spiritual-energy portals overseen by a **Trial Judge**. Nathan crosses early and repeatedly, drawing the factions' attention.
- **Frozen Tundra / Howlslanders** (ep 96–100): a deadly cold region; brutish Howlslander warrior packs; Nathan falls gravely ill (fever) and is sheltered by **Artemis** (a steward's outcast son) in a canopy treehouse; **Halifax the Raven** appears (ep 96).

## Flavor / tone notes
- Register is heightened pseudo-Shakespearean in dialogue ("thee/thou," "I cry your mercy," "boon companion," "until satisfaction") against Nathan's dry modern interior monologue.
- Recurring motif: **the broken training sword** Nathan stabs into the ground as a monument to the boy he replaced (ep 2) — the only monument to the *original* Nathan.
- Family-hearth warmth (parties, parlor games, cake, Old Billy's flute) is deliberately woven between the cruelty as the emotional ballast.
