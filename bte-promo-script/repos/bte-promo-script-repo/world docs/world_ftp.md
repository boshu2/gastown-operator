# LAYER 0 — ESSENCE  ·  *the only default load*

## Three Pillars (locked)
1. **Heir-to-Slave Reversal Promise** *(Thruline)* — the Young Master made a human punching bag who turns the abuse into power.
2. **Family-Betrayal Class Hierarchy** — Great Houses, branches, and Sect ranks where the strong own the weak.
3. **The Secret Weapon-Body (Godforge)** — getting beaten makes him stronger; no one knows why.

## Protagonist — Zane Lowell
**Identity.** Former Young Master of House Lowell — a fourteen-year-old generational prodigy (peak Strength Level) until his father Milo was poisoned by his brothers and the surviving branches framed the family as traitors. For two years he's been a shackled slave, the "human punching bag" for the Lowell children who once bowed to him. Engine is **Underdog Awakening**: the night he burns the Codex of Ascension, a hidden gold script grants the **Godforge Technique** — every blow that should break him forges him stronger. His sister Elara, imprisoned on Infernal Peak, drives every choice.
**Combat.** His body IS the weapon. Skin deflects blades, bones denser than steel; ordinary attacks can't hurt him and instead purge impurities and advance his cultivation. He fights with the Purple Flash Fist (Shadow Fist — one strike, multiple internal ruptures) on precise vital points, and must constantly **fake injury** so no one learns the truth.
**Power Source.** Godforge: "Transform flesh into forge, body into weapon. Purify the vessel through suffering" (Ep 4). Being beaten = taking an Aether Crystal; impurities flush a thousand times faster than normal cultivation. Hard limit: it scales with the *force* hitting him — once his body outgrows an attacker, weak blows do nothing; he needs stronger opponents to advance. Ladder: Skin → Strength → Bone Refining → Core Strength → Marrow Refining, then the Nature Level.
**Voice.** Cold, patient, internally narrated; rage that "froze" rather than flared. Wry and sardonic — savors beatings while pretending to suffer. Highborn dignity even in shackles. Vengeance-driven but principled: invokes "the rules" against those who abuse them.

**Voice Samples (verbatim — never paraphrase):**
1. "Repaying cruelty with kindness... Such nobility delivered my father to his grave." (Ep 1) — bitter, disillusioned
2. "My fist is stronger than yours, so I'm above you... Respect the person, not the position." (Ep 8) — code, defiant
3. "I ate it." (Ep 25) — deadpan, on the Hellfire Blade
4. "Let him come. I need the practice." (Ep 9) — cold, on Andrew's revenge
5. "Just let me die!" (Ep 4) — internal, mid-forging agony

**Drift tripwires.** Visibly wins through brute strength | shows his body's secret to anyone | is seriously harmed by ordinary attacks | rescues Elara | becomes a confident swaggering hero | loses the fake-injury concealment | resolves the dagger / furnace / ring secrets.

## Setting — Entry-Window World (House Lowell, Castermere County)
**Feel.** A damp cellar where a former heir scratches tallies counting the days since his father's murder. Above it: the marble-and-basalt Lowell Training Hall, an open-air arena where ten-year-olds in gray robes beat shackled, leather-armored slaves as "live targets" — many maimed or killed. A decaying Great House: rich, comfortable, rotting from within, its two stolen Aether Crystals and lost Greenwind Blade the markers of a glory it betrayed.

**Sensory Samples (verbatim):**
1. "Zane Lowell etched another tally into the damp cellar wall—seven hundred and thirty days since his father's assassination." (Ep 1)
2. "The hall quickly filled with the sickening thud of fists on flesh and the pathetic whimpers of men begging for mercy. To the Lowell kids, these slaves weren't people; they were objects." (Ep 1)
3. "Cracks began to spiderweb across his skin, a dark red light glowing from within, like he was a piece of red-hot iron." (Ep 4)

**Drift tripwires.** Lowell estate as warm/noble | beatings as sport rather than lethal injustice | slavery softened | the Capital/Sect anchoring the opening (that's the second phase).

## Tone Constraint
Underdog *injustice-into-power* drama, not a flex. The beatings must read as a child tortured by his own family — the strengthening is Zane's secret, hidden under faked agony. If a beat lets him visibly dominate or openly reveal his power, empathy collapses into power fantasy.

# LAYER 1 — ACTIVATION CANON  ·  *load at W1 + canon gate*

## First 5 minutes  *(the 50%-drop gate)*
Zane tallies 730 days since his father's assassination, planning vengeance. Hauled in shackles to the Training Hall, he's shoved into the slave line — leather-armored "punching bags" for Lowell children. His cousin Perrin, the new Young Master, arrives flaunting a stolen Aether Crystal ("that was just from one-tenth of its power"), kills a begging slave with one punch, then twists the knife: "I heard our dear cousin, Elara... Got herself sent to Infernal Peak." The family's former pride, now property, learning his sister is condemned — and unable to do a thing.

## First 5 episodes  *(the first sitting)*
In despair, Zane burns the Codex of Ascension — and a hidden gold script erupts from the ashes into his flesh: the **Godforge Technique**. A nine-dragon furnace and black flame forge his soul and body in agony. Next day, Melvin's brutal punch produces not pain but *warmth* — impurities purging, cultivation surging. Zane realizes: **being beaten makes him stronger.** He volunteers for beatings, fakes injury to hide it, and rockets from peak Strength to Bone Refining in days.

## First 20 episodes  *(texture band)*
Zane secretly trains the Purple Flash Fist (Shadow Fist) at night; thrashes the stewards Grey Hawn and Darren for breaking House rules; the elders (uncle Owen, who poisoned Milo) plot his death. On Family Training Day, his cousin Andrew — wielding the second stolen Aether Crystal — calls him to a Fatal Duel and unleashes a lethal Talisman weapon; Zane survives untouched and ends Andrew with "One Punch" (Ep 16), crippling his cultivation. The arc builds toward escaping House Lowell to reach the Skyreach Sect and save Elara.

## HARD BLOCKS  *(opening-binding)*
1. **Zane's body IS a weapon (Godforge Technique) — literal, not metaphor.** Skin deflects blades, bones denser than steel; he gets STRONGER by being beaten (each blow purges impurities). Never show him seriously harmed by ordinary attacks.
2. **The Godforge Technique is absolutely secret.** No one knows his body is a mystical weapon. Observers note a "bizarre physique" / impossible toughness but have no explanation; Zane must fake injury to conceal it.
3. **The Cobalt Dragon / nine-dragon Furnace / Dragon Scale system is secret.** Black flame, the furnace forging his soul, Celestial Essence production — none of it is known to anyone.
4. **The Shattered Dagger's true nature is unknown.** Appears worthless/battered but "could slice through anything"; found in Evil Jace's Storage Ring. Do not resolve the dagger's nature.
5. **Elara is NOT rescued by Ep 100.** Imprisoned on Infernal Peak; Zane has NOT freed her. The rescue is his driving goal but is NOT accomplished.
6. **Zane's father Milo is confirmed dead** (poisoned by his brothers — Owen's role explicit). Not a mystery. The Greenwind Blade (Milo's legendary sword) is missing/hidden, not recovered.
7. **The Codex of Ascension is destroyed.** The gold script was a one-time event, absorbed into Zane. Cannot recur.
8. **The Storage Ring is secret.** Legendarily rare (pocket dimension); Zane uses it discreetly.

## Engines the opening supports
1. **Underdog Awakening (UNTESTED — STRONGEST):** enslaved former heir who gets stronger from being beaten; every win looks impossible from outside.
2. **Secret Power (UNTESTED — VERY STRONG):** five stacked secrets (weapon-body, furnace, dagger, ring, soul-forge) no one can see; concealment-vs-power is the loop.
3. **Institutional Injustice (UNTESTED — VERY STRONG):** Great Houses monopolize crystals, blades, Sect access; class hierarchy at every level.
4. **Bond / Family Loyalty (UNTESTED — STRONG):** Elara on Infernal Peak drives every decision; never reached.

## IM sources — opening window
- **Perrin flaunts the stolen Aether Crystal that should have been Zane's** (Ep 2): birthright theft. *Institutional Injustice, T2*
- **Perrin kills a begging slave with one punch** (Ep 2): casual murder of the powerless. *Status Destruction, T2*

**Injustice density** *(primary conversion driver):*
- **T1 (mandatory):** a generational-prodigy heir was made his own family's human punching bag after his father was murdered and framed by his uncles — beaten daily by the children who once groveled at his feet, while his sister rots on Infernal Peak.
- **T2 (supporting):** his stolen birthright (the Aether Crystal) now powers the talentless cousin who lords it over him; slaves are killed for sport.
- **T3 (deepening):** even servants and stewards (Grey, Darren) now bully and try to trade away the former Young Master; the House that betrayed his father grows fat on the theft.
- **Drift risk:** if a promo leads with the cool weapon-body / "gets stronger from beatings" gimmick and skips the family murder + daily humiliation + Elara, it reads as a flex, not an injustice, and empathy collapses.

# LAYER 2 — RAILS (eps ~20–100)

## 2a · Conversion Threads
1. **Rescue Elara from Infernal Peak** — driving goal from Ep 2; still unaccomplished at Ep 100. Sell the vow and the closing distance; never deliver the reunion (she's glimpsed, never freed).
2. **Avenge Milo / reclaim House Lowell** — the uncles (Owen) murdered his father and stole the Crystals + Greenwind Blade. Initiate the reckoning; the full purge is unresolved.
3. **The Shattered Dagger's hidden power** — a battered knife from Jace's ring that "could slice through anything." Tease its mystery; never explain it.
4. **Lighting the Dragon Scales / the Furnace's ceiling** — one Legendary weapon barely lights a single Cobalt Dragon scale; thousands remain across nine dragons. Sell the vastness of the climb, never the summit.
5. **Celestial Essence — his Black Fire secretly makes the rarest substance in the Empire** — Great Houses bid fortunes for a trace (Ep 99–100); Zane produces it as a byproduct. Tease the implication; withhold what it means for him.

## 2b · Canon Limits / Never-Happens
- **The show never lets an ordinary attack seriously hurt Zane** — blows forge him; pain is faked for cover.
- **The show never reveals the Godforge/furnace/dagger/ring secrets to other characters** — observers only ever sense a "bizarre physique" with no explanation.
- **The show never frees Elara** — Infernal Peak is the unreached destination through Ep 100.
- **The show never resolves the dagger or the Greenwind Blade** — both stay open.
- **Cultivation is body-refining, not spellcasting** — power is forged flesh, vital-point strikes, and martial forms (Purple Flash / Shadow Fist), not magic.
