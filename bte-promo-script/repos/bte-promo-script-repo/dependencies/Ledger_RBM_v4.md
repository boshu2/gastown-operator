## 1. THE MASTER ARC REPORT

**PUBLIC PRICING — PRESENT, strong. Device variant** (star-rating in a newborn beast's eyes + the public selection square).

The instrument, stated: *"The stars in the eyes of newborn life bound beasts show their potential"* (Jasper, Ep 3) and the verdict it returns: *"His heart sank when he saw a single star."* / *"A one star beast was weak. Common."* (Ep 3). The public pricing rite is Eastwind Square, Ep 12 — Albie invites the measurement (*"Well then, let's see it. If you're this confident, it must be something special. Six star? Seven star?"*), Jasper summons a tiny yellow chick, and the square prices him to laughter: *"He's brought a bloody chick!"* (crowd, Ep 12); *"That thing isn't even enough for a starter course, and you made it your lifebound beast?"* (Albie, Ep 12). Even the dignitaries on the spire laugh.

**RE-PRICING — PRESENT but demonstration-only, and deliberately undercut.**

Laughter becomes silence twice at the same instrument: *"The laughter vanished. A chill swept through the crowd."* (Ep 12) and *"No one laughed now. The square had fallen completely silent."* (Ep 12), capped by the authority chorus *"How is that possible? Albie's at the seventh level of the Beastblood Tier. That outcast shouldn't be able to touch him!"* (dignitary, Ep 13) and the crowd flip *"He's got nerve, I'll give him that. The lad's got spine."* (Ep 14).

**Authorship note for W3:** the pricing *instrument itself* never re-measures him in public. The device-level re-pricing image exists — the single star splitting into *"one star became ten. Ten became a hundred. A thousand"* until Jasper is *"lost in a vast starscape"* (Ep 3) — but it happens in private, at hatching. The world still reads "one star" at Ep 20. That gap is the promo's loaded gun: W3 can stage the private starscape against the public laughter. The source immediately re-flips the board (Euan, Eps 15–16): the re-pricing is paid for with a fresh, deeper pricing and a one-month clock — a wager, not a coronation. **The promo arc should end on the wager, not on false triumph: PRICING → false RE-PRICING → THE WAGER.**

## 5. THE THIN-SLOT REPORT (authorship map)

- **SLOT 9 — COST-OF-BEING-RIGHT: effectively EMPTY.** Nearest ore is narrated and oblique: Jasper "had told his father everything, how he had been framed... Archie's answer had been silence" (Ep 14). No scene punishes him *for being correct*. W3 must author this — cleanest seam is the three-year gap (right about Lydia/Redfern, believed by no one, priced as the attacker).
- **SLOT 21 — THE RECOGNITION DROP: MISSING.** No gatekeeper collapses into deference; nobody yet knows what a Primordial Chaos Beast is, so there is no instrument that CAN recognize him. The public star-count still reads one. This is the show's withheld payoff — W3 authors the drop as promise, not event (the private starscape of Ep 3 is the verbatim anchor for what recognition will look like). Mini-drops as texture: "It can talk?" (Sophie, Ep 5); rooms freezing when the chick speaks.
- **SLOT 22 — DEATHBED CHARGE: variant only.** Freya delivers charge-grade lines while dying slowly, but there is no deathbed scene and no interrupted word.
- **SLOT 3 — SACRIFICE LINE: partial.** No concrete family-cost line. The Midas self-sacrifice covers the emotional function; the *material* family sacrifice is absent.
- **SLOT 15 — DEATH CLOCK: present but unspoken.** "Months left at best" and the dwindling clear spirit grass are narrated, never said aloud. W3 will likely need to voice the clock. The one-month wager clock IS spoken and carries well.
- **SLOT 17 — CONCEALMENT TAX: weak.** Jasper conceals but never absorbs public humiliation *to protect* the secret. The RD-lane engine is not this show's engine; don't force it.
- **IMAGE CLASS H — DESTROYED TOKEN: thin.** The hairpin's fate after the betrayal is unstated — a loaded object the source leaves on the table; ask the operator whether to author its return/destruction.
- **Structural:** build the promo master arc PRICING → false RE-PRICING → THE WAGER, which the source supports verbatim end to end.
