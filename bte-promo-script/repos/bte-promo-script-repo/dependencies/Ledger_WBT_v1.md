## VARIANT-CANON NOTES (declare one continuity per promo — mixing is a defect)

- **Chief bully:** canon eps use **Jin** (spirit tiger) with unnamed scale-boy and eagle-girl; Five Exams promotes **Klein Goldcrest** (canon Ep 1 has him only as the ceremony mocker) to lead with **Kai** as enforcer. Both are proven — pick one pair and hold it.
- **Klein's beast:** Golden Lion in Five Exams; canon Ep 1 gives the Crimson Salamander to **Ron Blackfire**; SSS Dragons hands it to Klein. The Golden-Lion-Klein is promo-native.
- **Family:** canon = both parents alive, cooks, sixty, mature Iron plants, leaf-and-vine hair. UK Cold Open kills the father and adds landlord **Garrick** menacing the mother — a market variant; its lines are PROVEN for that continuity only.
- **The power:** canon's knowledge flood comes from completing the two-half medicine (father's male plant → his birth; the female plant found at ten). SSS/Egg Boy compress this to a single "half-flower" trip. Either compression is proven; the *knowledge-not-strength* result is invariant.
- **Register:** UK Cold Open runs UK-idiom ("mummy," "skint," "bruv") — do not import into non-UK builds.
- **Timeline:** canon ceremony is at age ten with school at ten; Five Exams runs an academy exam arc; Egg Boy moves the hatch to the academy gates. The hatch-moment is movable; the mockery that follows it is not.
