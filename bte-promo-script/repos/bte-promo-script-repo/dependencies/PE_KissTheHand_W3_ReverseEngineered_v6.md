**CAST** — EBONY: a princess trained to manage men politely, running out of politeness. NOVA: the thousand-year dragon inside her — part weapon, part conscience; begs to be let out and lectures her about cruelty in the same hour. KING LIAM: a father who cares what things look like, not what they are; hard in public, and twice — almost invisibly — not. EDWIN: fourteen, their father's worst beliefs in a smaller body. BERIOD: an old king who likes that she can't refuse him. GREYSON: the one man in the room not appraising her. RADION: the ancient dragon inside Greyson — Nova's mate; patient, absolute. GERALT: the captain who never stopped serving her. THE COURT / THE CROWD: Allarion itself — it blames the woman.

---

### 1 · THE BURNING — castle courtyard, ten years ago

**MB1** — They chained a woman in irons in the middle of the castle courtyard, and the crowd screamed for the fire — **"Burn her! Dragon witch!"** The woman had made one mistake. Having a dragon — a privilege this kingdom reserved for men. Fourteen-year-old Ebony watched it from beside her father, the King.

**MB2** — **"Let this be a lesson to every woman in Allarion."** Ebony pulled at his sleeve — **"Father, please, let her go!"** — **"Ebony, it is the law of this Kingdom."** She tried to look away. He made her watch as they burned the woman alive. And when the fire was done, he bent to his daughter: **"Never forget, even as a Princess… you obey men."**

### 2 · GOOD BONES — the throne room

**MB3** — Ebony was made to meet King Beriod — a potential bride; he was old enough to be her grandfather. He studied her face and tilted her chin with one finger. **"She'll do. Good bones. She'll fill out."** His hand moved toward her hip.

**MB4** — Ebony slapped it away. **"Don't touch me! I don't belong to you!"** The throne room went dead quiet.

**MB5** — Her father grabbed her arm hard enough to bruise. **"Apologize."** — **"He was going to grab me."** — **"Apologize. Now."** She shook her head. **"Now, girl!"** For refusing, she was locked in a pantry for a week. In this Kingdom, girls didn't fight back. They disappeared.

### 3 · THE SCROLL — the study door, then the old archive, night

**MB6** — Then one evening, her father's voice came through the study door: **"I would rather see a beggar sit on my throne than any woman. Even my own daughter."** But Ebony had heard a different story — whispers from her mother, a prophecy that she would one day be Queen. That night she went looking for proof.

**MB7** — She tore through the scrolls and tomes of the old archive. Nothing. Then, behind a rotting tapestry, the wall began to rotate — a hidden room, scrolls everywhere, and one glowing on a pedestal. The parchment was warm in her hands: *The Princess of Allarion who inherits a dragon will become Queen of two realms.*

**MB8** — The scroll came alive. Light poured from the letters, and something inside Ebony's chest answered it — heat flooding through her, building, swelling, too much to hold. The scroll fell from her hands. Black scales tore through her arms as her eyes went black, and then she saw her — a dragon, ancient and enormous. **"I choose you."** The voice shook the chamber; dust fell from the ceiling. **"I am Nova. I have waited centuries for you, Ebony. And every word on that scroll is true."**

**MB9** — A dragon older than every bloodline in the kingdom had chosen her — in a country where a woman hiding a dragon was burned alive. Her own father had signed that law. **"And if my father finds out… he'll be the one who kills me."**

### 4 · THE ULTIMATUM — the castle library, years later, dawn

**MB10** — Years later. The door slammed. **"You will choose a husband tonight. Refuse — and I will drag you to the castle gates and marry you to the first man who walks through them."**

**MB11** — Heat ripped up Ebony's spine; inside her, Nova clawed to the surface. **"Say the word, Ebony. And I will burn him where he stands."** Four years she had kept Nova secret, and sometimes the secret threatened to explode on its own. **"Your eyes, Ebony. They're shifting."** Silver bleeding to black — the one thing she could never control.

**MB12** — Tonight, every prince in the realm would arrive with a dragon of his own. If even one of them sensed Nova, Ebony would be dead before morning. **"Well?"** She forced the heat down. Swallowed the fire. And chose the most dangerous room in Allarion. **"I'll go to the ball, Father."**

**MB13** — Alone, she pressed her palms against the cold library table and waited for the heat to pass. Nova paced inside her mind like a caged beast. **"One day, Ebony. One day you will stop bowing to that man."** — **"One day. Not today."**

### 5 · LIVESTOCK — the corridor, morning

**MB14** — She hadn't made it ten paces before the voice she hated most found her. **"How does it feel? Being auctioned off like livestock?"** Edwin leaned against a pillar, arms crossed, grinning — fourteen years old, already a mirror of their father's worst beliefs. **"Princes from every corner of the realm. All here to bid on you. How thrilling."** — **"You sound eager to see me gone."** — **"Everyone is eager. Once you're married off, Allarion can finally stop pretending you matter."**

**MB15** — She cut him back: **"And once I'm gone, you'll be the most disappointing person in this castle all by yourself. At least now you have competition."** His grin faltered. And Nova stirred: **"Cruelty makes you no different from them, Ebony."** Ebony ignored the lecture — not because Nova was wrong, but because kindness in this castle was a luxury that got you killed.

**MB16** — Edwin recovered, leaned close, dropped his voice. **"Oh — I almost forgot. Father's invited King Beriod to sit at the head table tonight. Right beside you. I hear he's been asking about you. Specifically."** He walked off whistling, and the name kept ringing in her skull — the man who had tried to buy her at fourteen, waiting for her at the ball. **"I do not like this, Ebony."** — **"Neither do I."**

### 6 · ARMOR — the castle ballroom, evening

**MB17** — She wore the red dress her mother had made her. On the night her father was selling her off, her mother made her look like a queen — black diamond tiara and all. Tonight, it felt like armor.

**MB18** — Ebony stepped into the ballroom — and felt them. Dragons. Dozens of them, inside dozens of princes, their awareness pressing against her like wolves circling prey. Nova shrank inside her. **"Too many. If even one senses me—"** — **"I know."**

**MB19** — Her father was waiting at the top of the stairs. He gripped her arm before she'd taken two steps. **"Stand up straight, Ebony. Your suitors are watching."** Not *are you alright*. Not *you look beautiful*. Her suitors — as if she were a horse being trotted past buyers. She straightened. She descended.

**MB20** — Halfway down, Edwin materialized beside her. **"Is that mother's dress? How sweet. She must have wanted you to feel special before they carted you off."** A knot of princes at the foot of the stairs glanced up, laughing: **"That's the one? She's the reason we traveled three kingdoms?"** Edwin grinned — **"All that effort, and still they're unimpressed."** — and Nova held her steady: **"Breathe. If your eyes shift here, we are dead."**

**MB21** — At the bottom step, Captain Geralt moved to her side. His hand rose — steady, careful — and adjusted the black diamond tiara where it had shifted. **"You look wonderful, Princess. Tonight is your night."**

**MB22** — She stepped deeper into the ballroom — and froze. A dark-haired prince she didn't recognize. The only man in the room who wasn't laughing, appraising, or looking away. The rest of the room disappeared. And Nova went silent.

**MB23** — Across the ballroom, something stirred inside Prince Greyson of Piroria. **"Those eyes."** — **"What about them?"** — **"I would recognize them anywhere. She carries Nova. My mate. Bonded to me centuries before either of you were born. And because our dragons are true mates, Greyson — so are you. Princess Ebony is your mate."** Their eyes locked across the room; she looked away first, flushed, and disappeared into the crowd. **"Do not approach. Her dragon is hiding. If we move too soon, we expose her… We are not leaving this kingdom without her."**

### 7 · THE DANCE

**MB24** — A courtier clinked his glass — **"Let the dances begin!"** — and to her disgust, the hand that claimed her was Beriod's. She had no choice but to take it. They danced. His hand settled on the small of her back. Then it slid lower. She kept her voice measured — the voice of a princess trained to navigate men like this without causing a scene. **"Your hand, sir… would you mind?"** Beriod smiled, and pressed it firmer. **"I like how uncomfortable you're getting. It's cute."**

**MB25** — Nova erupted — heat ripping up her spine, black creeping into her vision, scales forming beneath the skin of her forearms. **"Let me OUT. I will melt the flesh from his bones—"** Ebony's voice came out louder than she intended, sharp enough to cut through the music: **"I said — let me GO!"**

**MB26** — The song ended. The room turned. And every eye landed on Ebony — not on the man whose hands had been crawling over her. Nobles whispered behind fans; women shook their heads. Beriod straightened his coat and walked off with a disgusted look, and the court nodded him past. Nobody looked at Ebony with sympathy. She was the problem.

**MB27** — Something inside her cracked. Done hiding. Done being touched by men the system protected and then blamed for objecting. Her hands were heating, scales rippling under her skin — seconds from shifting in front of every prince in the realm. Then a hand caught her wrist — firm, not rough. Greyson pulled her toward him as the next song began, and before she could speak: **"I'm sorry that happened."** Four words no man in Allarion had ever said to her.

**MB28** — The dance carried them forward, his hand at her waist — correct, respectful, nothing like Beriod's grip. And she couldn't stop the questions. **"Is it true? That Pirorian women train for combat?"** — **"It's true. Women serve in the military. Hold government positions. Train alongside men."** — **"As equals?"** — **"As equals."** The word hung between them. A concept that didn't exist in Allarion. Something cracked open inside her — not Nova, not fire. Something older. Hope.

**MB29** — She went further than was safe. **"I believe women should rule."** The music wavered; his eyes widened. **"There's a prophecy. Spoken at my birth. It says I will be queen. That I'll rule two nations as one. And I intend to make it happen."** — **"You're telling me this? Here? Now? Please — keep your voice down—"** And she laughed. Not bitterly. Worse — with the exhaustion of someone who had already lost. **"What are they going to do? Sell me to the highest bidder? They're already doing that. Marry me off to a stranger? That's tonight's plan. The only thing left they can take from me is my life — and most days, I'm not sure that's even mine to keep."**

**MB30** — The music stopped. Ebony stood in the center of the ballroom with tears running down her face — not sobbing, not dramatic. Just sad, crying quietly in front of everyone. The court stared. Greyson gripped her hand tighter. **"Ebony… your father."**

### 8 · KISS MY HAND

**MB31** — King Liam crossed the floor and took her by the arm, his grip iron, his voice low enough that only she could hear. **"You're crying. In front of every royal family in the realm. Where is your decorum?"** Not *what did you say*. Not *what did you tell the Pirorian*. He hadn't heard a word. He didn't care what she felt — he cared what it looked like. He steered her toward the throne dais, Edwin watching from the corner with a cup in his hand, Geralt tensing at his post — and extended his hand. Palm up. The command was ancient. Public. Absolute. **"Kiss my hand. Then go back to the Pirorian. Smile. Dance. And obey — the way a princess should."**

**MB32** — Ebony stared at the hand. She thought of Beriod's grip on her waist. Edwin's smirk. The hundred men who had watched her be groped and said nothing. She thought of Greyson — the one person who had said he was sorry. And Nova, for the first time in four years, was not surging. Not begging. Waiting. **"Whatever you decide, Ebony. I am with you."** Thirty years of rule. Nobody had ever left that hand unanswered. Ebony stepped away from her father. The room went dead silent. The hand stayed extended. Unanswered. The King of Allarion stood before every royal family in the realm — and his daughter walked away from him.

### 9 · THE SCREAM

**MB33** — Then Nova erupted — and Ebony didn't fight her. She fed her. Four years of hiding. Four years of swallowing fire so this kingdom wouldn't kill her for what she was. Four years of bowing while being the most powerful being in the room. It all tore loose. The scream hit every dragon in the ballroom at a frequency that dropped men to their knees. Princes collapsed, goblets shattered, and the chandeliers swung. **"There you are, Princess Ebony."**

**MB34** — She moved through the stunned ballroom — and the room parted. Not because she pushed. Because the dragons inside every prince were screaming at their humans to get away from her. Prince Tye, who had mocked her for years, was on his knees first. **"Stand up."** He couldn't.

**MB35** — She stopped in front of King Beriod. This time, he didn't smile. **"I will never be yours. You disgusting old man."** She didn't touch him. She didn't need to. His glass shattered in his hand.

**MB36** — Then the guards came — the only men in the room still standing, blades drawn. And Geralt stepped between them and her. **"Put your weapons down, she is a Princess of this Kingdom!"**

**MB37** — In the middle of the wreckage, Greyson stood perfectly still. Something shimmered around him — faint, crystalline, like ice catching light, visible only to her. Two words, quiet enough that only she could hear: **"Pick me."** The words she wanted to scream — *I will not have you* — caught in her throat. Her mouth opened. Nothing came out. The mate bond physically would not let her reject him. **"Ebony… please."** She ran — past the shattered goblets, the bleeding princes, the stunned court. Behind her, Greyson didn't follow. **"She'll understand. Eventually."** — **"And if she doesn't?"** — **"Then I'll make sure she survives long enough to learn."**

### 10 · THE AUCTION

**MB38** — King Liam stood before what remained of his court. His daughter had committed treason against every visiting kingdom — alliances shattered, reparations demanded — and he would fix it the only way the law allowed. **"My daughter is no longer under my protection. Any man who will take responsibility for her may claim her as his wife. One hundred thousand silver."** Silence. **"Fifty thousand gold."** Stone silence — Ebony at the edge of the room, guards gripping her arms, watching her father sell her. A prince near the front raised his hand — then sat down as if shoved by something no one could see, his mouth opening on nothing. Because Radion was already inside him. Inside all of them. **"None of you will claim her. Not one."** — **"Seventy thousand gold!"** Nothing.

**MB39** — **"Very well. Bring her before the Council."** — **"Father, please!"** The guards seized her arms; she dug her heels into the stone; they shoved her forward. She hit the ground hard, and her dress tore at the knee. **"For once, just do as you're told, girl!"** Girl. Not Ebony. Not daughter. Not princess. Girl.

### 11 · THE VOTE — the council chamber, night

**MB40** — **"Nova… they're going to kill me. We have to do something."** — **"I need your help, child. I can reach into a dragon's mind… but only one. Listen to me. You are a princess. You are MY princess. You know these men — choose the weakest, and focus everything you have on him."**

**MB41** — Five councilmen voted, one by one. **"Death."** Ebony flinched — **"Do not get distracted."** — **"Death."** — **"Push, Ebony. Focus only on the one."** — **"Death."** — **"I can do this."** — **"Death."** She remembered Beriod's hand on her waist, Edwin's cocky smile, the greedy faces of the princes. She would not allow a man to decide her death. Not today. Not ever. **"Death."** Five votes. One left.

**MB42** — Lord Lyndon, the sixth councilman, stood — and Ebony pushed everything she had at one man. His mouth worked. His jaw trembled. His eyes bulged, as though something invisible had him by the throat. Because something did. **"Duh… duh… ba… ba-banishment."** The word fell into the room like a stone into water. Ebony collapsed to her knees. And King Liam let a breath escape — shoulders dropping, eyes closing for half a second. Relief. He didn't want his daughter dead. He just couldn't save her inside a system he'd built. He hid it immediately, straightening and hardening. **"The sentence is banishment. Effective immediately."**

### 12 · STONES — the castle streets, night

**MB43** — Geralt walked beside her. Title stripped. Dress stained. Tiara gone, and her name gone with it. The mob pressed in from both sides, pelting her with rotten tomatoes and stones. The words she'd heard whispered behind doors her whole life were screamed in the open now: **"Cursed!" "Witch!" "Monster!"** Then a woman pushed through the crowd — not throwing food, shaking with rage, the bruises around her eye fresh and purple and swollen shut. **"My husband beat me because of YOU! Because of what you did at the ball! He said if a princess can't behave, his wife sure as hell better!"** A stone caught Ebony in the shoulder. The realization hit harder: her rampage hadn't hurt the princes — they'd gone home with bruised egos; it was the women who paid. The system didn't punish the powerful — it punished the powerless.

**MB44** — Another stone. Her knee buckled; she went down, palms on cold cobblestone, the mob closing in above her. A hand reached down. Geralt. He pulled her to her feet and stepped in front of her — not with his weapon drawn, but with his armored back. Then he leaned close, close enough that only she could hear. **"You will always be Princess of Allarion to me."** He pressed his fist to his chest: the Knight's Guard salute, reserved for royalty. She didn't have a title anymore. He gave her one anyway. Her throat closed; she nodded once, and kept walking.

### 13 · BORIS — the castle gates, night

**MB45** — The gates stood open. She heard him before she saw him — a shape emerging from the dark road, hunched, limping into the torchlight. Matted hair in greasy clumps. Teeth blackened and broken. A smell that made her eyes water from ten feet. A guard stepped forward — **"State your name and business."** — and the man's eyes, dark and calm, found Ebony through the torchlight. **"Boris. I heard there was a woman in need of a husband."** Just Boris. No family name. No title. No land. **"No."** — **"Ebony…"** — **"Look at him, Nova. Look at him."** — **"I see him. And you don't have a choice."** She turned to her father — **"Father, I promise… I will do whatever it takes."** — and his jaw tightened. **"It is too late, Ebony… it is done."** He took her hand, his grip firm and his fingers trembling, and walked her to the officiant.

**MB46** — The officiant unrolled a scroll and did not look at her. **"Under the law of Allarion, this woman is bound to this man in the sight of the King and the realm. She will bear his name. She will bear his children. She will not speak in council. She will not hold property. She will not leave his home without permission."** Not even an ancient dragon could burn laws from the scroll. Ebony's hands shook; she pressed them together to stop it. **"Do you submit to these bindings for the rest of your natural life?"**

**MB47** — The courtyard was quiet — no jeering now. She thought of the bruised woman, the stone, what defiance cost the women of this kingdom. Her father was looking away; his hand rose, quick, almost invisible, and wiped a tear from his eye. **"I will do as best I can."** Not *I do*. Not submission. Not surrender. But close enough for the law to accept. **"It is done. Close the gates when they leave."** Boris turned for the road — **"We need to move. It isn't safe to stay near these walls."** — and Ebony walked out of her city without looking back. The gates groaned shut behind them.

### 14 · THE TRAIL — mountain road, night

**MB48** — They walked in silence, Boris ahead, the castle shrinking to a smudge of torchlight against the dark. Then, slowly, she noticed it. The hunch was gone — not all at once, but as if each step away from the kingdom peeled something off of him. His shoulders rolled back. The shuffling limp smoothed into a steady, even stride. The body beneath the rags wasn't starved — it was muscular, built by training. And his eyes moved constantly, scanning the tree line, reading the terrain like a soldier cataloguing threats.

**MB49** — Ebony opened her mouth — *who are you?* — and the words died in her throat, because she felt something that stopped her cold. A presence, from inside him — something ancient, something powerful. A dragon. **"That's not possible. He's a beggar. Beggars don't have dragons."** — **"Nova — I think he has a—"** — **"His dragon… Ebony! I know his dragon."**
