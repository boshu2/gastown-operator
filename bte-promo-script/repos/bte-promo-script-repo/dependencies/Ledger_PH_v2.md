## 1. THE MASTER ARC REPORT

**Beat 1 — PUBLIC PRICING: PRESENT BUT WEAK (device variant only; no laughter instrument).** The source prices Jake at the bottom, but *privately and administratively*, not publicly to mockery. Best candidates:

- Device: **"Human (G) – The lowest level of humans in the system."** (SYSTEM, Ch 2) — the system literally names him lowest on first contact; his opening status screen shows single-digit stats across the board (Str 7, Willpower 6, Ch 2).
- Device (self-priced): **"He wasn't an extraordinary person, but just another face in the crowd."** (narration, Ch 1 office scene) — the pricing is anonymity, not ridicule.
- Social pricing inversion: the group prices him as *dangerous*, not worthless — **"What the fuck is wrong with you?"** (Jacob, Ch 6) and **"But to just murder someone…"** (Caroline, Ch 10).

**No gatekeeper variant, no rite variant, and critically NO public laughter beat exists in this span.** Nobody laughs at Jake; the world simply doesn't see him. If the promo needs the crowd-laughs-then-goes-silent engine, the laughter half is **W3 authorship territory** (the raw material — his mediocre status screen, the "lowest level of human" tag, Richard not even noticing him — is harvested below).

**Beat 2 — RE-PRICING: PRESENT AND STRONG (two instruments).**

- Device: **"*Bloodline Awakened*: [Bloodline of the Primal Hunter (Bloodline Ability - Unique)] – Dormant power lies in the very essence of your being. A unique, innate ability awakened in the Bloodline of the newly initiated human, Jake Thayne."** (SYSTEM, Ch 9) — the same system that called him "lowest level of human" now writes his personal name into a Unique ability. The classification-cycling beat, native.
- Human instrument: the Richard standoff (Ch 12) — the veteran leader ends it by raising a hand to stop his own bowman, and **"Jake made the exact same motion. The other men appeared stunned by the revelation that he could still see them with his back turned."** Plus the two-word verdict: **"What… what happened?" / "I won."** (Ch 8).

**Arc verdict:** re-pricing rich, pricing thin. The show's native arc is INVISIBLE → FEARED rather than MOCKED → WORSHIPPED. W3 must either author a public pricing scene or lean the promo on the fear-arc.
