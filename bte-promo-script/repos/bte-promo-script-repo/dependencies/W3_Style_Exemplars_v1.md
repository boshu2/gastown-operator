# W3 STYLE EXEMPLARS · v1 — five ideal microbeats, mined from proven scripts

*This file replaces cadence rules as the driver of W3 narration. Each sample below is a winner's own moment folded into microbeat form — 3P past, lines verbatim and bold, apparatus stripped — with the winner's sentences kept as close to verbatim as the form allows. The microbeat imitates THESE. The lint rules (no 40-word sentences, no "the way you'd…" narration scaffolds, no five short sentences in a row) survive only as tripwires, not as targets.*

*Why this exists: v2 narration failed purple (word budgets read as quotas), v4 failed staccato (a sentence cap read as a metronome). Both were rules driving prose. The proven register is rhythm — measured across these five samples: sentences swing 2–28 words; the staccato is SAVED for a single punch — **isolate ONE punch and let the rest flow**; a run of stacked short sentences stilts even at three, so keep one punch and connect the surrounding beats into motion (12–22-word sentences); motion rides 12–22-word sentences; rarely more than two narration sentences sit between voiced lines in a confrontation; and one short, plain verdict-sentence is allowed at the punch ("She was the problem.") — never a clause riding on an action.*

## SAMPLE 1 — THE SILENCE-PEAK · from Princess Ebony (operator-written)
*What it teaches: the public command answered with silence; narration that counts the cost in memories, then steps aside; the punch carried by three fragments and one long final sentence.*

**MB** — Every eye was on them as King Liam extended his hand. Palm up. The command was ancient. Public. Absolute. **"Kiss my hand. Then go back to the Pirorian. Smile. Dance. And obey — the way a princess should."** Ebony stared at the hand. She thought of Beriod's grip on her waist, of Edwin's smirk, of the hundred men who had watched a man grope her and said nothing. And she stepped away from her father. The room went silent. The hand stayed extended. Unanswered. The King of Allarion stood before every royal family in the realm — and his daughter walked away from him.

## SAMPLE 2 — THE RECOGNITION DROP · from the CSR showroom (operator-selected anchor)
*What it teaches: the turn staged in three beats — the laughter, the world shifting, the deference — with the hero speaking last and least.*

**MB** — The laughter spread through the showroom again — smug, poisonous, loud. Lucas didn't rise to it. He stood calm and silent, eyes on the Lamborghini, and sent a single text. Then the world shifted. Engines rumbled outside, deep, synchronized, powerful. A convoy of black SUVs rolled up in perfect formation. The laughter died instantly. A man in a fitted black T-shirt walked past the manager without a glance and straight up to Lucas — and when Lucas spoke, mildly annoyed, every jaw in the room hit the floor. **"I told you to keep your distance, Jordan."** **"My apologies, sir. What happened, sir?"**

## SAMPLE 3 — THE CONCEALMENT TAX · from Draft Selection (shipped)
*What it teaches: humiliation absorbed on purpose; the secret leaking through one physical detail; the winner's own editorial beat, three words, plain.*

**MB** — Rex stepped up in front of him. Took his time. He knew Quinn couldn't move — the whole room knew. He cleared his throat, and the spit hit the side of Quinn's head. **"Thought you were quick, Level One?"** The auditorium erupted. Rex grinned and took a bow. And Quinn stood there with it running down his face, because he couldn't show three hundred trained killers what he really was. It was so unfair. His fists clenched — and the floor cracked, one hairline fracture splitting the tile under his feet. The room was too busy laughing to notice.

## SAMPLE 4 — THE IMPOSSIBLE ACCEPTANCE · from FLBM "Bond" (shipped)
*What it teaches: the device's one-word verdict; the crowd as a punchline chorus; the sacrifice in a single sentence; acceptance built from three flat lines.*

**MB** — The nurse set the scanner flat against Karl's arm. The panel lit red. Seven. Laughter broke across the rows behind him, and Kruger tilted his forearm so Karl could see the fourteen from across the room. **"Seven,"** someone repeated, like a punchline. **"Three below the minimum."** The soldier by the cart stepped forward. **"Below ten, this injection is fatal. You can go home, mine kid."** His mother had sold the last of her pension to put him in front of this needle. Karl looked at it. **"I want to try."** The room went quieter than it had been before the laughter. **"Where's the death waiver?"** The soldier pulled a single sheet from the briefcase at his feet. **"You die, your family gets nothing."** He signed it without reading the rest.

## SAMPLE 5 — THE PRICE OF BEING RIGHT · from WBT "Five Exams" (shipped)
*What it teaches: the kneel; knowledge offered free and mocked; crowd voices doing the cruelty so the villain can watch; the turn landed in one short closing line.*

**MB** — The Mantis's forelimbs rose, cornering Ren against the rock, and Kai crouched at the lip of the ravine to watch. **"Kai. The arm locks before it strikes. Hit it then and it goes down. I'm telling you exactly how."** **"That's the funny part, gray badge. You know exactly how. You just can't do it."** Kai smiled. **"Beg me to do the one thing all your knowing can't. Beg loud — so even Klein can hear it."** His posse spread along the ridge, laughing, in no danger at all. **"On your knees, mushroom. You're good at kneeling."** The scythe-arms rose to their full height. And Ren got on his knees. **"Please. Please, help me. Hit it where I told you. Please."**

## HOW W3 USES THIS FILE

1. **Loaded with the register anchors into Steps 2–3.** When a run mines a proven script, it keeps the winner's sentences nearly verbatim (tense-fold, name insertion, apparatus stripped) — the narration is already written; do not rewrite it into any house style.
2. **When a run writes FRESH narration** (source-only shows, authored beats), it imitates these five — the rhythm, the interleave, the one plain verdict at the punch — not a cap and not a quota.
3. **Lint tripwires only** (checklist-side): a narration sentence over ~35 words · a "the way you'd…" scaffold in narration · four+ consecutive sentences under 6 words · more than three narration sentences between voiced lines inside a confrontation beat. Tripping one flags the block for a re-read; nothing mechanical rewrites toward a number.
4. **The calibration exemplar** (`PE_KissTheHand_W3_ReverseEngineered`) gets recut against this file — its narration folded back toward the PE asset's own sentences — before the next run calibrates on it.
