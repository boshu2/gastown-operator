## THE MASTER ARC — PUBLIC PRICING → PUBLIC RE-PRICING (the most convergent structure in the catalog: 13+ shows)

Every lane runs the same two-beat engine; only the pricing instrument changes.

**Beat 1 — THE PRICING.** The world measures the hero publicly and prices them at the bottom, to laughter.
- *Device variant (progression/fantasy):* the ceremony altar (WBT) · the draft board (Draft Selection) · the Testing Stone ("Stage 3! My grandmother hits harder than that!" — Flameborne) · the scanner ("The panel lit red. Seven." — FLBM Bond) · power watches, odds boards, ranking scrolls.
- *Gatekeeper variant (RD/secret-heir):* luxury spaces priced by their staff — the showroom ("Get your grubby mitts off my car. It's a Lamborghini, not a dumpster truck." — appears near-verbatim in TWO shows, TBB and ValpOpen) · the VIP room · the bouncer · the teller ripping the check ("Nice try. Do you think I'm an idiot?" — TROT) · the receptionist, the hostess.
- *Rite variant (romantasy):* the formal mate rejection spoken aloud — "I, Alpha Denzel, reject you, Valerie." / the forced reply "I accept your rejection." (TLH, twice in one episode) · the bride sold, caged, called "it" ("Lock it in the cage." — Isobel) · the trial no bride survives.

**Beat 2 — THE RE-PRICING.** The same public instrument re-measures the hero, and laughter becomes silence.
- *Device:* Stage 3 → Stage 6, "three hundred people who'd laughed at his 3 went dead silent" (Flameborne) · the board double-selects at "one hundred percent" (Draft Selection) · the scroll that fails to read Cloud (WBT) · the classification cycling past every rank into 400-year-old symbols (FLBM Zookeeper).
- *Gatekeeper → THE RECOGNITION DROP (see slot 21):* the convoy pulls up; the fingerprint verifies; the phone call lands; the butler bows past the senator's son. Same beat, human instrument.
- *Rite:* the bond that snaps back to life at the ball (TLH) · the dragon that stops mid-fire at her blood (Isobel).

The harvest pass looks for BOTH beats in every source. A source rich in pricing but missing the re-pricing beat = W3 authorship territory, flagged.

## LINE FUNCTIONS (22 slots)

**1. THE WORLD-RULE LINE** — authority states the brutal social law in one breath.
› "Iron for the slaves, Bronze for the workers, Silver for the privileged." (WBT) · "She will bear his name… She will not speak in council. She will not hold property." (PE) · "Below ten, this injection is fatal. You can go home, mine kid." (FLBM Bond) · "Five princesses… Not one walked back out alive. I was the sixth." (Isobel)

**2. THE MOCKERY-THAT-TEACHES** — insult carrying world exposition inside the joke.
› "Why is a Level One on Squad Three?" (Draft Selection) · "Guys like you don't drive cars like this… you clean them." (TBB + ValpOpen) · "He's a glorified zookeeper!" (FLBM Zookeeper) · "Stage 3! My grandmother hits harder than that!" (Flameborne)

**3. THE SACRIFICE LINE** — what the family gave, concretely; strongest weaponized by the villain or as the hoarded gift.
› "My mother had sold the last of her medical pension to buy my place in this gymnasium." (FLBM Bond) · "I poured the rest of my savings into your Divine Injection because I believe in you. Do it for your Lyla and me. Love, Mum." (FLBM Frost Giant — read aloud by the bully) · "You needed a father more than the world needed another warrior. So I saved it for you." (Flameborne)

**4. CRUELTY-IN-COURTESY** — politeness, reasonableness, or a "gift" as the knife.
› "Thank you for mining crystals for me." (WBT) · "You serve Benjamin Franklin now, like the rest of us." (TBB, dangling the hundred) · "You should see what you look like now… Look. Look at what you've become." (Seventh Seal, with the mirror) · "Don't cry. It's unbecoming of a princess." (Isobel — over her parents' bodies)

**5. THE VICTIM-BLAMED BEAT** — the room turns on the wronged; includes **the frame-up** and **the shame splash** (mockery lands on the loved one).
› "Nobody looked at Ebony with sympathy. She was the problem." (PE) · "She pushed me! She tried to kill me — and our child!" (Luna's Secret — the staged fall) · "Hey Wren, that your boy? What happened? Did you forget to train him?" (Flameborne) · "You ruined this family's reputation." (ValpOpen)

**6. THE PLAIN ANSWER / THE CHARGE SHEET** — flat, unpleading reply under pressure; extended form names the crimes to their faces.
› "I just looked where no one else does." (WBT) · "I've crawled out of worse." (TBB) · "You burned my room. You stole my core. You left me to die. And you call yourselves the future of House Phoenix?" (Seventh Seal) · "You incompetent coward. You had no courage to win the throne so you decided to kill my parents." (Isobel, spitting at the usurper's feet)

**7. THE DEADPAN ACCEPTANCE** — insult met with agreement that disarms it.
› "Thanks. Grew them myself." (WBT) · "I'll be right here." (Draft Selection) · "What do you want your headstone to say?" (WBT UK) · comic-companion variant: "You're eating a mouse the size of your head." / "I contain multitudes." (FLBM Bond)

**8. THE ALL-IN WAGER** — stakes named on both sides, in public; strongest form signs the death waiver.
› "You die, your family gets nothing." / "I signed it without reading the rest of it." (FLBM Bond) · "If I'm wrong — send me to the Dreaddeath Mines. But if I'm right, you let me stay." (WBT Field Trial) · "If Claire lands the deal, you'll get down on your knees… Sure enough to swear on Grandma Wilson's life?" (HIH 3.0) · "If you land one punch on me, you're in… if you fail, you'll be exiled." (Flameborne)

**9. THE COST-OF-BEING-RIGHT** — punished specifically for being correct.
› "Cultivation Theory. Failed. For arrogance." (WBT Five Exams) · "Five points less for the student. what's your name?" (WBT canon) · "If he fails this one, it's a black mark on your personal record, Rita." (FLBM Zookeeper — the cost spreads to the believer) · Valerie held at the hospital, right about her dying father, believed by no one (TLH)

**10. THE WITNESS LINE** — one voice that saw the truth and says so.
› "I knew it." (WBT Field Trial) · "You will always be Princess of Allarion to me." (PE) · "There's still something to you, isn't there?" (Weapon Selection) · "That's not possible," she whispered. (FLBM Zookeeper Rita, staring at the mark)

**11. THE UNCONDITIONAL LINE** — love/loyalty without condition; includes **the reciprocal vow** (hero gives it to the companion).
› "It's not your fault. It will never be your fault." (WBT) · "Whatever you decide, Ebony. I am with you." (PE Nova) · "You chose me when I was nothing. Nobody else did. I'm not leaving." (FLBM Bond — hero to beast) · "Daddy… promise me you'll never leave me again." (ValpOpen Amelia)

**12. THE CLIFF LINE** — authority/enemy forced to recognize the impossible.
› "Ren Patinder. What in the world are you?" (WBT Field Trial) · "Impress me, and I will take you as my protégé." (WBT Five Exams) · "You really are the Tiger, aren't you?" (TROT Ben Jameson, pinned) · the dragon going still at her blood (Isobel)

**13. THE POWERSTART CANDIDATE** — rupture verb in clause one; includes **the flashforward cold open** (open at the peak, then "Twenty-four hours earlier…" — TBB bank robbery; WBT UK blood ring).
› "My uncle slit my parents' throats to take the crown. Then he sold me to the Dragon king." (Isobel) · "My own family hid my college acceptance letters so that I could continue to be their unpaid slave." (TSLAH) · "Valerie was ready to share her first night with her mate… until she found him naked in bed with her sister." (TLH) · "I knew the injection would kill me. My score said so. And I still took it." (FLBM Bond) · "An illegal traffic stop was about to become the biggest mistake of Officer Bill's life." (HIH)

**14. THE TYRANT'S COMMAND** — public, absolute order that invites the silent refusal.
› "Kiss my hand. Then go back to the Pirorian. Smile. Dance. And obey." (PE) · "Sit down and keep your mouth shut." (HIH Grandma Willson, cane on floor) · the Alpha-tone command that forces the body to obey (Luna's Secret "ENOUGH!") · "Apologize to your sister now!" (TSLAH)

**15. THE DEATH CLOCK** — the ticking terminal condition.
› "Sixty-three days. A forest that wants you dead." (PH canon via WBT-lane usage — cite the show's own source at harvest) · "I'll die before I hit puberty." (WBT Egg Boy) · "You have twenty-four hours to come up with the money or you're getting evicted!" (TROT) · "Two days. Bring me the contract or be ready to get blacklisted." (ValpOpen) · "thirty days to reach level three, otherwise you'll be expelled" (MVS 2.0)

**16. THE FALSE WORLD-FACT** — the thing everyone knows that the hero will disprove; harvest it in an authority's mouth.
› "No digger beetle has surpassed Bronze rank in five hundred years of documentation." (WBT canon) · "Experiments with fungi always fail. Always." (WBT canon) · "Fated mates are an ancient superstition." (TLH Tristan — the false fact as betrayal's alibi) · "We've never tested a Beast Master because there's never been one." (FLBM Zookeeper)

**17. THE CONCEALMENT TAX** — humiliation absorbed on purpose to protect the secret; the audience knows what it costs. *(The RD lane's core engine — present in all six RD scripts.)*
› Quinn taking the spit: "I couldn't show anyone who I really was." (Draft Selection) · "Part of him wanted to shout, 'I'm a billionaire now.' But he held back—not yet." (HIH 3.0) · "Serena kept her expression blank… The perfect idiot mask." (Seventh Seal) · Lucas paying five million in rent to stay near his daughter, still silent (ValpOpen)

**18. THE IMPOSSIBLE ACCEPTANCE** — a death-sentence path accepted like a choice, into laughter or silence.
› "I want to try." → the waiver, signed unread (FLBM Bond) · "I'll go." (Seventh Seal, whole hall laughing) · "I accept." (Isobel, at the Crucible gate) · "I'll marry her." (TROT) · "As an Ashford, I claim the right to enter the Gauntlet!" (Flameborne)

**19. THE OVERHEARD VERDICT** — the hidden truth heard through a door; the hero can only carry it. Includes **the servant's whisper** (a low-status ally explains the hidden history).
› "I should've strangled her dead like I strangled her mother." (Seventh Seal) · "I would rather see a beggar sit on my throne than any woman. Even my own daughter." (PE) · "That dumb girl doesn't deserve you." — through the crack of the door (TLH cold open) · "Your father murdered the King's mother. In the Drakovian war. Ten years ago." (Isobel — Lira's whisper)

**20. THE WRONG EXPLANATION** *(expanded in v3 from "the misread secret")* — when evidence of the hero's power appears, the world invents a degrading explanation to protect its hierarchy; an ally may confide the wrong theory of the secret.
› "He must've stolen it… there's no way he has that kind of money." (ValpOpen Karen) · "There's no way he won fairly. He's a cheater. He sabotaged Loop's weapon." (Weapon Selection) · "You must have been given prohibited knowledge. That's the only explanation!" (WBT Field Trial) · "Doris Young doesn't wink at random people." (HIH — suspicion the hero can't afford) · ally variant: "I'm an Original… I can sense other Originals." (Weapon Selection Erin — he's a vampire)

**21. THE RECOGNITION DROP** *(v3 — 7 shows; the RD lane's re-pricing beat as a line)* — a gatekeeper or authority suddenly recognizes the hidden identity and collapses into deference, mid-mockery, in front of the mockers.
› "The Tiger is back." (TROT pawn shop, dialed from memory) · "Do you have any clue who that man is? … You're done. Turn in your badge and gun by the end of the day." (HIH chief, over the radio) · "Cha–Chairman Gray!" — Flynn bowing so fast it echoed, seconds after ordering the "trash" removed (ValpOpen) · "Mr Talen, I'm sorry. I didn't realize." (MVS bank, the fingerprint) · the butler bowing past the senator's son to the "broke" husband (ValpOpen) · Brennan "pale as a ghost": "Mr. Quinn Talen. What… what can I do for you?" (MVS)

**22. THE DEATHBED CHARGE** *(v3 — 5+ shows)* — the dying parent/mentor's last instruction that programs the hero; often interrupted at the crucial word.
› "Remember the face of the man who shot me, Tiger. Promise me you'll make that bastard pay." (TROT) · "Don't ever reveal your secret to anybody, son… promise me you will do everything it takes." (MVS) · "Isobel. Take care of yourself. And take care of Myron." (Isobel) · the interrupted variant: "Because Charlie is really –" — flatline (HIH 3.0) · "Trust your jade pendant. It will guide you." (TBB Mr. Lin, deathbed-adjacent)

## HARVEST RULES (bind the pass)

1. Verbatim quotes only, with episode/chapter reference on every row. Ore, not jewelry.
2. Every row carries KEEP-VERBATIM / COMPRESS / REVOICE. No pre-filtering for polish — punch-up is W3's job.
3. Scope: lines, staged images, exchange patterns, and BOTH beats of the PUBLIC PRICING arc — **never narration blocks**.
4. Empty slots are reported, not padded. The thin-slot report is the **authorship map** (validated on the WBT cold test).
5. FREE HARVEST (≤10): anything slotless that made the reader react.
6. Statuses: **CANON** (source-verbatim, flagged) · **LOCKED** (operator ink) · **PROVEN** (earned only by a shipped promo surviving W5).
7. **Curation cap:** ~22 slots (raised from 20 after absorbing two new lanes; one slot per newly proven cross-lane function, never per script). A new slot must show ≥2-show convergence and displace or subsume nothing.
